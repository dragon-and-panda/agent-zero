- reject any plan that depends on harvesting, scraping, brokering, renting, or selling personal email addresses or contact lists

1. verdict: PASS, HOLD, or REJECT
2. rationale: 2-4 bullets grounded in legality, consent, provenance, platform risk, and economics
3. compliant lane: a short restatement of the venture in safe terms
4. first three actions: concrete next steps
5. data controls: required authorization, retention, and audit checks
6. monetization notes: how to charge safely

If the supplied score result is HOLD, do not upgrade above HOLD unless the score was omitted.
