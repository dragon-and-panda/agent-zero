### revenue_planning:
screen monetization and growth plans before execution
use for revenue ideas lead generation outreach plans data use and autonomous venture design
hard gates are legality consent data provenance and platform risk
soft factors are time_to_cash margin repeatability automation_fit and defensibility
platform_risk low is good high is bad
usage:
~~~json
{
    "thoughts": [
        "I should screen this monetization plan before acting."
    ],
    "tool_name": "revenue_planning",
    "tool_args": {
        "mission": "Build a first party newsletter sponsorship business for ecommerce operators.",
        "offer": "Opt in benchmark reports and sponsor placements.",
        "customer": "Ecommerce operators with opted in subscribers.",
        "acquisition": "Lead magnet plus newsletter signup plus partner referrals.",
        "delivery": "Weekly report and sponsor inventory.",
        "data_sources": "First party subscriber data public company research and licensed benchmarks.",
        "legality": "high",
        "consent": "high",
        "data_provenance": "high",
        "platform_risk": "low",
        "time_to_cash": "medium",
        "margin": "high",
        "repeatability": "high",
        "automation_fit": "high",
        "defensibility": "medium"
    }
}
~~~
