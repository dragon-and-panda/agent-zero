### revenue_planning:
evaluate a venture or growth idea before execution
use for revenue planning monetization design or autonomous venture scoping
this tool applies legality consent provenance and platform-risk gates
never use it to justify selling personal data scraped contacts email lists or non-consensual outreach
usage:
~~~json
{
    "thoughts": [
        "..."
    ],
    "tool_name": "revenue_planning",
    "tool_args": {
        "mission": "short description of the business goal",
        "offer": "product or service being sold",
        "acquisition": "how customers are found or reached",
        "data_handling": "what data is collected or processed",
        "monetization": "how revenue is earned",
        "notes": "constraints risks or open questions"
    }
}
~~~
