### revenue_planning:
screen revenue or monetization ideas before acting
use for lead generation, data extraction, growth, outreach, and monetization tasks
reject personal-data resale, inbox scraping, and non-consensual contact harvesting
prefer first-party, opt-in, client-authorized, and auditable business models
args:
- mission
- assets
- constraints
usage:
~~~json
{
    "thoughts": [
        "...",
    ],
    "tool_name": "revenue_planning",
    "tool_args": {
        "mission": "Evaluate a proposed revenue lane",
        "assets": "Current tools, datasets, audience, workflows",
        "constraints": "Legal, consent, platform, or operational limits"
    }
}
~~~
