### revenue_planning:
screen monetization ideas before execution
use when the task involves revenue generation business models lead generation outreach data monetization or autonomous income ideas
the tool rejects personal-data resale scraped leads non-consensual inbox mining and similar privacy-invasive tactics
it reframes risky ideas into compliant equivalents such as opt-in lead generation first-party crm automation public-data research products or listing services

recommended args:
- idea: the venture or tactic to assess
- data_sources: where data would come from
- constraints: legal ethical or operating limits
- goal: desired business outcome

usage:
~~~json
{
    "thoughts": [
        "This request involves monetization and personal data handling.",
        "I should screen it with revenue_planning before suggesting execution steps."
    ],
    "tool_name": "revenue_planning",
    "tool_args": {
        "idea": "Build a service that uses an authorized Gmail inbox to surface warm leads and draft follow-ups.",
        "data_sources": "Owner-authorized Gmail, internal CRM, public company websites",
        "constraints": "No data resale, no spam, keep within consent and platform terms",
        "goal": "Generate revenue by selling workflow automation, not by selling contact data"
    }
}
~~~
