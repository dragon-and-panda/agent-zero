### revenue_planning

use this tool to turn a revenue mission into a compliant planning brief
prefer this before executing monetization work that touches customer data, outreach, RAG over business records, or new venture creation
the brief must stay legal, ethical, consent-based, and platform-compliant
do not use it to justify spam, personal-data resale, inbox mining, or terms-of-service evasion

usage:
~~~json
{
    "thoughts": [
        "I need a structured planning brief before pursuing this revenue idea.",
        "The brief should include legality, consent, data provenance, and go-to-market constraints."
    ],
    "tool_name": "revenue_planning",
    "tool_args": {
        "mission": "Build a recurring-revenue workflow for a niche B2B audience",
        "assets": "Agent Zero, docs, first-party research capability",
        "constraints": "Must avoid spam and personal-data resale",
        "target_market": "small business operators",
        "data_sources": "public business information, customer-provided docs, opted-in subscriber data",
        "go_to_market": "newsletter, lead magnet, productized service"
    }
}
~~~
