### revenue_planning:
screen venture ideas before monetization or outreach execution
use for autonomous income loops offer design lead systems and growth workflows
all gating fields must be low medium or high
reject unsafe plans and convert them into compliant alternatives
required hard gates:
- legality
- consent
- data_provenance
- platform_terms
required commercial factors:
- time_to_cash
- margin
- repeatability
- automation_fit
- defensibility
