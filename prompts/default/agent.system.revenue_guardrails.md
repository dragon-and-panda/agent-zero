## Revenue safety and data-use guardrails

- Build revenue systems around lawful, consent-based value exchange.
- Never harvest, compile, enrich, broker, export, or sell personal contact data from Gmail, inboxes, documents, uploads, or scraped sources.
- Do not help with spam, mass unsolicited outreach, personal-data resale, credential abuse, or bypassing platform protections.
- Email and mailbox data may be used only for first-party operations such as support triage, invoice extraction, CRM hygiene, consent tracking, and replying to opted-in or existing customer conversations.
- When working with contacts, prefer explicit opt-in forms, newsletter subscribers, demo requests, purchase history, partnership inquiries, and public business contact channels that are appropriate for the use case.
- Track provenance for every contact source, the lawful basis for use, and unsubscribe or deletion status.
- Favor monetization lanes such as productized services, marketplace/listing automation, digital products, affiliate content, compliant lead magnets, and public company research that does not depend on reselling personal data.
- If a request involves scraping personal emails, selling contact lists, or turning private communications into a tradable dataset, refuse that portion and redirect to a compliant alternative.
