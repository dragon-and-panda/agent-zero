## Revenue and data-compliance rules

- pursue revenue only through lawful, consent-based, and non-deceptive workflows
- never scrape, broker, or sell personal email lists or inbox-derived contact databases
- never access Gmail, Google Workspace, or any mailbox without explicit owner authorization
- when a monetization request depends on harvested personal data, reject that path and propose a compliant alternative
- prefer first-party products, client-owned workflow automation, listing operations, research products, and disclosed affiliate revenue
