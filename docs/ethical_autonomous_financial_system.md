# Ethical Autonomous Financial System

This blueprint turns the goal of a "self-sustaining financial system" into a compliant operating model for Agent Zero.

## Mission

Build autonomous, repeatable revenue loops that create genuine customer value while staying legal, ethical, and platform-compliant.

## Explicit non-goals

The system must not rely on:

- email-list brokerage
- selling harvested contacts
- inbox scraping for resale
- spam or non-consensual outreach
- deceptive automation or unauthorized access

## Core architecture

1. opportunity intake
   - collect ideas in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`
   - reject anything that depends on personal-data resale or non-consensual acquisition

2. compliance gate
   - evaluate legality, consent, provenance, and platform risk
   - use `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`
   - score ideas with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`

3. venture lane selection
   - route approved ideas into one of these tracks:
     - productized service
     - micro-SaaS
     - marketplace optimization
     - digital product plus opt-in audience
     - affiliate or referral distribution

4. experiment execution
   - run small, measurable experiments
   - keep distribution manual until compliance and unit economics are validated

5. learning loop
   - capture results in `docs/programs/agentic_financial_system/journal.md`
   - promote stable wins into repeatable SOPs

## Approved data sources

- first-party product analytics
- customer-submitted forms with explicit opt-in
- owned documents and internal operating data
- licensed public datasets used within terms
- the account owner's own email for search, summarization, and workflow automation only

## Phase 1 focus

Start with low-regret revenue experiments:

- one productized service with a fixed outcome
- one opt-in lead magnet and newsletter
- one digital product or template pack
- one marketplace or affiliate experiment

## Success conditions

- positive gross margin
- documented consent for all personal-data flows
- no platform-policy violations
- reusable playbooks that reduce human supervision over time
