## 2026-06-16 - Program Seed

### What changed
- Created the initial program blueprint at `docs/programs/ethical_financial_system/blueprint.md`.
- Added a compliance pack at `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`.
- Seeded strategy intake at `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
- Added an opportunity-scoring instrument for compliant venture selection.

### Key decisions
- Rejected email-list brokerage and any workflow that resells personal email data.
- Approved inbox RAG only for owned or explicitly authorized mailboxes.
- Positioned Orange DataScaping as a compliant analysis layer for consented data.
- Chose consent-based offers, productized services, and repeatable automation as the Phase 1 monetization path.

### Why
- The requested resale-list model creates legal, spam, privacy, and platform-enforcement risk.
- The repo already has stronger leverage in knowledge ingestion, memory, and agentic execution than in list arbitrage.
- Durable businesses compound better from owned workflows and customer trust than from one-off data extraction.

### Next hypotheses
- A narrowly scoped inbox-to-offer service can reach first revenue faster than a broader "agency OS" pitch.
- Marketplace/listing automation may monetize faster for sellers with clear inventory and visible workflow pain.
- A scoring rubric will keep the system focused on high-consent, high-automation opportunities.

### Evidence to collect next
- which owned inbox categories produce the clearest monetizable insights;
- how much manual cleanup is needed before email exports become useful RAG corpora;
- which offer track closes fastest in a pilot.
