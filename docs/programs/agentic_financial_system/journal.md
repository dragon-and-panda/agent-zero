# Agentic Financial System Journal

## Baseline Entry

### Context

The incoming mission targeted autonomous revenue generation and explicitly mentioned extracting and selling email lists. That monetization path is not being implemented.

### Decision

The program is being reset around compliant first-party revenue lanes:

- consent-based Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene
- Autonomous Listing Service
- research and intelligence products
- client-owned CRM enrichment

### Why

- email-list resale creates privacy, consent, and platform-risk problems
- first-party service lanes still fit the autonomy and profitability goals
- the repo already contains useful listing and agent-orchestration building blocks
