# Agentic Financial System Journal

## Baseline entry

### Context

The mission is to establish a durable autonomous financial system. An unsafe proposal included compiling email address lists from email data and selling them to third parties.

### Decision

Rejected the contact-list resale path.

### Why it was rejected

- personal contact resale creates privacy and consent failures
- inbox-derived list brokerage is incompatible with compliant data-use principles
- the monetization path carries platform, legal, and reputation risk

### Replacement direction

Activated a compliance-first plan centered on:

1. authorized inbox-to-CRM extraction for first-party business operations
2. autonomous listing and resale services
3. productized research and operational intelligence
4. opt-in audience assets and subscription workflows

### Immediate next steps

- score candidate lanes with the strategy instrument
- build the `revenue_planning` tool into the agent workflow
- encode prompt-level prohibitions against inbox scraping and contact-data resale
- keep mission updates in this journal as the system evolves
