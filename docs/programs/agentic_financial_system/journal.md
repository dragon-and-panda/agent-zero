# Agentic Financial System Mission Diary

## Baseline Entry
- Mission: build a self-sustaining financial system using ethical, legal, automation-friendly online ventures.
- Guardrails:
  - no scraping or resale of personal email lists
  - no inbox access without the owner's explicit consent
  - no spam, deceptive outreach, or platform circumvention
  - no high-risk trading automation without a separate risk program and staged simulation gates
- Initial lane priorities:
  1. opt-in lead generation and qualification for client services
  2. productized research and data-backed market intelligence
  3. adjacent autonomous services such as listing optimization and managed outreach inputs
- Initial hedge lanes:
  - templates, audits, and playbooks sold as digital products
  - consent-based CRM enrichment for client-owned records
  - managed B2B appointment systems that rely on explicit compliance checks
