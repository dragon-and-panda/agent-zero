# Agentic Financial System Journal

## 2026-05-23 Baseline

### Mission intake

Initial mission: create a self-sustaining financial system with minimal human involvement.

### Rejected component

Rejected the idea of compiling and selling email lists from Gmail or other files.

Reason:

- weak consent
- unacceptable privacy risk
- likely platform and legal violations
- poor durability as a business foundation

### Approved pivot

Focus Phase 1 on compliant revenue lanes:

1. authorized inbox-to-CRM intelligence for owner-controlled mailboxes
2. autonomous listing services for seller-authorized inventory
3. research and intelligence products built from public or licensed sources
4. opt-in audience systems instead of harvested-contact brokerage

### First execution priorities

- encode compliance rules in docs and prompts
- add a revenue-planning gate before monetization work
- add a simple scoring instrument for PASS, HOLD, and REJECT decisions
- keep the intake queue explicit about rejected and approved opportunities
