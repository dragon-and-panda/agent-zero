# Agentic Financial System Journal

## 2026-05-13

### Intake Summary

The mission requested an autonomous financial system with data extraction and monetization components. One requested monetization path involved compiling and selling email lists sourced from Gmail and related files.

### Decision

Rejected the contact-list brokerage path.

### Why It Was Rejected

- personal email list resale is a privacy and consent failure
- inbox extraction for resale creates legal and platform risk
- the strategy pushes the system toward spam and abuse patterns
- it is incompatible with the program's compliance baseline

### Approved Reframe

The program now focuses on:

- authorized email RAG for internal insight extraction only
- opt-in audience building
- productized services
- micro-SaaS opportunities
- public or licensed research products

### Next Actions

1. maintain a scored opportunity queue
2. document compliant experiments
3. route all future monetization ideas through the scoring instrument before execution
