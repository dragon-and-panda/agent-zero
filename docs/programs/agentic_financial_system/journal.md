# Agentic Financial System Journal

## Baseline entry

- Mission mode: compliant autonomous monetization only.
- Rejected lane: scraping, brokering, or selling personal email lists from inboxes,
  files, or third-party sources.
- Active lanes:
  1. Inbox-to-CRM assistant for the operator's own business workflows.
  2. Productized research briefs and lead magnets.
  3. Listing, brokerage, or operational services using first-party and client-owned
     data with consent.
  4. Software or automation assets sold as subscriptions, retainers, or setup fees.

## Operating notes

- Every new revenue lane must receive a documented score using
  `/workspace/instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
- Lanes that fail legality, consent, provenance, or platform-rule checks are
  rejected immediately rather than "improved" through workaround tactics.
- The agent should favor repeatable, auditable revenue systems over one-off
  questionable wins.
