# Agentic Financial System Journal

This journal captures decisions, experiments, blockers, and outcomes for the compliant financial-system program.

## Baseline

- Mission: create a self-sustaining financial system through legal, ethical, consent-based online ventures.
- Rejected lane: personal email list extraction, brokerage, or resale from Gmail or other files.
- Active principle: prefer compounding assets, first-party data, and repeatable service systems over privacy-invasive arbitrage.

## Current active lanes

1. Inbox-to-CRM operations for the data owner
   - Scope: classify user-owned messages, extract consented leads, invoices, and follow-up tasks.
   - Boundary: no third-party resale, no harvesting unrelated contacts, no unauthorized outreach.

2. Autonomous listing and resale operations
   - Scope: build seller-owned listing, pricing, publishing, and negotiation workflows.
   - Boundary: respect marketplace policies, no evasion, no spam tactics.

3. Research and intelligence products
   - Scope: benchmark reports, curated public-data maps, and proprietary internal analysis.
   - Boundary: only lawful sources with clear provenance and redistribution rights.
