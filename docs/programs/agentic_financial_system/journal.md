# Agentic Financial System Journal

This journal tracks what the autonomous system is learning while it searches
for compliant, repeatable revenue.

---

## Baseline Entry

### Mission framing
- Build a self-sustaining financial system through lawful, ethical online
  ventures with minimal supervision.
- Reject any plan that depends on scraping inboxes without permission, selling
  personal data, or violating platform terms.

### Approved near-term lanes
1. Client-owned inbox-to-CRM automation for users who explicitly connect their
   own accounts and approve each workflow.
2. Autonomous listing and resale operations based on owned inventory or
   client-authorized inventory.
3. Research products, prompt packs, workflow templates, and lightweight
   services that improve small-business operations.

### Deferred lanes
- Any paid media or cold outreach lane remains deferred until a consented lead
  source, sender reputation policy, and platform-specific messaging rules are
  documented.
- Any finance or trading workflow remains deferred until it has a simulation
  harness, risk budget, and drawdown guardrails.

### Rejected lane example
- Harvesting or selling email lists from Gmail, files, or any dataset without
  explicit participant consent is rejected.

### Next execution priorities
1. Score candidate ventures using `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
2. Stand up the compliance-first planning workflow in prompts and tools.
3. Produce one revenue lane with clear pricing, delivery workflow, and lawful
   data provenance.
