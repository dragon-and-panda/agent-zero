# Agentic Financial System Journal

## Baseline entry

### Decision
- Rejected the requested email-list extraction and resale workflow.
- Approved a compliance-first program centered on client-owned automation, marketplace services, and public-data research products.

### Why
- Selling or brokering personal email lists is privacy-invasive, likely unlawful in many contexts, and incompatible with platform anti-abuse policies.
- The repo already supports a governed autonomy model, so the correct implementation is to encode durable guardrails and safer revenue lanes in-repo.

### Initial active lanes
1. Inbox-to-CRM workflow for consenting client businesses.
2. Autonomous listing concierge based on `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md`.
3. Public or licensed market-research subscriptions.

### Metrics to track
- Qualified discovery calls booked.
- Pilot customers activated.
- Gross margin by lane.
- Automation coverage percent.
- Compliance incidents.

### Next hypothesis
- The fastest compliant path to revenue is a client-owned service workflow where the customer grants authorization, the data stays in their business context, and the agent drafts but does not autonomously send risky outbound communications.
