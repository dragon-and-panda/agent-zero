# Agentic Financial System Journal

## Baseline Entry
- Mission mode: compliance-first autonomous revenue research and execution.
- Hard exclusions: personal email harvesting, non-consensual inbox access, spam, list brokerage, and resale of personal contact data.
- Active lanes:
  1. Inbox-to-CRM for user-owned or opt-in contacts.
  2. Autonomous Listing Service commercialization.
  3. Research products and operating playbooks.
  4. Service automation with explicit customer authorization.
- Next checkpoint: score each lane with the strategy instrument before promoting any lane to active execution.

## Operating Notes
- Any lane that fails legality, consent, provenance, or platform-rules checks must be rejected, not patched around.
- HOLD outcomes mean "compliant but commercially weak" and should trigger redesign rather than immediate launch.
