### Summary

Created the initial mission record for an ethical and legal autonomous revenue
program.

### Decision Log

- Rejected the idea of compiling and selling email lists.
- Declared owner-authorized inbox operations as acceptable only when used for
  CRM, support, fulfillment, or first-party follow-up.
- Kept the Autonomous Listing Service active as a compliant monetization lane.
- Added a requirement that all future lanes pass a scored legality/privacy gate
  before activation.

### Current Active Lanes

1. Inbox-to-CRM operations assistant
2. Autonomous listing service
3. Productized research and intelligence

### Risks

- Broad strategy can drift into non-compliant data use if data provenance is not
  documented early.
- Revenue attractiveness can bias scoring unless legality and consent are hard
  gates rather than soft factors.
- Marketplace and email automations both carry platform-policy risk and need
  explicit reviews before launch.

### Next Actions

1. Use the scoring instrument on the three active lanes.
2. Write the first Inbox-to-CRM service specification.
3. Convert one lane into a sandbox pilot with measurable outputs.

### Notes for Future Runs
