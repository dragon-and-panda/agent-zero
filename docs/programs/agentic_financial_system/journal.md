# Agentic Financial System Journal

## 2026-05-13 - Baseline program setup

### Decision
- Rejected the proposed personal email-list resale path.
- Reframed the mission into a compliant portfolio centered on first-party automation, listings, research products, and owned-audience monetization.

### Why
- Selling compiled email lists creates major privacy, consent, spam, and platform-risk failures.
- The repo is better suited to durable, tool-driven services than to risky brokerage schemes.

### Activated work
- Added a compliance pack with explicit prohibitions and safe substitutes.
- Added a strategy intake queue and scoring instrument.
- Added a first-class revenue planning tool so agents can screen monetization ideas before execution.

### Next focus
- Prioritize the first-party inbox-to-CRM lane because it uses authorized data and can become a repeatable service.
- Keep the autonomous listing service as the second active hedge.
- Package at least one research or template product that can be sold without personal-data handling.
