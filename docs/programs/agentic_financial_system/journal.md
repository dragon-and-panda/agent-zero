# Agentic Financial System Journal

## Active thesis
- Build a portfolio of legal, consent-based online revenue workflows that can be operated and improved by Agent Zero with minimal human supervision.

## Guardrails in force
- No scraping or mining private inboxes without explicit user-scoped authorization and a legitimate operational purpose.
- No selling, brokering, or sharing personal email lists or other contact datasets.
- No spam, bulk cold outreach, deceptive lead generation, or platform-TOS evasion.

## Current workstreams
1. Revenue opportunity intake and scoring.
2. Policy-aware planning tool for compliant ventures.
3. Prompt hardening so autonomous agents reject unsafe monetization tactics.

## Validation notes
- Any proposed monetization flow must pass the strategy scorecard before further implementation.
- Plans that depend on personal data resale, non-consensual inbox use, or unclear data provenance are rejected rather than refined.
