# Agentic Financial System Journal

## Baseline

### Decision

Adopt a compliance-first approach to autonomous monetization. Reject any lane built on scraping inboxes for third-party contact extraction or selling compiled email lists.

### Rejected lane

- Idea: use Google email data and other files to build email lists for sale.
- Status: rejected.
- Why: fails privacy, consent, provenance, and anti-spam rules.

### Active focus

- Primary lane: first-party inbox-to-CRM operations for the account owner or authorized clients.
- Hedge lane: autonomous listing workflows from `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md`.
- Hedge lane: research products built from public or licensed inputs.

### Next actions

1. Screen new revenue ideas with `revenue_planning`.
2. Score promising ideas with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
3. Pilot only lanes that stay within the compliance pack.
