# Agentic Financial System Journal

## Baseline Entry

### Decision

Rejected the request pattern of harvesting Google email data, compiling email address lists, and selling those lists. That model depends on privacy invasion, poor consent, weak data provenance, and likely platform abuse.

### Replacement Direction

Pivot to compliant lanes that still support the core goal of autonomous revenue:

- inbox-to-CRM processing for the consenting account owner;
- autonomous listing services for sellers;
- research products built from lawful sources;
- opt-in audience products and micro-SaaS workflows.

### Immediate Next Steps

1. keep the compliance pack discoverable in docs;
2. screen future revenue ideas with the scoring instrument;
3. build a reusable revenue-planning tool into the prompt/tool stack;
4. prioritize first-party and opt-in data flows over scraped or purchased data.
