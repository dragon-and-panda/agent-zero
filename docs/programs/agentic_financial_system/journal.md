# Agentic Financial System Journal

## 2026-05-23 baseline

- Original mission included compiling and selling email address lists.
- That lane is rejected under `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`.
- The program is reframed around compliant revenue lanes:
  - inbox-to-CRM workflows for first-party or client-authorized accounts
  - listing and sourcing assistance
  - research and intelligence products
  - internal automation services
- First active operating rule: score every new lane before activation and preserve a written record of why it passed, held, or failed.
- Current priority: develop the compliant inbox-to-CRM lane first because it can use authorized data, demonstrates real utility, and fits the existing RAG-oriented architecture.
