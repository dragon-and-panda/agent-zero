# Agentic Financial System Journal

## Baseline
- Mission opened as a compliant rewrite of an unsafe request involving personal email harvesting and list resale.
- Prohibited path rejected: extracting or brokering personal contact data from Gmail or other sources for sale.
- Approved operating lanes:
  1. Inbox-to-CRM automation for a consenting business owner using first-party data.
  2. Productized services such as listing optimization, research briefs, workflow automation, and analytics.
  3. Opt-in lead generation assets, landing pages, newsletters, and partner programs.
  4. Ethical market intelligence products built from lawful public or licensed sources.

## Initial priorities
1. Stand up compliance and scoring artifacts so future autonomous runs screen ideas before execution.
2. Prefer short time-to-value offers with clear delivery and repeatability:
   - seller listing optimization,
   - outreach copy and CRM hygiene for client-owned contacts,
   - inbox triage and lead qualification,
   - niche research briefs and monitoring.
3. Keep all customer acquisition opt-in, documented, and reversible.

## Current blockers
- No live customer pipeline or first-party inbox integration has been configured in this branch.
- No lawful data source inventory or consent records exist yet.
- No billing, offer packaging, or KPI dashboard has been implemented yet.
