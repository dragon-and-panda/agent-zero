# Agentic Financial System Improvement Backlog

## Priority order
1. Inbox-to-CRM assistant for user-owned, consented inboxes.
2. Compliance-aware offer and pricing research for first-party products.
3. Proposal generation and follow-up tooling for legitimate service sales.
4. Lightweight experiment tracking for acquisition and conversion funnels.

## Candidate improvements

### 1. Consent-aware Gmail triage
- Build a workflow that only operates on inbox data when the account owner has explicitly connected and approved access.
- Extract only business-relevant metadata needed for classification, routing, and CRM updates.
- Store provenance, retention, and permission status with every record.

### 2. Revenue idea scoring in-band
- Use a dedicated planning tool to evaluate legality, consent, provenance, platform risk, repeatability, margin, and automation leverage.
- Reject ideas involving contact harvesting, credential abuse, data brokerage, deception, or terms-of-service evasion.

### 3. First-party lead magnet pipeline
- Produce opt-in assets such as calculators, audits, reports, or templates.
- Capture leads through explicit forms and route them into a CRM with source attribution.

### 4. Client-owned service lane
- Package the framework as a lawful service:
  - inbox organization
  - sales operations support
  - listing optimization
  - research and proposal drafting
- Require client authorization before touching private systems or data.

### 5. Feedback loop
- Track each experiment with:
  - target customer
  - acquisition source
  - consent basis
  - CAC proxy
  - conversion signal
  - next action

## Explicit rejects
- scraping personal contact details from third-party inboxes or files
- compiling bulk email lists for resale
- selling or renting personal data
- sending spam or bypassing platform protections
