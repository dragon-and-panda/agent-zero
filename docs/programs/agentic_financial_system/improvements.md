# Agentic Financial System Improvement Backlog

## Highest Priority

### 1. Launch Candidate Scoring Cadence
- Add every new idea to `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
- Require a recorded score before work begins.
- Reject ideas that fail legality, consent, provenance, or terms checks.

### 2. Build the Inbox-to-CRM Safe Lane
- Support customer-requested inbox triage and CRM enrichment only for the inbox owner.
- Convert useful signals into tagged CRM fields, not resale datasets.
- Add clear source provenance and opt-out handling.

### 3. Productized Service Offer Pack
- Create a narrow offer menu with defined outcomes, pricing bands, intake questions, and delivery SOPs.
- Favor offers that can be fulfilled largely from existing repo capabilities and adjacent service blueprints.

### 4. First-Party Audience Funnel
- Define a lead magnet, landing page concept, newsletter cadence, and follow-up sequence for opted-in subscribers.
- Measure signup rate, activation, reply rate, and conversion to paid work.

## Medium Priority

### 5. Digital Product Pipeline
- Convert successful internal SOPs into sellable templates, playbooks, or packaged research.
- Track build cost, margin, update burden, and refund risk.

### 6. Marketplace and Listing Expansion
- Reuse the Autonomous Listing Service as an operational hedge.
- Score adjacent marketplace opportunities with the same compliance and repeatability gates.

### 7. Revenue Telemetry
- Add a weekly report covering pipeline count, gross margin, automation coverage, and trust/compliance exceptions.
- Surface outputs in `logs/reports/` once the instrument layer expands.

## Always On

### 8. Compliance Drift Checks
- Review active lanes against `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`.
- Flag any request that trends toward scraped contacts, list resale, or unclear data provenance.

### 9. Knowledge Capture
- When a lane succeeds, promote the playbook into durable docs and instruments.
- When a lane fails, record why so the system does not retry the same bad pattern.
