# Agentic Financial System Improvements

## Priority backlog

### P0
- stand up a compliant opportunity intake queue
- require `revenue_planning` before any contact, inbox, outreach, or monetization workflow
- score every candidate venture for legality, consent, provenance, durability, and platform risk

### P1
- build reusable operating procedures for first-party CRM hygiene
- add client-ready deliverables for support inbox triage and listing optimization
- create an opt-in audience funnel with a lead magnet and privacy notice

### P2
- add telemetry for revenue, conversion, retention, and unit economics
- add automated review of data provenance artifacts before campaign launch
- package best-performing workflows into productized services or software modules

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- chasing fast revenue through personal-data resale
- building growth systems that depend on spam or unclear consent
- mixing internal operational contacts with marketing contacts without governance
- using scraped or purchased lead files because they appear cheaper in the short term
