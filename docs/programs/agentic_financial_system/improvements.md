# Agentic Financial System Improvement Backlog

Prioritize revenue lanes that are legal, consent-based, cash-efficient, and realistic for autonomous execution.

## Active backlog

| Priority | Item | Why it matters | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Ship the revenue scoring instrument | Gives the agent a hard gate before it builds risky lanes | PASS, HOLD, and REJECT examples work locally |
| P0 | Add a first-class revenue planning tool | Converts vague monetization asks into structured, compliant plans | Tool returns legal guardrails, lane options, and next steps |
| P1 | Stand up first-party inbox-to-CRM workflow | Useful, sellable automation without third-party list resale | Works on owner-authorized mailbox data only |
| P1 | Package autonomous listing service offer | The repo already contains a strong adjacent monetization blueprint | Clear service scope, intake, and pricing model exist |
| P1 | Build opt-in lead magnet funnel templates | Creates lawful acquisition channels instead of purchased lists | Landing page plus CRM handoff pattern documented |
| P2 | Add lane-level metrics and reserve policy | Prevents unbounded risk and false-positive "wins" | Every lane has KPI and downside thresholds |
| P2 | Curate compliant public-data research products | Generates subscription or report revenue from non-personal data | Provenance and licensing are documented |

## Rejected backlog themes

These are intentionally excluded even if they look profitable on paper:

- selling scraped or compiled personal email lists
- scraping private inboxes for third-party data extraction
- bulk unsolicited outreach to non-opted-in contacts
- bypassing anti-bot or anti-abuse controls on marketplaces or email platforms
