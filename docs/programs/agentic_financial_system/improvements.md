# Agentic Financial System Improvement Backlog

## Prioritization rubric
- Impact: revenue, learning, or durable capability creation
- Risk: legality, privacy, platform policy, operational complexity
- Leverage: repeatability, automation potential, compounding effects

## Active backlog
1. Build a lightweight opportunity scorer into the agent toolchain.
2. Create compliant inbox-to-CRM workflows for founder-owned mailboxes.
3. Add program-specific prompts for legal, ethical, opt-in customer acquisition.
4. Create a mission intake queue that automatically rejects abusive data plans.
5. Add observability around spend, channel mix, and experiment outcomes.

## Candidate revenue lanes
- Productized services:
  - listing optimization
  - outbound copywriting for opted-in leads
  - inbox triage and qualification
  - market research briefs
- Digital products:
  - playbooks
  - templates
  - niche datasets built from public or licensed sources
- Software:
  - internal tools later packaged into paid SaaS
  - vertical workflow automations for small businesses

## Explicitly rejected lanes
- Personal email list brokerage
- Scraping private inboxes without consent
- Sending spam or bulk unsolicited outreach
- Selling personal data gathered from third parties
- Violating marketplace or platform terms of service
