# Agentic Financial System Improvement Backlog

## Active priorities

1. Add a system-prompt extension that injects the compliance pack into missions involving monetization, outreach, or regulated domains.
2. Expand `revenue_planning` to pull live policy references and market evidence before greenlighting a lane.
3. Create reusable launch kits for compliant lanes:
   - seller-owned listing concierge
   - opt-in lead magnet plus CRM follow-up
   - research/report productization
   - agency services with documented client consent
4. Add telemetry on lane economics:
   - gross revenue
   - direct cost
   - contribution margin
   - repeatability
   - automation rate
5. Add a sandboxed outreach simulator so copy and workflows can be tested without contacting real people.

## Rejected ideas to keep rejected

- Inbox scraping without account-owner consent
- sale or brokerage of personal email lists
- spam automation or bulk unsolicited outreach
- credential harvesting or account takeover workflows
- bypassing platform anti-abuse controls
