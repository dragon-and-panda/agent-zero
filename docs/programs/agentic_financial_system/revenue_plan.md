# Revenue Plan

## Objective

Generate lawful revenue quickly while building reusable assets that can compound into recurring income.

## Phase 1: service revenue

### Lane A: Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene Service

Offer a client-authorized workflow that:

- connects to a client-owned mailbox,
- retrieves only messages relevant to the stated business purpose,
- extracts structured customer and company data,
- scores relationship quality and consent status,
- outputs a reviewed CRM import plus action summary.

Primary pricing models:

- setup fee for ingestion and schema mapping
- monthly retainer for ongoing hygiene
- premium tier for analytics, segmentation, and playbooks

### Lane B: Autonomous Listing Service

Use the existing blueprint to help sellers create better listings, manage inquiries, and close items faster.

Primary pricing models:

- per listing fee
- monthly seller plan
- premium negotiation or analytics add-on

### Lane C: Research and insight packs

Sell focused reports or dashboards built from public, licensed, or customer-approved data.

Primary pricing models:

- one-off report
- recurring benchmark subscription
- custom research retainer

## Phase 2: productize the best service lane

Convert the highest-signal workflow into a repeatable product:

- templates,
- CRM connectors,
- compliance checklists,
- dashboards,
- self-serve onboarding,
- subscription packaging.

## Phase 3: compounding distribution

Replace illegal list-selling ideas with compliant audience and distribution systems:

- opt-in newsletters
- webinars and lead magnets
- case studies
- referral loops
- partnerships
- client-approved outbound based on consented or first-party relationships

## What not to do

Do not monetize by selling, brokering, or swapping raw email lists. That creates legal, privacy, and deliverability risk while weakening the long-term brand and platform position.

## Activation checklist

Before starting a lane:

1. run `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
2. confirm the lane passes legality, consent, and data-rights gates
3. define pricing and a smallest sellable offer
4. log the lane in the mission journal
