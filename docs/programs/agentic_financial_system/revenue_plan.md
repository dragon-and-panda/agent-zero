# Revenue Plan

## Portfolio Goal

Create a mix of near-term service revenue and longer-term subscription revenue, while keeping every lane lawful, consent-based, and automatable.

## Lane 1: Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene Service

### Why this lane

- close to the user's stated interest in email data, but compliant;
- fast to deliver with existing AI summarization and extraction capabilities;
- naturally recurring for sales teams, founder inboxes, and shared support inboxes.

### Offer

- classify inbound threads;
- extract company/contact metadata from authorized messages;
- draft CRM updates and follow-up queues;
- summarize unanswered or high-intent conversations;
- generate weekly pipeline hygiene reports.

### Inputs

- customer-owned Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox;
- customer-approved API or export access;
- optional CRM export for reconciliation.

### Deliverable

- cleaned CRM import;
- follow-up task list;
- lead-status summary;
- missed-opportunity report.

### Pricing model

- setup fee + monthly retainer;
- or usage-based pricing per mailbox / per thread volume.

## Lane 2: Autonomous Listing Operations

### Why this lane

- aligns with existing repo direction;
- easier compliance boundary because the customer owns the listings and assets;
- can expand into cross-posting, message triage, and pricing insights.

### Offer

- listing draft generation;
- image/title optimization;
- multi-platform posting support;
- inquiry prioritization and response drafting;
- performance reporting.

## Lane 3: Public-Data Research Products

### Why this lane

- avoids personal-data dependence;
- can become a subscription asset;
- suitable for RAG, scraping of allowed public sources, and structured monitoring.

### Offer examples

- competitor pricing watch;
- supplier directory with public contact channels;
- vertical market briefs;
- niche demand trend monitors.

## Lane 4: Affiliate or Partner Content

### Why this lane

- low operational cost;
- pairs well with research/reporting workflows;
- can monetize authority built in lanes 2 and 3.

### Guardrails

- disclose affiliate relationships;
- avoid misleading claims;
- do not buy or target scraped contact lists.

## Lane Selection Criteria

Use the strategy score instrument and prioritize lanes that:

- pass legality and consent gates;
- have repeatable delivery;
- can be fulfilled with current repo capabilities;
- produce retained revenue rather than one-off arbitrage.

## Deferred / Rejected Lanes

### Rejected

- selling harvested or compiled personal email lists;
- inbox mining for third-party resale;
- bulk cold outreach fueled by non-consensual data collection.

### Deferred

- higher-risk regulated strategies that require deeper legal review, licensing, or capital controls.
