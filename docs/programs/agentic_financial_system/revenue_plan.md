# Revenue Plan

## Lane Summary

| Lane | Offer | Who Pays | Why It Fits Autonomy | Compliance Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene | Turn a client's own inbox into structured CRM updates, summaries, and follow-up tasks | SMBs, agencies, founders | Repeatable parsing, tagging, and workflow automation | Requires clear client authorization and in-scope data handling |
| Autonomous Listing Ops | Create, enhance, publish, and refresh marketplace listings | Individuals, resellers, small stores | Existing repo direction, measurable output, SOP-friendly | Avoid counterfeit goods, platform abuse, and deceptive claims |
| Research Briefs | Sell niche opportunity maps or competitor reports | Operators, investors, agencies | High leverage from agent research + templating | Use lawful/public/company data, not personal dossiers |
| Digital Products | Prompt packs, templates, workflow kits, internal tooling | Creators, operators, teams | Asset can be sold repeatedly | Validate IP ownership and truth in advertising |
| Affiliate/Partner Content | Comparison pages and tutorials with disclosures | Platforms via commissions, direct sponsors | Content pipeline is automatable | Maintain disclosure and accuracy |

## Recommended Order

### 1. Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene

Reason:

- fastest path to paid pilot with clear operational value
- uses authorized first-party data
- can be productized into recurring retainers

Pilot scope:

- one mailbox or label
- summarize threads
- extract company names, stage, last reply, next action
- write back to a CSV or CRM-safe format

Not allowed:

- exporting a sellable email sheet
- reselling sender identities
- using one client's inbox to market to unrelated third parties

### 2. Autonomous Listing Operations

Reason:

- already adjacent to repo capabilities
- clear before/after metrics
- easier to validate than broad "make money online" mandates

Pilot scope:

- optimize listing titles, descriptions, pricing suggestions, and repost cadence
- generate seller-facing reports

### 3. Research Brief Product

Reason:

- scalable once a repeatable niche is selected
- strong synergy with knowledge and memory layers

Candidate niches:

- local service business software stacks
- niche marketplace demand maps
- supplier and competitor landscape summaries

## Customer Acquisition Without Spam

- publish useful content and capture opt-in leads
- partner with agencies or operators who already own distribution
- use founder-led outreach only to public business contacts with a legitimate B2B basis and low volume, after legal review
- turn satisfied pilot clients into case studies and referrals

Do not depend on harvested personal contact lists.

## Decision Rules

- GO: legality >= 7 and consent >= 7, plus weighted score >= 7.5
- HOLD: passes hard gates but weighted score is between 6.0 and 7.49
- REJECT: fails legality/consent gate or weighted score < 6.0

## Minimum Viable Operating Loop

1. choose lane
2. score lane
3. define narrow offer
4. run one pilot
5. capture SOP and metrics
6. productize the repeatable pieces
7. add a second lane only after the first one has stable evidence
