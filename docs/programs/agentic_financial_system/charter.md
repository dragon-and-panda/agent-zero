# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, legally compliant revenue engine around autonomous services, research products, and repeatable workflows that can operate with low day-to-day supervision.

The system must earn through value creation, explicit consent, and durable assets rather than through data brokerage, spam, or other privacy-invasive shortcuts.

## Success Criteria

1. Revenue comes from products or services that customers knowingly choose to use or buy.
2. Data use stays limited to first-party, consented, or client-authorized sources.
3. Each active revenue lane has a clear operating playbook, pricing model, and measurable unit economics.
4. The agent framework can prioritize, execute, and improve work loops with minimal human input while preserving auditability.

## Rejected Mission Shapes

The following mission variants are out of scope and should be treated as rejected:

- Building or selling scraped email lists.
- Mining inboxes or files to extract third-party contacts for resale.
- Unsolicited bulk outreach campaigns that lack opt-in or lawful basis.
- Revenue plans that depend on violating platform terms or privacy law.

## Approved Initial Revenue Lanes

### 1. Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene Service
- Input: client-owned inboxes, contact forms, and support mailboxes.
- Output: structured CRM records, routing labels, summaries, and follow-up drafts.
- Revenue mode: setup fee plus monthly automation retainer.
- Constraint: process only mailboxes the client controls and only for internal business use.

### 2. Autonomous Listing Concierge
- Input: seller-provided photos, notes, and platform targets.
- Output: upgraded listings, pricing guidance, and compliant marketplace operations.
- Revenue mode: per-listing fee, subscription, or success-based premium support.
- Constraint: no deceptive claims, no fake urgency, and no anti-bot evasion.

### 3. Research and Ops Intelligence Products
- Input: public data, client-authorized internal data, and owned research notes.
- Output: market maps, competitive briefs, pricing studies, workflow dashboards, and recurring insight reports.
- Revenue mode: subscription, project fee, or advisory retainer.
- Constraint: maintain source attribution and avoid personal-data resale.

### 4. Owned Audience and Education Products
- Input: first-party content, case studies, and workflow documentation.
- Output: templates, prompt packs, newsletters, mini-courses, and operator playbooks.
- Revenue mode: direct sales, memberships, and sponsorships.
- Constraint: audience growth must be opt-in.

## Control Principles

- legality before speed
- consent before automation
- first-party data before third-party data
- services before speculation
- repeatable systems before one-off hustle

## Operating Loop

1. Score opportunities for legality, consent, margin, repeatability, and automation fit.
2. Launch the highest-scoring compliant lane.
3. Capture SOPs, pricing, objections, and delivery data in mission docs and memory.
4. Productize successful service steps into templates, tools, or subscriptions.
5. Reinvest profits into better tooling, distribution, and compliance monitoring.

## Required Inputs for Every New Revenue Idea

- customer segment
- problem solved
- data sources used
- proof of data ownership or consent
- acquisition channel
- fulfillment workflow
- monetization model
- key legal or platform constraints
