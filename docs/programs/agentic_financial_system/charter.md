# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission
Build a self-sustaining financial system through legal, ethical, and automatable online ventures that can be operated by Agent Zero with minimal human intervention.

## Non-negotiable constraints
- No scraping, brokering, or selling personal email lists or contact data.
- No non-consensual inbox access, spam, impersonation, or account abuse.
- No workflow may proceed without a lawful data source, clear provenance, and platform-terms compatibility.
- High-risk financial activities must begin in simulation or low-risk service delivery before any live capital exposure.

## Phase 1 revenue lanes
1. Inbox-to-CRM assistant for client-owned, consented inbound leads.
2. Productized research briefs sold as reports, subscriptions, or implementation retainers.
3. Autonomous listing and lead-qualification services using first-party or client-provided data.
4. Internal opportunity scouting that ranks compliant ventures before activation.

## Operating model
- Intake new ideas in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
- Score them with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
- Reject any idea that fails legality, consent, provenance, or TOS gates.
- Record progress and blockers in `journal.md`.
- Keep cross-cutting fixes in `improvements.md`.

## Success metrics
- Positive cash flow from compliant lanes.
- Repeatable acquisition channel that relies on opt-in or client-owned data.
- Clear audit trail for how each venture was selected and validated.
- Minimal manual intervention for routine execution and reporting.
