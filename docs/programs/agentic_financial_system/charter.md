# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, low-touch revenue system on top of Agent Zero using legal, ethical, and repeatable online businesses.

## Non-Goals

This program does not:

- Harvest inboxes or files for personal email extraction.
- Sell contact lists or personal data.
- Run spam-based acquisition strategies.
- Enter regulated financial activity without a separate control framework.

## Approved Strategy

The system should compound through several mutually reinforcing lanes:

1. **Service lane: autonomous listing operations**
   - Help sellers publish higher-quality listings, improve descriptions, and manage marketplace workflows.
2. **Audience lane: opt-in demand capture**
   - Build landing pages, surveys, newsletters, lead magnets, and CRM flows using explicit consent.
3. **Product lane: research and templates**
   - Sell knowledge products, operating playbooks, automation packs, and managed workflows.
4. **Operations lane: first-party CRM automation**
   - Organize, score, summarize, and route inbound conversations for customers who already own the relationship.

## Decision Framework

Every candidate workflow must be evaluated on:

- Legality
- Consent / data provenance
- Time to cash
- Operational complexity
- Ability to automate
- Defensibility
- Cross-sell potential with existing lanes

Any lane that fails legality or consent is rejected regardless of revenue upside.

## Near-Term Priority Queue

### Priority 1: Inbox-to-CRM automation for inbound demand
- Convert compliant, customer-owned inbox traffic into structured CRM records, summaries, and follow-up tasks.
- Focus on inbound leads, support, and transactional workflows.

### Priority 2: Autonomous listing service
- Use the existing listing service scaffold as a monetizable delivery system for clients selling goods online.

### Priority 3: Productized research and playbooks
- Package reusable insights, templates, and automation bundles derived from operating experience.

## Operating Rules

- Use `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md` as the controlling policy for any growth or data workflow.
- Record launches, experiments, and results in the mission journal.
- Score new lanes with `instruments/strategy/score.sh` before activation.
- Prefer first-party data, explicit consent, and high-trust channels.

## Success Criteria

The system is on track when it can:

- Launch at least one compliant revenue lane with minimal operator input.
- Reuse knowledge across lanes.
- Improve conversion, delivery speed, or margins through automation.
- Reject unsafe ideas automatically and route effort toward high-trust monetization.
