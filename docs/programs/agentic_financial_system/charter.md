# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system on top of Agent Zero using ethical, legal, and automation-friendly revenue lanes that can operate with minimal human supervision.

## Important Scope Correction

The original mission included compiling and selling email address lists. That lane is explicitly excluded from this program because it creates privacy, consent, and platform-risk problems. This program replaces it with compliant equivalents centered on first-party data, client-authorized workflows, and service/software revenue.

## Program Thesis

The fastest durable path is not data brokerage. It is a portfolio of agent-assisted products and services that:

- use first-party or client-authorized data;
- solve operational problems people already pay for;
- can be standardized into repeatable tools, prompts, instruments, and SOPs;
- generate reusable knowledge inside the repo over time.

## Phase 1 Revenue Lanes

### Lane A: Inbox-to-CRM Assistant

Primary near-term lane.

**What it does**
- Connects to an authorized mailbox.
- Uses retrieval and classification to identify leads, customers, vendors, invoices, and follow-up obligations.
- Produces structured CRM-style records, task queues, and summaries.
- Keeps raw email access scoped to the user's own account or a client-authorized mailbox.

**Why it fits**
- high automation potential;
- strong value to small businesses and operators;
- converts existing communication chaos into operational clarity;
- does not require reselling personal data.

**Allowed outputs**
- relationship summaries;
- lead/opportunity extraction for the mailbox owner;
- vendor and customer status dashboards;
- follow-up recommendations;
- CRM sync payloads.

### Lane B: Autonomous Listing Service

Use the existing listing-service direction as a compliant service business. This offers immediate operational value without depending on questionable data acquisition.

### Lane C: Research and Intelligence Products

Turn lawful research workflows into paid deliverables:

- market maps;
- supplier intelligence;
- niche briefings;
- workflow libraries;
- premium templates and playbooks.

### Lane D: Client-Owned Operations Automation

Automate repetitive client workflows using their own data:

- inbox triage;
- support classification;
- procurement routing;
- lead qualification for inbound or opted-in contacts;
- internal knowledge capture.

## Orange DataScaping Usage

Orange DataScaping or similar analysis tools may be used only for:

- organizing first-party or client-authorized data;
- deduplication and clustering;
- funnel and response analysis;
- segmentation of opted-in contacts.

It must not be used to prepare personal-data resale inventories.

## Operating Guardrails

- Follow `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`.
- Reject any lane with low legality, weak consent, unclear provenance, or likely terms-of-service conflict.
- Record every accepted lane and the reason it passed.
- Prefer products and services over brokerage.

## Phase 1 Success Criteria

1. At least one lane produces a repeatable, documented workflow.
2. The workflow can be run by the agent with bounded human review.
3. All data sources have documented provenance and purpose.
4. The repo contains reusable prompts, instruments, and SOPs for the lane.

## Immediate Build Priorities

1. Establish intake and scoring for candidate revenue lanes.
2. Prioritize the Inbox-to-CRM lane.
3. Define a lawful email RAG pattern that avoids bulk extraction-for-resale.
4. Reuse adjacent service lanes already present in the repo.
5. Keep a mission diary so future automation runs can continue execution cleanly.
