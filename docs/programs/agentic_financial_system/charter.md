# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system through lawful, ethical online ventures that can be operated with high autonomy and low human overhead.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- No personal-data brokerage.
- No harvested contact lists.
- No inbox scraping for resale.
- No spam or unsolicited bulk outreach.
- No revenue lane that depends on violating platform rules.

The system should replace any prohibited idea with a compliant alternative that preserves the commercial intent.

## Strategic objective

Create a portfolio of small, repeatable revenue lanes that share tooling, memory, scoring, and operating discipline.

## Phase 1 portfolio

### Lane A: Inbox-to-CRM assistant
- Use case: user-owned or client-owned email accounts.
- Outcome: turn inbound messages into structured opportunities, support cases, and follow-ups.
- Revenue model: subscription, setup fee, or managed service.
- Constraint: data remains owned by the customer and is never resold.

### Lane B: Autonomous listing and concierge services
- Use case: client-provided products, inventory, or listings.
- Outcome: improved listings, channel posting, inquiry triage, and follow-up.
- Revenue model: per listing, subscription, or success fee.

### Lane C: Research and market intelligence products
- Use case: lawful public or licensed data collection and synthesis.
- Outcome: niche reports, buyer guides, supplier maps, or opportunity dashboards.
- Revenue model: paid reports, memberships, or client retainers.

### Lane D: Opt-in demand capture
- Use case: forms, newsletters, waitlists, gated reports, and first-party communities.
- Outcome: consented lead pipelines the business owns directly.
- Revenue model: downstream product or service sales, sponsorship, or subscriptions.

## Venture scoring standard

Every candidate lane must be scored before activation:

- Hard gates: legality, consent, data provenance, platform risk
- Soft factors: time to cash, margin, repeatability, automation, defensibility

Only lanes that clear the hard gates may move into build or launch status.

## Operating loop

1. Capture candidate ideas in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Score each lane with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
3. Use the `revenue_planning` tool to convert promising ideas into execution plans.
4. Log experiments, outcomes, and blockers in `journal.md`.
5. Promote durable improvements into `improvements.md`.

## Success criteria

- At least one active lane with clear customer value.
- Revenue plans that are repeatable and automatable.
- Compliance pack referenced before any growth or data workflow.
- No launch blocked by weak consent, weak provenance, or high platform risk.
