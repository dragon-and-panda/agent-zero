# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, low-touch financial system on top of Agent Zero by pursuing lawful, ethical, and repeatable online revenue lanes.

## Explicit Non-Goals

The system will not:

- traffic in personal email lists;
- monetize scraped contact data;
- use inbox access to create resale databases;
- rely on spam, deception, or Terms-of-Service evasion.

## Program Principles

1. **Compliance first:** No revenue lane is worth launching if legality, consent, or provenance are weak.
2. **First-party advantage:** Prefer workflows based on user-owned inboxes, owned audiences, client-provided data, and public research.
3. **Automation with auditability:** Every lane should leave behind a decision trail, source metadata, and measurable KPIs.
4. **Small repeatable systems over one-off wins:** The target is compounding process quality, not opportunistic extraction.

## Initial Revenue Lanes

| Lane | Description | Data Policy | Target Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inbox-to-CRM Assistant | Use RAG over a user-owned Gmail or team inbox to identify real opportunities, summarize threads, and draft next actions for existing relationships. | First-party inbox only, documented authorization, no resale. | Follow-up queue, CRM notes, drafted responses. |
| Opt-In Audience Engine | Turn owned traffic into subscribers through forms, lead magnets, or newsletters, then segment and nurture that audience. | Opt-in records only. | Subscriber growth, booked calls, direct sales. |
| Productized Research Studio | Package recurring public-domain or client-supplied research into reports, databases, and monitoring products. | Public, licensed, or client-owned data only. | Paid briefs, subscriptions, custom reports. |
| Workflow Automation Services | Sell internal tools and automations to businesses using their own inbox, CRM, and operational data. | Client-controlled data, scope-bound access. | Setup fees, retainers, usage-based revenue. |
| Autonomous Listing Service | Expand the existing listing-oriented service into a durable monetization lane. | Lawful marketplace data and client-owned assets. | Listing management revenue. |

## Gmail / RAG Lane Definition

The compliant version of the inbox request is:

1. Connect a user-owned mailbox or approved shared mailbox.
2. Retrieve only emails relevant to support, sales follow-up, partnerships, or account management.
3. Extract structured opportunity fields such as company, thread state, next action, urgency, and relationship type.
4. Push those fields into an internal CRM or task queue with provenance attached.
5. Never classify the output as a list for sale or external distribution.

## Orange Workflow Definition

Orange can support the system by analyzing approved first-party datasets, for example:

- grouping existing customers by intent or issue type;
- spotting follow-up clusters in inbox-derived CRM records;
- identifying which opt-in segments respond best to offers;
- scoring opportunity quality in client-owned pipelines.

Orange should be treated as an internal analytics step, not as a contact-harvesting engine.

## Phase Priorities

### Phase 0: Safety and Scoring
- Add a compliance pack.
- Add mission intake and scoring.
- Reject non-compliant monetization ideas at intake time.

### Phase 1: Fast, Compliant Cashflow
- Launch Inbox-to-CRM assistant for first-party mailboxes.
- Launch opt-in audience capture and nurture loops.
- Package one repeatable research product.

### Phase 2: Scale and Diversify
- Add more service/product lanes only after they clear the same scoring gates.
- Increase delegation, telemetry, and weekly review automation.

## Success Criteria

- Every active lane passes legality, consent, provenance, and TOS gates.
- At least one lane produces repeatable revenue without requiring new policy exceptions.
- Mission journals and scoring outputs make future autonomous improvement straightforward.
