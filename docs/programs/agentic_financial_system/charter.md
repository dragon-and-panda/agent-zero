# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, compliance-first portfolio of online ventures powered by Agent Zero. The system should prioritize repeatable value creation, low-touch operation, and measurable profitability without relying on privacy abuse, spam, or illegal data acquisition.

## Strategic Objective

Create a portfolio of revenue lanes that can be researched, launched, and optimized by agents with minimal human oversight while preserving:

- legality
- consent
- platform compliance
- customer trust
- auditability

## Non-Goals

This program does not include:

- compiling or selling personal email lists
- scraping Gmail or other inboxes to build marketing databases
- mass unsolicited outreach
- resale of private or ambiguously sourced personal data
- fraudulent, deceptive, or manipulative growth tactics

## Approved Revenue Lanes

### Lane 1: Productized Automation Services

Offer narrowly scoped automation builds for businesses, such as:

- workflow automation audits
- internal agent setup
- document or support triage systems
- reporting and dashboard automation

Why it fits:

- clear customer value
- low inventory risk
- fast path to cash flow

### Lane 2: Micro-SaaS and Internal Tools

Convert repeatable service workflows into software products:

- lead qualification for opted-in inbound leads
- knowledge retrieval for teams
- proposal generation
- niche operations dashboards

Why it fits:

- compounding revenue
- strong reuse of Agent Zero capabilities

### Lane 3: Research and Data Products

Sell intelligence products only when the source data is public, licensed, or fully authorized:

- market maps
- vendor databases from public sources
- pricing monitors
- trend digests

Why it fits:

- aligned with agentic research strengths
- can become subscription content

### Lane 4: Media, Education, and Distribution

Build owned distribution with consent-based channels:

- newsletters
- tutorials
- templates
- affiliate partnerships
- community memberships

Why it fits:

- lowers customer acquisition cost over time
- creates defensible demand for products and services

## Permitted Email RAG Usage

Email data may be used only for owned or authorized inbox workflows such as:

- summarizing inbound demand
- extracting product feedback
- routing support requests
- identifying frequently requested services

Email-derived insights must stay inside the operating system and may not be transformed into contact lists for sale.

## Operating Loop

1. Intake opportunities into `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Score them with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
3. Reject ideas that fail legality, consent, provenance, or platform checks.
4. Advance approved ideas into experiments with a clear offer, customer, and channel.
5. Record results in the program journal and improvement backlog.

## Initial Success Metrics

- number of pass-rated ventures in the pipeline
- number of launched experiments
- revenue per lane
- gross margin by lane
- percentage of work automated
- zero compliance violations

## Immediate Next Focus

1. validate the scoring and governance workflow
2. prioritize one service lane and one product lane
3. use authorized internal data only for discovery
4. build consent-based acquisition systems before any scale efforts
