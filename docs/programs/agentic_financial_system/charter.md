# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining portfolio of online ventures that can be operated largely by agents while remaining lawful, privacy-respecting, and economically durable.

## Operating Principle

The system must earn revenue by creating useful products and services, not by extracting or reselling personal data.

## Primary Lanes

### Lane 1: Inbox-to-CRM Automation for Opt-In Customers

- Use RAG over the customer's own email and files.
- Extract tasks, follow-ups, and relationship summaries for the account owner.
- Convert this into a paid service or SaaS for small businesses, freelancers, or sales teams.

### Lane 2: Autonomous Listing Concierge

- Use the existing listing-service blueprint to help sellers produce better listings, manage inquiries, and close sales within guardrails.
- Monetize via subscription, transaction fees, premium add-ons, or white-label deployments.

### Lane 3: Research Products and Knowledge Assets

- Build paid reports, workflows, templates, prompt packs, and niche intelligence products.
- Monetize via subscriptions, one-off purchases, or consulting upsells.

### Lane 4: Consent-Based Workflow Automation

- Offer automations that save time for customers using their own systems and data.
- Focus on repeatable B2B use cases with clear ROI and low compliance overhead.

## Explicitly Rejected Lane

- Contact-list brokerage, inbox-derived email list resale, or any model that depends on spam or privacy abuse.

## Success Metrics

- Monthly recurring revenue or recurring service revenue
- Gross margin after model and infrastructure costs
- Time-to-cash for each lane
- Customer retention and repeat usage
- Compliance incident count, which should stay at zero

## Hard Constraints

- Follow `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md` before launching any lane.
- Reject ideas with weak legality, weak consent, unclear provenance, or platform-rule conflicts.
- Prefer products and services with durable customer value and defensible workflows.

## Near-Term Priority

Start with the compliant inbox-to-CRM lane first because it reuses the repo's RAG, memory, and workflow strengths without requiring third-party data brokerage.
