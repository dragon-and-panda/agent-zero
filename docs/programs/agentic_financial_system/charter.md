# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining portfolio of legal, ethical, automation-assisted revenue streams that can operate with minimal supervision while preserving user trust, privacy, and platform compliance.

## Success criteria

- recurring revenue from value-creating products or services
- repeatable opportunity scoring and launch workflows
- clear PASS/HOLD/REJECT governance on sensitive ideas
- reusable SOPs for research, pricing, delivery, and customer support

## Non-goals

- monetization through personal-data resale
- inbox scraping without authorization
- spam-based lead generation
- policy evasion or deceptive automation

## Approved venture classes

1. First-party analytics and operations services
2. Productized research and niche intelligence
3. Seller-authorized listing and marketplace operations
4. Opt-in audience businesses
5. Software subscriptions and internal copilots

## Standard workflow

1. Intake idea in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Score with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
3. Review with the `revenue_planning` tool.
4. Launch only with a PASS verdict or after HOLD issues are resolved.
5. Log outcomes in `journal.md` and add improvements to `improvements.md`.
