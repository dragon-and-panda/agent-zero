# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system through lawful, consent-based, automatable online ventures that can compound over time with minimal supervision.

## Non-goals

The program will not pursue:

- contact-list brokerage
- Gmail scraping for resale
- personal-data resale from inboxes or files
- spam-based lead generation
- policy-evasion growth tactics

## Approved venture lanes

### Lane 1: first-party operations services

Primary pattern: client-authorized inbox-to-CRM hygiene, lead routing, response triage, and CRM cleanup.

Why it fits:

- high trust
- recurring service revenue
- clear authorization model
- easy to audit

### Lane 2: autonomous listing and marketplace optimization

Primary pattern: use the existing autonomous listing service to improve titles, descriptions, images, and publishing workflows for inventory owners.

Related doc: `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md`

### Lane 3: research products

Primary pattern: turn lawful public or licensed data into paid briefs, market maps, lead magnets, and subscription intelligence products.

### Lane 4: affiliate and referral systems

Primary pattern: ethical content, comparison pages, or niche automation assets with transparent disclosures and opt-in acquisition.

## Core operating loop

1. generate opportunity ideas
2. screen them with `revenue_planning`
3. score them with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
4. run a constrained pilot
5. measure revenue, margin, repeatability, and compliance friction
6. standardize the winning lane into tools, prompts, and instruments

## Acceptance criteria for a live lane

A lane is eligible for ongoing automation only if it:

- clears legality, consent, provenance, and platform gates
- produces auditable output for a first-party owner or customer
- has a repeatable fulfillment loop
- avoids dependence on a single fragile exploit or policy gap

## Initial KPIs

- monthly recurring revenue by lane
- gross margin by lane
- fulfillment minutes per delivered unit
- percentage of work handled autonomously
- compliance exceptions raised per month
