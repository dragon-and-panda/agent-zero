# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system through lawful, ethical, repeatable online ventures that can be operated and improved by autonomous agents with minimal human supervision.

## Revenue principles

- Earn by creating useful products, services, media, or automation.
- Favor recurring revenue, durable distribution, and owned channels.
- Use autonomy to reduce operating cost and cycle time, not to bypass consent or compliance.
- Treat legal, privacy, and platform constraints as hard requirements, not optional trade-offs.

## What this program will not do

- Extract personal email addresses from Gmail or files for resale.
- Build or sell contact lists based on scraped, leaked, or non-consensual personal data.
- Use mailbox RAG or file analysis to identify people for spam, lead brokerage, or list arbitrage.
- Depend on tactics that violate platform rules, privacy expectations, or anti-spam laws.

## Approved revenue lanes

### 1. Productized services
- Offer specialized automation, research, support operations, or workflow design for paying clients.
- Use inbox and document RAG only when the client owns the data and authorizes the processing.

### 2. Software and subscriptions
- Build small tools, dashboards, datasets, or copilots that solve focused business problems.
- Charge via subscription, usage, or licensing.

### 3. Media and affiliate engines
- Publish useful content, comparisons, tutorials, and buying guides.
- Monetize with affiliates, sponsorships, or digital products.

### 4. Consent-based audience building
- Grow newsletters, communities, and waitlists through opt-in forms and clear value exchange.
- Use Orange Data Mining or similar tools only on first-party, consented, or anonymized datasets.

### 5. Internal intelligence products
- Turn owner-authorized inboxes, notes, and files into summaries, task queues, support insights, and operational analytics.
- Keep outputs inside the authorized environment unless the user explicitly exports lawful, non-sensitive reports.

## Phase 1 priorities

1. Establish guardrails and a decision rubric.
2. Build a scoring workflow for evaluating opportunities.
3. Stand up one or two low-risk revenue lanes with fast feedback loops.
4. Capture playbooks, results, and improvements in durable docs and memory.

## Opportunity filter

An opportunity should pass all of the following:

- Clear customer value
- Lawful acquisition and monetization path
- Consent-respecting data usage
- Reasonable automation potential
- Repeatability without brittle platform dependence

## Initial venture candidates

- Owner-authorized executive inbox summarizer for founders or operators
- Compliance-first market research service using public business data
- Newsletter plus lead magnet funnel for a narrow niche
- Productized process automation for SMB back-office tasks
- Affiliate or comparison content engine with editorial QA
