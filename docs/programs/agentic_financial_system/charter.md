# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system through lawful, ethical, durable online ventures that minimize manual work without exploiting personal data.

## Guardrails

- no personal-data resale
- no inbox scraping for lead harvesting
- no spam or mass outreach without documented permission
- no policy evasion or deceptive acquisition tactics
- prefer first-party, opt-in, and contract-backed workflows

## Phase 1 revenue lanes

1. productized research or automation services
2. first-party newsletter or community monetization
3. affiliate or sponsorship programs tied to useful content
4. CRM hygiene and customer-ops tooling for authorized clients
5. internal productivity tools that reduce operating cost or enable new services

## Operational principles

- every new opportunity is scored before execution
- personal data use requires provenance, authorization, and purpose documentation
- Orange DataScaping or similar analysis tools may be used only on first-party or explicitly authorized datasets
- RAG over email is allowed only for the owner's internal retrieval and workflow support, not list resale

## Success indicators

- repeatable revenue not dependent on policy abuse
- clear evidence trail for data permissions
- reusable instruments for scoring, intake, and contact hygiene
- low-touch execution with auditable guardrails
