# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system through legal, ethical, and automatable online ventures that can compound over time with minimal human supervision.

This program explicitly excludes personal-data resale, harvested contact brokerage, inbox scraping for list building, and other privacy-invasive monetization schemes.

## Program Constraints

- Follow `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md` for every monetization and outreach workflow.
- Prefer recurring, defensible revenue over short-term extraction.
- Use automation to reduce operating cost, not to evade law, platform rules, or consent requirements.
- Keep human approvals lightweight but mandatory for new external-facing campaigns and regulated workflows.

## Revenue Lanes

### Lane 1: Productized Services
- Offer narrow, repeatable services that the agent can help scope, fulfill, and document.
- Example categories: listing optimization, workflow automation, research synthesis, documentation systems, analytics reporting.
- Advantage: fastest path to cash with low inventory risk.

### Lane 2: Digital Products
- Create templates, playbooks, datasets without personal information, calculators, guides, and niche software utilities.
- Use the framework for research, packaging, pricing experiments, and customer support.
- Advantage: scalable distribution with high gross margin.

### Lane 3: Opt-In Audience Engine
- Build first-party lead capture via landing pages, newsletters, waitlists, and downloads.
- Use RAG only over consented assets such as product docs, support tickets, internal SOPs, and customer-approved CRM exports.
- Advantage: compounds distribution while remaining privacy-safe.

### Lane 4: Marketplace and Listing Operations
- Operate or support listings, directories, service marketplaces, or catalog businesses.
- Reuse the adjacent blueprint in `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md`.
- Advantage: structured, operationally measurable, and compatible with agent automation.

### Lane 5: Research and Benchmark Products
- Publish niche market maps, pricing studies, benchmark reports, and intelligence digests built from lawful, non-personal data.
- Advantage: monetizes synthesis and distribution rather than personal data.

## Explicitly Rejected Lane

The following lane is out of scope and must remain disabled:

- Harvesting emails from Gmail or local files to compile lists for sale or cold outreach.

That lane fails the consent, provenance, and anti-spam requirements in the compliance pack and is not allowed even if it appears profitable.

## Agent Roles

- **Apex Orchestrator:** Chooses which lane to activate based on score, capacity, and cash-flow fit.
- **Compliance Guardian:** Blocks non-compliant data use and ensures every lane cites the policy pack.
- **Offer Builder:** Turns capabilities into concrete service or product offers.
- **Audience Builder:** Runs opt-in funnels, content calendars, and newsletter experiments.
- **Revenue Analyst:** Tracks payback, margin, retention, and repeatability.
- **Operations Agent:** Standardizes delivery, SOPs, and instrumentation.

## Activation Protocol

Before a lane is activated, the orchestrator must:

1. Write the candidate idea into `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Score it with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
3. Reject it immediately if legality, consent, provenance, or terms compliance are not all strong.
4. Prefer lanes that are repeatable, automatable, margin-accretive, and defensible.
5. Record rationale and next actions in the mission journal.

## Phase 1 Priority

Start with the safest and shortest feedback loops:

1. Productized services
2. Opt-in audience engine
3. Digital products
4. Marketplace/listing operations
5. Research products

This sequencing creates revenue while building first-party distribution and reusable delivery assets.
