# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system through legal, ethical, and automation-friendly online ventures that can be operated with minimal human supervision.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- no brokering or resale of personal email lists
- no spam or non-consensual outreach
- no unauthorized access to inboxes, accounts, or datasets
- no platform evasion, anti-bot circumvention, or deceptive automation
- no deployment of regulated financial activity without the required compliance path

## Operating thesis

The system should favor revenue lanes that compound through software, reusable knowledge, and lawful first-party data rather than through gray-market acquisition tactics.

## Preferred venture lanes

### Lane 1: Authorized inbox-to-CRM operations

Use RAG and structured extraction on user-authorized business email accounts to:

- triage inbound demand
- extract leads that already opted in or initiated contact
- update CRM records
- create follow-up tasks and pipeline summaries

Output is internal business intelligence, not a salable contact dataset.

### Lane 2: Autonomous listing and resale services

Operate tooling that helps a seller turn inventory into listings, manage negotiations, and close sales across permitted channels.

### Lane 3: Productized research and intelligence

Generate market maps, competitor snapshots, sourcing intelligence, pricing briefs, and operational playbooks that can be sold as reports or subscriptions.

### Lane 4: Opt-in audience and publisher assets

Build newsletters, communities, calculators, directories, and educational products where subscribers knowingly opt in and value compounds over time.

## Prioritization logic

Choose work that maximizes:

- legal clarity
- consent clarity
- fast validation loops
- reusable automation
- defensible process or data advantage
- low operational overhead

## Required artifacts per lane

Each lane should maintain:

- a scoring record in `instruments/strategy/`
- a running journal entry in `journal.md`
- a backlog item in `improvements.md`
- explicit success metrics and stop conditions

## Exit rule

If a lane trends toward privacy abuse, weak provenance, or terms-of-service conflict, freeze it and replace it with a compliant alternative.
