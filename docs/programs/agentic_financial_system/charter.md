# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, legally compliant revenue system using autonomous research, productization, and operations inside Agent Zero.

## Non-Negotiable Guardrails

- No harvesting, brokering, or selling personal email lists.
- No inbox or Google account access without explicit owner authorization.
- No spam, deception, or platform-rule evasion.
- No monetization lane launches without passing the scoring and compliance gates.

See `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md` for the governing rules.

## Initial Revenue Thesis

The system should prioritize recurring and defensible revenue over short-term gray-area tactics. The first operating principle is to monetize capability, not personal data.

## Phase Structure

### Phase 0: Foundation

- Encode compliance rules in prompts, docs, and scoring instruments.
- Create a shortlist of candidate revenue lanes.
- Score each lane for legality, consent, repeatability, margin, and execution fit.

### Phase 1: Fastest Compliant Cashflow

Prioritize low-complexity, first-party service lanes:

1. Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene Service
   - Use client-authorized inbox exports or APIs.
   - Deduplicate contacts, classify conversations, draft CRM entries, and produce follow-up tasks.
   - Deliver value to the mailbox owner; do not resell data.

2. Autonomous Listing Operations
   - Reuse the repo's listing-service direction for marketplace optimization, content syndication, and response automation.

3. Research and Monitoring Briefs
   - Sell niche research digests, supplier maps, pricing monitors, or market watch products built from public or licensed data.

### Phase 2: Productization

- Convert the most successful service lane into a repeatable product or subscription.
- Add dashboards, reporting, and client self-service controls.
- Build retention through measurable ROI and operational lock-in.

### Phase 3: Portfolio Expansion

- Add adjacent compliant revenue lanes such as affiliate content, internal tooling subscriptions, or vertical intelligence products.
- Use the scoring instrument before activating each new lane.

## Operating Metrics

Track at minimum:

- monthly recurring revenue or retained service revenue;
- gross margin by lane;
- hours or tokens per dollar earned;
- client retention or repeat purchase rate;
- compliance incidents and rejected ideas;
- conversion rate from experiment to active lane.

## Decision Rule

If a proposed idea depends on unconsented personal data or inbox-derived list sales, mark it REJECT and replace it with a consent-based equivalent.
