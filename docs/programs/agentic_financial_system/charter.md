# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission
Build a self-sustaining revenue system that uses Agent Zero to discover, launch, and improve lawful, ethical, and automation-friendly online ventures with minimal supervision.

## Non-negotiable constraints
- No scraping, brokering, or sale of personal email addresses or contact lists.
- No use of inbox, CRM, or platform data without explicit authorization from the data owner.
- No spam, deceptive outreach, impersonation, or terms-of-service circumvention.
- No trading, lending, or payments automation without defined risk limits, logging, and human review where required.
- Every venture must pass legality, consent, provenance, and platform-policy checks before execution.

## Approved revenue lanes
1. Opt-in lead capture and CRM workflows
   - Build landing pages, waitlists, newsletters, and lead magnets.
   - Use only consented first-party data.
   - Monetize via product sales, booked calls, or subscriptions.

2. Autonomous listing and resale operations
   - Help a lawful seller improve listings, pricing, and marketplace operations.
   - Monetize through service fees, SaaS subscriptions, or revenue share with the seller.

3. Research products and intelligence briefs
   - Produce niche market reports, benchmarks, and workflow templates.
   - Monetize via subscriptions, one-off reports, or consulting retainers.

4. Service automation for small businesses
   - Automate intake, proposal drafting, support triage, knowledge capture, or scheduling.
   - Monetize via setup fees, retainers, and usage-based plans.

5. Digital product incubation
   - Create templates, internal tools, prompt packs, playbooks, and micro-SaaS products.
   - Monetize via marketplaces, subscriptions, and direct sales.

## Operating sequence
1. Intake new venture ideas in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Score each lane with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
3. Reject anything that fails hard gates.
4. For viable lanes, create an execution brief with the `revenue_planning` tool.
5. Run only low-risk experiments first and log outcomes to the mission journal.
6. Promote successful playbooks into reusable docs, prompts, tools, or instruments.

## Primary KPI categories
- Revenue quality: gross revenue, contribution margin, payback period.
- Repeatability: proportion of work automated, operator minutes per sale, variance across runs.
- Compliance: consent coverage, policy incidents, takedowns, complaint rate.
- Durability: retention, repeat purchase rate, referral rate, defensibility.

## Initial priority order
1. Opt-in inbox-to-CRM workflow service.
2. Autonomous listing service expansion.
3. Research-product pipeline for niche operational intelligence.
4. Productized automation services for SMB workflows.
