# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Objective

Launch a portfolio of compliant revenue experiments that can be run and improved by Agent Zero with minimal human intervention.

## Guardrails

- follow `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`
- use only first-party, consented, or properly licensed data
- do not monetize by selling access to personal data
- keep every acquisition channel within its platform rules

## Initial workstreams

1. productized service offers
2. digital products and templates
3. opt-in audience building
4. marketplace listing and fulfillment automation
5. internal tooling that can become a SaaS offer

## Operating metrics

- revenue per experiment
- gross margin
- opt-in conversion rate
- activation rate
- retention or repeat purchase rate
- compliance incidents

## Exit criteria for an experiment

Stop or redesign an experiment if:

- consent or provenance is unclear
- the channel depends on policy evasion
- unit economics stay negative
- customers do not receive clear value
