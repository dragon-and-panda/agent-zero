# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, ethical, and legally compliant revenue system using Agent Zero as the operating framework.

The system should maximize:

- durable revenue
- repeatable workflows
- low-touch operations
- data provenance and auditability
- platform-safe automation

It must not depend on privacy abuse, contact-list brokerage, spam, credential misuse, or deceptive acquisition tactics.

## Phase 1 revenue lanes

### Lane A: Authorized inbox-to-CRM intelligence

Use owner-authorized Gmail or email exports with RAG to:

- summarize threads
- extract qualified opportunities, tasks, and follow-ups
- organize records for internal CRM use
- export structured datasets for Orange Data Mining or similar analysis tools

Monetization path:

- sell the workflow as a service to a business that already owns the inbox and contacts
- package the workflow into software or a managed ops offering

Explicit boundary:

- no resale of extracted addresses
- no third-party list brokerage
- no cold outreach based on non-consensual inbox harvesting

### Lane B: Autonomous listing service

Use the seller-authorized listing workflow documented in `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md` to create and syndicate listings, manage inquiries, and improve sell-through.

Monetization path:

- listing fees
- subscription SaaS
- revenue-share on closed sales

### Lane C: Research and intelligence products

Build paid reports, market maps, pricing briefs, sourcing packs, and workflow templates grounded in public or licensed data.

Monetization path:

- one-off reports
- subscriptions
- premium libraries and templates

### Lane D: Opt-in audience and funnel systems

Create newsletters, lead magnets, landing pages, and waitlists where contacts knowingly opt in.

Monetization path:

- affiliate revenue
- sponsorships
- software or service upsells

## Operating rules

1. Screen each new lane with the strategy score instrument and the `revenue_planning` tool.
2. Treat legality, consent, provenance, and platform compliance as hard gates.
3. Prefer first-party or client-owned data over scraped or purchased data.
4. Log assumptions, experiments, and results in the program journal.
5. Pause any workflow that drifts toward privacy-invasive or deceptive behavior.

## Success metrics

- positive gross margin
- repeatable acquisition channel
- documented compliant data path
- automatable delivery steps
- measurable customer value

## Stop conditions

Stop or redesign a lane if:

- it requires non-consensual personal-data collection
- platform rules forbid the automation pattern
- economics depend on spam or list resale
- auditability or consent evidence is missing
