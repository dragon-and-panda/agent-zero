# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a durable, low-touch revenue system using lawful, consent-based online ventures that can be operated and improved by Agent Zero with human oversight at approval boundaries.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Selling personal email lists
- Scraping private inboxes or contact databases
- Spam-based acquisition
- Any workflow that depends on deceptive or privacy-invasive behavior

## Approved venture lanes

1. **Digital products**
   - Templates
   - Research packs
   - Playbooks
   - Lightweight software utilities

2. **Productized services**
   - Prospect research on public company data
   - Internal automation for consenting clients
   - Listing optimization for the operator's own inventory

3. **Marketplace commerce**
   - Listings for owned products or authorized partner inventory
   - Service packages sold through compliant marketplaces

4. **Audience and trust assets**
   - Opt-in newsletter
   - Educational content
   - Case studies
   - Affiliate content with disclosures

## Operating loop

1. Capture candidate ideas in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`
2. Score them with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
3. Use `revenue_planning` to shape a compliant execution plan
4. Launch only after a human approves the channel, offer, and data usage
5. Measure revenue, conversion, churn, and compliance incidents
6. Record improvements in `improvements.md`

## Success metrics

- Revenue is attributable to approved channels
- Data use remains first-party or permissioned
- No compliance incidents
- Repeatable offers outperform one-off manual work

## Guardrails

- Prefer first-party data over third-party data
- Prefer opt-in distribution over unsolicited outreach
- Prefer platform-native sales flows over custom gray-area automations
- Require evidence before scaling spend or automation
