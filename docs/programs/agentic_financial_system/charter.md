# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Objective

Build a self-sustaining, low-touch revenue system on top of Agent Zero that is profitable, auditable, and compliant by default.

The program should optimize for repeatable value creation rather than quick-win tactics that depend on privacy abuse, spam, or unclear data rights.

## Mission constraints

The system must:

- operate through ethical and legal revenue channels,
- rely on first-party, consented, licensed, or public-nonpersonal data,
- avoid selling personal email lists or mailbox-derived contacts,
- document every monetization lane, decision, and rejection,
- favor durable assets such as software, content, research, and operations tooling.

## Non-goals

This program will not pursue:

- email-list compilation for sale,
- inbox scraping for prospect harvesting,
- mass unsolicited outreach,
- reselling personal data,
- growth tactics that depend on evading platform policy.

## Program lanes

### Lane 1: Agent Operations as a Service

Offer autonomous workflow setup, reporting, and internal tooling for small businesses.

Candidate deliverables:

- sales-ops dashboards on business-owned CRM data,
- support triage and knowledge assistants,
- inbox summarization for the mailbox owner,
- internal research copilots using licensed documents.

### Lane 2: Productized Research

Package research and monitoring outputs into paid recurring products.

Candidate deliverables:

- industry watch briefs,
- competitor monitoring reports,
- niche intelligence dashboards,
- workflow and tooling benchmarks.

Only use licensed, owned, or public-nonpersonal sources.

### Lane 3: Opt-In Audience Assets

Build assets where the audience intentionally subscribes.

Examples:

- opt-in newsletter,
- paid community,
- premium template library,
- gated playbooks exchanged for explicit signup consent.

### Lane 4: Compliance-First Data Services

Provide data cleaning, structuring, classification, and enrichment services for datasets the operator already owns or is authorized to process.

Examples:

- CRM deduplication,
- support ticket tagging,
- vendor and invoice classification,
- consented lead routing.

## Handling mailbox data with RAG

RAG over email can be used only for operator-owned or explicitly authorized accounts and only for internal productivity or customer-service purposes.

Approved uses:

- summarize threads,
- extract tasks, commitments, and invoices,
- retrieve prior decisions,
- group similar support issues,
- draft replies for the account owner to approve.

Rejected uses:

- turning mailbox data into saleable contact lists,
- identifying people for cold outreach without consent,
- exporting third-party contacts for brokerage.

## Operating workflow

1. Add candidate ideas to `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Score each idea using `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
3. Record the decision and rationale in `journal.md`.
4. Promote promising items to implementation only if they pass compliance gates.
5. Track new experiments and upgrades in `improvements.md`.

## Initial opportunity priorities

### Priority A: Consent-based inbox operations assistant

Build a workflow that helps a business owner search, summarize, and operationalize their own email.

Monetization:

- subscription access,
- setup fee,
- support retainer.

### Priority B: Niche research brief engine

Build a recurring analyst product using lawful sources and strong attribution.

Monetization:

- monthly subscription,
- premium archive,
- custom report upsells.

### Priority C: CRM cleanup and routing service

Build structured classification and workflow automation for existing business datasets.

Monetization:

- implementation projects,
- recurring automation support,
- premium dashboards.

## Definition of success

The program is successful when it creates repeatable revenue from value-added services or products while maintaining:

- explicit consent boundaries,
- platform-policy compliance,
- auditable data provenance,
- low operational overhead,
- a documented improvement loop.
