# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining revenue system composed of legal, ethical, consent-based online ventures that can be operated by agents with minimal supervision.

## Constraints

- no personal-data resale
- no non-consensual inbox access
- no spam or bought lead lists
- no terms-of-service evasion
- no live capital deployment without hard risk controls

## Active revenue lanes

1. inbox-to-CRM automation for opted-in business contacts
2. autonomous listing and resale services for client-owned inventory
3. research and analytics products based on public, licensed, or user-supplied data
4. workflow automation retainers for operators who control their own systems and data

## Execution standard

Each lane must be:

- documented
- scored before activation
- reversible if risk rises
- measurable on time-to-cash, gross margin, repeatability, automation depth, and defensibility

## Phase policy

### Phase 0: validation

- define the offer
- identify lawful inputs
- confirm consent and provenance
- score the lane with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`

### Phase 1: service revenue

- prefer high-trust, low-capital lanes
- start with workflows and services using client-owned assets and data
- prioritize fast feedback loops over speculative scale

### Phase 2: productization

- convert repeated service motions into reusable tools, prompts, and instruments
- productize only lanes with repeatable demand and clean compliance posture

### Phase 3: capital deployment

- simulation first
- only consider controlled capital allocation after reserves, monitoring, and drawdown limits are defined and passing

## Default substitution rule

If a proposed lane depends on scraping contact data, compiling email lists, or reselling personal information, reject it and replace it with a consent-based equivalent.
