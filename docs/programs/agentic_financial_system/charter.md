# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining portfolio of online ventures that can be operated with high automation and low human supervision while remaining legal, ethical, auditable, and commercially useful.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

- no personal-data resale;
- no inbox scraping without explicit authorization from the inbox owner;
- no spam or abusive outreach;
- no platform evasion or anti-bot circumvention;
- no deceptive claims, fake identities, or fabricated inventory;
- no expansion into higher-risk regulated workflows without dedicated controls.

## Operating Thesis

The system should compound by launching several small, lawful revenue lanes rather than relying on a single fragile tactic.

## Initial Revenue Lanes

1. Inbox-to-CRM assistant
   - customer: the inbox owner;
   - value: turn inbound demand, receipts, leads, and follow-ups into structured CRM tasks;
   - data rule: only user-connected or customer-owned accounts.
2. Autonomous listing service
   - customer: sellers who want help creating, publishing, and managing listings;
   - value: higher-quality listings and faster response handling.
3. Research and intelligence products
   - customer: operators who pay for curated market signals, summaries, benchmarks, or alerts;
   - value: synthesis, monitoring, and workflow acceleration.
4. Opt-in audience products
   - customer: subscribers, prospects, or communities that explicitly joined;
   - value: newsletters, lead magnets, automation templates, or niche micro-SaaS products.

## Venture Selection Rules

Use the following sequence before activating a new lane:

1. screen with `python/tools/revenue_planning.py` or `instruments/strategy/score.sh`;
2. reject any lane with weak legality, weak consent, weak provenance, or weak platform alignment;
3. prefer lanes that are repeatable, automatable, and easy to validate with small pilots;
4. log the decision in the mission journal.

## Success Criteria

- recurring revenue from at least two compliant lanes;
- documented operating procedures for each active lane;
- measurable automation coverage without increased compliance risk;
- retained customer trust through transparent data handling.
