# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system by deploying autonomous but governed workflows that create value through lawful, ethical, and repeatable online ventures.

## Success definition

- revenue comes from durable, consent-compatible offerings
- the system can prioritize, execute, learn, and improve with limited supervision
- every data workflow has clear ownership, provenance, and lawful basis
- risky ideas are filtered out before execution

## Approved venture lanes

### 1. first-party service automation

Offer automation, analysis, and operations support to businesses using their own approved assets and customer data.

Examples:
- CRM hygiene
- support inbox triage
- listing preparation
- survey processing

### 2. digital products and knowledge assets

Package repeatable expertise into:

- playbooks
- prompt packs
- templates
- premium reports
- niche software utilities

### 3. opt-in audience building

Create newsletters, lead magnets, and research products where subscribers knowingly provide their information.

### 4. marketplace and commerce workflows

Use seller-provided product information to improve listings, merchandising, and fulfillment coordination.

## Explicitly rejected lane

Contact-list brokerage, inbox scraping, or resale of personal email addresses is out of bounds.

## Operating loop

1. capture opportunities in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`
2. run `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
3. if the idea touches monetization, outreach, inboxes, or contacts, run `revenue_planning`
4. ship only PASS items or HOLD items after controls are satisfied
5. record wins, failures, and reusable assets in docs and memory
