# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission
Build a self-sustaining, compliant revenue system using Agent Zero to research, validate, launch, and improve online ventures that can operate with minimal human supervision.

The system must stay inside four non-negotiable limits:
- legal in the jurisdictions where it operates
- consistent with platform terms and anti-spam rules
- respectful of privacy, consent, and data minimization
- reviewable by a human through logs, memory, and documented policies

## Explicitly Rejected Approaches
- Compiling, buying, scraping, enriching, brokering, or selling personal email lists.
- Using Gmail or other inbox data to extract third-party contacts for resale or cold outreach without clear consent.
- Sending bulk unsolicited outreach from harvested inbox data.
- Any monetization path that depends on deceptive identity, evasion, or prohibited automation.

## Approved Revenue Lanes
1. Inbox-to-CRM automation for the user's own business
   - Classify inbound leads from first-party email.
   - Extract structured opportunities from opt-in messages.
   - Draft follow-ups for human approval or policy-constrained sending.

2. Productized services
   - Listing enhancement and cross-posting.
   - Research reports and market intelligence.
   - Workflow automation packages for small businesses.

3. Digital products
   - Templates, playbooks, prompt packs, and niche knowledge bases.
   - Verticalized micro-tools built on top of Agent Zero.

4. Content and audience
   - SEO content generation from approved sources.
   - Educational newsletters for opted-in subscribers.
   - Lead magnets that collect consented first-party contacts.

5. Client-owned outreach systems
   - Only where contact provenance is documented.
   - Only where consent basis and platform rules are clear.
   - Only with audit logs, throttling, and unsubscribe handling.

## Gmail and RAG Scope
Gmail-derived data may be used only for the account owner's first-party operations, such as:
- summarizing inbound conversations
- extracting tasks and opportunities from existing customer or partner threads
- building an internal knowledge base from the owner's own correspondence
- drafting compliant responses for review

Gmail-derived data may not be used to:
- build brokerable contact lists
- identify third-party contacts for sale
- bypass consent requirements
- repurpose inbox data beyond the original relationship context

## Operating Sequence
1. Intake opportunities into `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Score each opportunity with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
3. Reject ideas that fail legality, consent, provenance, or terms-of-service checks.
4. For surviving ideas, create a scoped experiment with a KPI, risk note, and rollback plan.
5. Record results in the mission journal and promote durable learnings into memory or knowledge.

## Initial Portfolio
### Priority lane
- Inbox-to-CRM assistant for first-party, opted-in inquiries.

### Hedge lanes
- Autonomous listing support service.
- Niche research product generation.
- Productized internal automation kits.

## Minimum Success Standard
No revenue lane may be activated until it has:
- a documented customer value proposition
- a legal and consent basis
- a data provenance explanation
- a platform-policy review
- a measurable KPI and a rollback path
