# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Purpose

Create a self-sustaining financial system built from legal, ethical, automation-friendly online ventures. The system should maximize repeatable cash flow while minimizing dependence on manual intervention.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- no personal-email harvesting or list brokerage,
- no spam or non-consensual outreach,
- no unauthorized access to inboxes, files, or platforms,
- no platform-evasion tactics,
- no monetization lane without clear provenance and consent.

See `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md` for the hard rules.

## Revenue thesis

Focus on high-trust, software-assisted lanes that can compound:

1. authorized inbox-to-CRM assistant
   - use RAG on the owner's mailbox or a client-authorized mailbox,
   - surface inbound opportunities, customer threads, and follow-up tasks,
   - convert opted-in or existing business contacts into a clean CRM,
   - sell the workflow or service, not the underlying contact data.

2. autonomous listing service
   - turn seller notes and images into polished listings,
   - publish and manage listings across approved marketplaces,
   - monetize through subscriptions, usage fees, or managed service retainers.

3. research and intelligence products
   - produce market maps, sector briefs, competitor digests, and niche reports,
   - sell subscriptions, premium reports, templates, or bespoke analysis.

4. first-party audience systems
   - build opt-in lead magnets, newsletters, and qualification funnels,
   - use analytics tools such as Orange on permissioned data for segmentation,
   - monetize via services, software, partnerships, or owned media.

## Phase structure

### Phase 0: compliance and operating system
- establish the compliance pack, intake queue, and scoring instrument,
- reject unsafe lanes early,
- document the portfolio and selection criteria.

### Phase 1: near-term cash flow
- launch one service lane with fast time-to-value,
- prioritize authorized inbox-to-CRM and listing-service workflows,
- keep delivery narrow and measurable.

### Phase 2: automation and productization
- codify successful playbooks into tools, prompts, and instruments,
- reduce manual review points,
- add dashboards, telemetry, and reusable templates.

### Phase 3: portfolio compounding
- reinvest profits into defensible software, datasets with proper rights, and distribution,
- diversify across services, products, and recurring subscriptions,
- keep each lane independently scoreable and auditable.

## Operating metrics

Track each lane on:

- legality and consent status,
- gross margin quality,
- repeatability,
- automation coverage,
- platform dependence,
- payback speed,
- retention and expansion potential.

## Portfolio preference order

Default order of preference when multiple lanes are available:

1. client-authorized workflows with clear pain relief,
2. owned products built from original research or tooling,
3. opt-in distribution systems,
4. only then broader experiments that still clear compliance gates.

## Excluded monetization lane

The following lane is explicitly out of scope and should remain rejected:

- compiling, packaging, brokering, or selling email address lists sourced from Gmail, files, or any other personal-data repository.
