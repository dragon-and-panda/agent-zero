# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining portfolio of online revenue lanes that can be operated with high automation and low supervision while remaining lawful, ethical, and auditable.

## Non-goals

This program will not pursue:

- resale of personal email lists
- scraping private inboxes for monetization
- spam or deceptive outreach
- gray-market data brokerage
- financial schemes that depend on policy, consent, or platform violations

## Core operating principle

Revenue quality beats raw speed. A slower lane with durable consent, strong margins, and automation potential is better than a fast lane that introduces legal or reputational risk.

## Priority lanes

### Lane 1: Inbox-to-CRM assistant for consenting owners

Use RAG on a mailbox only for the owner or an explicitly authorized team. Focus on:

- summarizing inbound leads
- extracting structured opportunities into a CRM
- drafting replies for review
- reporting demand trends

Revenue model:

- monthly retainer for setup and oversight
- usage-based automation support

### Lane 2: Autonomous listing and merchandising services

Build on `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md` and `services/autonomous_listing/` to offer:

- listing intake
- image enhancement
- copy generation
- multi-channel listing support
- pricing and merchandising assistance

Revenue model:

- per-listing fee
- managed service subscription

### Lane 3: Public-data research products

Create reports, monitors, or niche dashboards from public, licensed, or aggregated sources.

Revenue model:

- paid report
- recurring subscription
- consulting upsell

## Activation criteria

A lane may move from idea to pilot only if it:

1. passes the compliance pack in `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`
2. scores PASS or HOLD with an explicit remediation plan in `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
3. has a documented owner, customer, and data provenance story
4. can be tested on synthetic or first-party consented data first

## Initial KPI set

- qualified revenue generated
- gross margin
- automation coverage
- customer retention or repeat usage
- compliance incidents
- operator intervention minutes per delivery cycle

## First execution sequence

1. Score candidate lanes in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`
2. Pilot the Inbox-to-CRM lane with only first-party or client-authorized inbox data
3. Continue the autonomous listing service as a parallel revenue hedge
4. Package one public-data research product for subscription or paid download
