# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission
Build a self-sustaining portfolio of ethical, legal, and automation-friendly online ventures that can generate revenue with minimal supervision while preserving user trust, privacy, and platform compliance.

## Hard Constraints
- No scraping or resale of personal email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal contact data.
- No non-consensual inbox access, account takeover, spam, or deceptive outreach.
- No workflow may activate unless legality, consent, provenance, and platform terms are documented.
- High-risk financial activities must start in simulation and only graduate after objective safety and reserve thresholds pass.

## Operating Thesis
The system should compound by combining multiple compliant lanes instead of depending on one fragile tactic:
1. first-party lead capture and CRM enrichment
2. productized services with clear delivery and pricing
3. research products and internal data assets built from lawful public or licensed sources
4. adjacent automation products that save clients measurable time or cost

## Revenue Lanes

### Lane 1: Inbox-to-CRM for Owned Accounts
- Use only client-owned or user-owned inboxes with explicit authorization.
- Extract structured signals from inbound mail and route them into a CRM or task queue.
- Monetize as a productized service, SaaS feature, or internal ops accelerator.

### Lane 2: Autonomous Listing and Resale Operations
- Use the listing-service blueprint to create compliant marketplace listings from seller-provided assets.
- Monetize through service fees, subscriptions, or managed marketplace operations.

### Lane 3: Research and Intelligence Products
- Build reports, niche databases, and market-monitoring digests from lawful public or licensed sources.
- Monetize through subscriptions, consulting retainers, or one-off intelligence packs.

### Lane 4: Lead Magnets and Consent-Based Demand Generation
- Capture leads through forms, newsletter signup flows, waitlists, free tools, or gated downloads.
- Only contact prospects who have opted in or who are contacted under a documented lawful basis and platform policy.

## Activation Gates
Before a lane becomes active, record:
- customer profile and pain point
- acquisition method
- data provenance
- monetization method
- legal or policy risks
- automation opportunities
- measurable KPIs

Every lane must also be scored using `/workspace/instruments/strategy/score.sh`.

## Initial Priorities
1. Stand up the compliant Inbox-to-CRM lane first because it uses first-party data and has clear B2B monetization.
2. Keep the autonomous listing service as a hedge with service-fee revenue.
3. Build at least one research product from lawful public information to diversify revenue.

## KPIs
- qualified leads captured
- pipeline value influenced
- revenue per lane
- gross margin per lane
- percent of tasks automated
- policy incidents
- customer retention

## Definition of Success
The system is successful when it can repeatedly activate compliant ventures, generate measurable revenue, and maintain a zero-tolerance record for privacy abuse, spam, and unlawful data use.
