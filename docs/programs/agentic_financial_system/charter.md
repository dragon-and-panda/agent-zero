# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission
Build a self-sustaining portfolio of online revenue workflows that are ethical,
legal, low-touch, and increasingly automatable inside Agent Zero.

## Operating constraints

- revenue must come from lawful products or services
- personal-data use must be consent-based, first-party, or public and lawful
- the system must reject personal email list brokerage and spam workflows
- every lane must have explicit provenance, platform-fit, and rollback notes
- autonomy should reduce repetitive work, not hide risky behavior

## Initial portfolio lanes

### Lane 1: Inbox-to-CRM assistant
Use RAG on a user-owned or client-authorized mailbox to summarize threads,
extract commercial intent, draft follow-ups, and sync approved records into a
CRM. This is the closest compliant substitute for the original email-data idea.

Potential functions:
- summarize inbound leads and partner requests
- classify intent, urgency, and next action
- extract company and contact details only from authorized conversations
- prepare CRM-ready notes and task queues
- use Orange DataScaping or similar tools only for lawful first-party analysis

### Lane 2: Public-source research products
Build research briefs, benchmarks, curated directories, and market maps using
public information with documented provenance.

Potential functions:
- competitor monitoring
- local or niche market directories
- partner landscape reports
- trend digests and benchmark packs

### Lane 3: Listing and marketplace operations
Use the existing Agent Zero autonomy patterns to manage client-owned listings,
marketplace content, and operational workflows tied to legitimate inventory.

## Decision standard
A lane can only move from idea to execution when it satisfies:

1. `PASS` or justified `HOLD` review from `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
2. alignment with `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`
3. a concrete buyer, offer, or internal efficiency target
4. a journal entry with next actions and stop conditions

## Anti-goals

The system must not optimize for:
- selling or renting personal contact databases
- inbox scraping without authorization
- mass unsolicited outreach
- brittle revenue dependent on terms-of-service violations
- hidden risk transfer to users or clients
