# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, low-touch revenue system using lawful, ethical, and repeatable online ventures that Agent Zero can help operate.

## Operating principle

Autonomy is only useful if it compounds trust. The system must prefer durable, consent-based revenue over fast but risky schemes.

## Approved venture lanes

### Lane 1: Inbox-to-CRM operations

- Customer-owned mailbox cleanup
- Consent-safe contact extraction
- CRM deduplication and segmentation
- Support and follow-up workflow automation

This lane is allowed only for first-party or client-authorized data and internal business use.

### Lane 2: Autonomous listing services

- Turn owned inventory into polished listings
- Price against market comps
- Publish and maintain listings across compliant marketplaces
- Assist with inquiry handling inside agreed boundaries

Reference: `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md`

### Lane 3: Research and information products

- Curated market maps
- Competitive intelligence packs
- Niche datasets built from public or licensed sources
- Playbooks, templates, and software sold as products or services

## Rejected lane

- compiling, brokering, or selling personal email lists
- scraping inboxes or files to create contact inventories for resale
- bulk outreach systems that depend on unclear consent

## Scoring rule

Every proposed lane must pass:

1. hard compliance gates
2. business-attractiveness scoring
3. a documented next-step plan with measurable outputs

Use `instruments/strategy/score.sh` and the `revenue_planning` tool before activation.

## Baseline KPIs

- number of compliant revenue lanes active
- validated gross margin per lane
- percent of workflows that remain fully automated after setup
- count of compliance exceptions or manual escalations
- retained knowledge assets created per cycle

## Initial priority order

1. Inbox-to-CRM operations for authorized first-party data
2. Autonomous listing service experiments
3. Research-product packaging

## Kill criteria

Pause or terminate any lane that:

- fails legality, consent, provenance, or platform-compliance review
- requires ongoing manual rescue to stay functional
- depends on selling or transferring personal data
- produces unclear unit economics after repeated trials
