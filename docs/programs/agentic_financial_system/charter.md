# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, low-touch revenue system on top of Agent Zero by pursuing lawful, ethical, and automatable online ventures.

## Program Constraints

- Revenue must come from legitimate products or services.
- Personal data may be used only with clear ownership, authorization, and purpose limitation.
- The system must reject email-list resale, unauthorized inbox extraction, and data brokerage.
- Automation should increase leverage, not bypass legal or platform controls.

## Phase 1 Objective

Launch a compliance-first revenue lane that can operate with minimal supervision and clear operating procedures.

## Initial Opportunity Lanes

### Lane A: Authorized Inbox-to-CRM
- Input: operator-owned or client-authorized mailbox exports, CRM files, and support threads
- Output: deduplicated relationship intelligence, CRM hygiene, follow-up queues, and account summaries
- Monetization: subscription, setup fee, or managed service

### Lane B: Autonomous Listing Service
- Input: seller-owned inventory, media, and notes
- Output: better listings, channel ops, and buyer-response workflows
- Monetization: per-listing fee, rev share, or monthly service

### Lane C: Research and Automation Products
- Input: public or authorized operational data
- Output: niche reports, internal dashboards, workflow agents, and SOP automation
- Monetization: recurring software or productized consulting

## Non-Goals

- selling compiled email lists
- harvesting addresses from inboxes or files for third-party resale
- using gray-market scraping as the primary business model
- running outreach that cannot document lawful basis and consent

## Operating Loop

1. Intake idea into `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Run `python/tools/revenue_planning.py` logic through the agent.
3. Score the lane with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
4. Only build experiments that clear compliance and attractiveness gates.
5. Log progress in `journal.md` and reprioritize via `improvements.md`.

## Success Criteria

- At least one lane reaches `PASS` under the compliance and strategy screen.
- The lane can be delivered with repeatable SOPs and bounded risk.
- The system captures reusable playbooks, prompts, and instruments after each iteration.
