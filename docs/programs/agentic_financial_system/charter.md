# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Objective

Build a self-sustaining financial system around Agent Zero using legal, ethical, repeatable online ventures that can be operated with low-touch human oversight.

## Guardrails

- No personal email list resale.
- No non-consensual inbox access.
- No spam-first growth strategies.
- No deceptive or platform-evasive automation.
- No execution on unclear data provenance.

All revenue ideas must align with `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`.

## Phase 1 Revenue Lanes

1. Client-owned inbox to CRM operations
   - owner-authorized mailbox search and summarization
   - internal contact routing
   - support inbox classification

2. Autonomous listing services
   - listing creation
   - listing enrichment
   - cross-posting
   - optimization for marketplaces and directories

3. Research products
   - vertical market maps
   - company intelligence briefs
   - public-data synthesis

4. Opt-in demand capture
   - landing pages
   - lead magnets
   - newsletters
   - waitlists

5. Commissioned automation for SMBs
   - reporting
   - content ops
   - CRM hygiene
   - workflow automation

## Operating Loop

1. Capture candidate lanes in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Screen each lane with the `revenue_planning` tool.
3. Score compliant lanes with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
4. Activate only PASS lanes.
5. Log experiments and outcomes in `journal.md`.
6. Feed lessons into `improvements.md`.

## Core Metrics

- time to first dollar
- gross margin
- repeatability
- automation coverage
- platform durability
- legal and compliance confidence

## Definition of Success

The system is successful when at least one compliant lane produces recurring revenue, requires limited human intervention, and passes the legality, consent, provenance, and platform-risk gates on every run.
