# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-improving financial system that can generate revenue through lawful, ethical, low-touch online operations.

## Rejected lane

The following lane is explicitly out of scope:

- compiling and selling email address lists

It fails privacy, anti-spam, consent, and platform-policy requirements and must not be implemented.

## Approved strategic lanes

1. Consented inbox-to-CRM
   - Convert customer-owned inbox exports and first-party records into structured CRM data.
   - Use only explicit consent signals for outreach or segmentation.

2. Autonomous listing operations
   - Extend the existing autonomous listing service to sell operational throughput, optimization, or reporting.

3. Research subscriptions
   - Package market maps, benchmark reports, and playbooks from lawful public or first-party data.

4. Productized service workflows
   - Build narrow agentic workflows with repeatable delivery and auditable inputs.

## Operating rules

- Every new monetization lane must pass the scoring instrument in `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
- Any workflow touching inboxes, contact data, or outreach must first pass the `revenue_planning` tool.
- Personal-data resale, scraping, and unsolicited outreach remain prohibited even if they appear profitable.

## Initial priority

Prioritize the consented inbox-to-CRM lane first because it aligns with the user's data-processing goal while staying inside legal and ethical boundaries.
