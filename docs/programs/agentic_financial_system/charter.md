# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system around autonomous, legally compliant, ethically operated online ventures that compound over time.

## Non-goals

This program does not pursue:

- sale of personal email lists or contact databases;
- unauthorized access to Gmail or other private inboxes;
- monetization strategies based on spam, deception, or policy evasion.

## Approved operating thesis

The system should favor durable, consent-based revenue lanes:

1. client-authorized inbox-to-CRM automation;
2. autonomous listing and directory services built from lawful sources;
3. research products and paid market intelligence;
4. internal agentic tooling sold as a service;
5. opt-in lead generation and conversion systems.

## Program principles

- compliance before speed;
- repeatability before novelty;
- first-party or client-authorized data before third-party data;
- services and products before data brokerage;
- documented scoring before activation.

## Phase 1 focus

Prioritize the inbox-to-CRM lane for consenting clients and keep adjacent lanes active as hedges:

- autonomous listing service;
- niche research subscription;
- workflow automation packs for small businesses.

## Standard activation flow

1. Capture the idea in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Run `instruments/strategy/score.sh` against the opportunity.
3. Screen the idea in-band with the `revenue_planning` tool.
4. Reject any lane with weak legality, weak consent, unclear provenance, or ToS conflicts.
5. Promote only PASS lanes into execution.

## Success measures

- recurring revenue from compliant products or services;
- percentage of lanes with documented consent and provenance;
- time from scored idea to first customer outcome;
- margin and operator time saved through automation;
- zero dependence on personal-data resale.
