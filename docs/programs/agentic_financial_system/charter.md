# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system through legal, ethical, high-leverage online ventures that can be operated autonomously with minimal supervision.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- No harvesting, brokering, or selling personal email/contact lists.
- No scraping inboxes for monetizable lead data.
- No spam, consent bypass, or platform-rule evasion.
- No workflow that depends on ambiguous authorization or unclear data provenance.

## Approved operating model

The system should focus on revenue lanes such as:

1. automation services
2. software subscriptions
3. paid research or intelligence products
4. opt-in newsletters and audience products
5. client-authorized CRM cleanup, segmentation, and enrichment
6. templates, playbooks, or digital products

## Data policy for this program

- Gmail and other email RAG may support owner-authorized search, summarization, and workflow assistance.
- Inbox-derived contact data may not be converted into a resale asset.
- Any contact-oriented workflow must preserve provenance, consent status, and unsubscribe controls.

## Program loop

1. Intake ideas in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.
2. Score them with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
3. Reject anything that fails legality, consent, provenance, or platform-risk gates.
4. Convert approved items into small experiments with measurable outcomes.
5. Log learnings in `journal.md`.
6. Feed durable improvements into `improvements.md`.

## First experiments to prefer

- Opt-in lead magnet plus newsletter funnel
- B2B automation audit offer
- Client-authorized inbox productivity assistant
- Niche research report subscription
- Workflow templates sold to a defined professional segment
