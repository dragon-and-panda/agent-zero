# Agentic Financial System Charter

## 1. Objective

Build a self-sustaining portfolio of online revenue workflows that can operate with high autonomy while remaining ethical, legal, and measurable.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Rules

- Follow `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md` for every opportunity.
- Do not build or monetize scraped or purchased personal email lists.
- Use Gmail or other inbox data only for the account owner or an explicitly authorized client.
- Prefer business models that monetize software, services, insights, audience trust, or operational efficiency rather than personal-data resale.

## 3. Initial Revenue Lanes

### Lane A: Inbox-to-CRM Concierge

Purpose:
- turn a founder's or client's own inbox into structured tasks, contacts, follow-ups, and CRM drafts

Allowed inputs:
- Gmail or mailbox data from the account owner
- consented CRM exports
- internal notes, proposals, and customer threads

Outputs:
- deduplicated contact records
- follow-up queues
- opportunity summaries
- support and sales drafts
- segmented first-party lists for newsletters or lifecycle messaging

Why this lane matters:
- it creates immediate service value without needing external acquisition
- it uses the user's own data rather than third-party lists
- it can evolve into recurring software or managed operations

### Lane B: Autonomous Listing and Commerce Operations

Purpose:
- sell operational automation for compliant marketplace listing, response handling, and closeout workflows

Primary reference:
- `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md`

Why this lane matters:
- it monetizes automation labor directly
- it avoids personal-data brokerage
- it can generate repeatable revenue from client workflows

### Lane C: Research and Audience Products

Purpose:
- produce reports, benchmarks, playbooks, newsletters, and sponsorship inventory using public, licensed, or anonymized data

Why this lane matters:
- it compounds knowledge into sellable assets
- it supports affiliate, sponsorship, and subscription revenue
- it can feed other lanes with market intelligence

## 4. Explicitly Rejected Lane

Rejected:
- compiling email address lists from inboxes or files and selling them to online services

Reason:
- weak consent, weak provenance, high privacy risk, and likely terms or legal violations

Replacement:
- build opt-in list growth systems and monetize the owned audience or the workflow itself

## 5. Phase Structure

### Phase 1: Services First
- launch Inbox-to-CRM concierge offers for owner-authorized inboxes
- use Orange to clean, cluster, and score first-party records only
- offer CRM hygiene, pipeline organization, and newsletter segmentation as paid services

### Phase 2: Productize Repeatable Work
- convert repeated service steps into instruments and reusable prompts
- add dashboards, reporting, and standard operating procedures
- package the most valuable workflow as a subscription product

### Phase 3: Compound with Audience and Research Assets
- publish opt-in content funnels
- create benchmark reports and sponsorship inventory
- use aggregate analytics to improve pricing, messaging, and retention

## 6. Operating Checklist

Before activating any lane:
1. record the opportunity in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`
2. score it with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
3. reject it if any hard gate is weak
4. log the chosen next step in `docs/programs/agentic_financial_system/journal.md`
5. add follow-up improvements to `docs/programs/agentic_financial_system/improvements.md`

## 7. Success Criteria

The program is on track when it:
- produces revenue from compliant automation or information products
- improves with low-touch operation and reusable instruments
- keeps data provenance and consent auditable
- avoids dependence on any workflow that resells personal contact data
