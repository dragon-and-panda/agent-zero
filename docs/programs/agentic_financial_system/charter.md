# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, low-touch financial system through ethical, legal online ventures that Agent Zero can operate with increasing autonomy.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- no personal email list brokerage
- no inbox access without authorization
- no spam or unconsented outreach
- no revenue lane whose main edge comes from violating privacy, law, or platform rules

## Approved revenue lanes

### 1. Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene Service

Use retrieval, classification, and structured extraction on a client-owned mailbox to:

- identify business-relevant contacts,
- normalize company and contact records,
- capture consent and relationship status,
- prepare approved records for CRM sync,
- generate summaries and follow-up tasks for the client.

This is the compliant replacement for list-resale ideas. Value comes from workflow cleanup and insight generation, not from selling identity data.

### 2. Autonomous Listing Service

Turn seller-provided inventory, photos, and notes into listings, syndication flows, and guarded buyer-response automation.

Reference: `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md`

### 3. Research and Insight Products

Create sellable reports, benchmarks, and playbooks using public sources plus customer-approved first-party data. Favor anonymized or aggregated outputs.

### 4. Productized Agent Operations

Package repeatable service workflows into tools, templates, or subscriptions for customers who want compliant automation in their own stack.

## Operating loop

1. intake the idea in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`
2. score it with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
3. reject, hold, or activate the lane
4. log work in the mission journal
5. promote reusable playbooks into repo docs, prompts, and instruments

## Success metrics

- share of revenue from recurring or retainer work
- percent of workflows with documented authorization
- time from intake to first customer value
- automation rate without compliance incidents
- gross margin per lane

## Immediate focus

Prioritize the Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene Service first, keep the Autonomous Listing Service active as a hedge, and treat research products as the next productization path.
