# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission
Build a self-sustaining, legally compliant, and ethically grounded revenue system on top of Agent Zero. The system should discover, evaluate, launch, and improve digital ventures that can operate with increasing autonomy while staying inside privacy law, platform terms, and explicit user-consent boundaries.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints
- No sale, rental, or brokerage of personal contact lists.
- No scraping or extracting inbox data, CRM data, or account data without explicit authorization and a documented lawful basis.
- No spam, cold outreach automation without consent review, or evasion of anti-abuse controls.
- No deceptive, fraudulent, or copyright-violating monetization.
- No products that depend on account takeover, impersonation, credential reuse, or shadow access.

## Approved Revenue Lanes
1. Consent-based lead magnets and newsletters
   - Build opt-in funnels, landing pages, and segmented content.
   - Use double opt-in where possible.
2. Productized AI services
   - Research briefs, competitive intelligence, workflow automation, documentation, support tooling.
3. Digital products
   - Templates, prompt packs, playbooks, dashboards, micro-SaaS utilities, premium reports.
4. Marketplace and affiliate programs
   - Only when disclosures, attribution, and platform terms are satisfied.
5. B2B automation with first-party data
   - Customer support, internal copilots, and document processing for organizations that own the data and authorize its use.

## Phase 1 Deliverables
- Intake queue for venture ideas and monetization experiments.
- Compliance scoring instrument that can reject unsafe or non-compliant plans.
- Revenue planning tool and prompt guidance for the core agent.
- Journal and improvement loop to compound learning from experiments.

## Data Handling Rules
- Treat inboxes, contact lists, and exported datasets as sensitive by default.
- Require documented provenance for every dataset used in a venture.
- Prefer first-party or explicitly licensed data.
- Store only the minimum data necessary for the workflow.
- Record retention and deletion expectations for each venture.

## Evaluation Rubric
Every opportunity should be scored on:
- legality and policy fit
- consent and user trust
- revenue potential
- time-to-value
- operational complexity
- repeatability
- defensibility

## Target Operating Loop
1. Capture an idea in the intake queue.
2. Run the compliance and opportunity score.
3. Reject unsafe ideas immediately.
4. Convert approved ideas into a short experiment brief.
5. Build the smallest shippable asset.
6. Measure conversion, retention, margin, and operational load.
7. Promote successful experiments into standard operating procedures.
