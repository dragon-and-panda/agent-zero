# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system through autonomous online ventures that are durable, ethical, legal, and minimally supervision-heavy.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- No personal-data brokerage.
- No selling email lists or contact lists.
- No spam or unsolicited bulk outreach to scraped or purchased contacts.
- No unauthorized access to Gmail, Google Workspace, drives, or uploaded archives.
- No deceptive, fraudulent, or platform-abusive monetization.

## Operating principles

1. Compliance first.
2. First-party data over third-party data.
3. Original value creation over arbitrage based on privacy abuse.
4. Repeatable systems over one-off hacks.
5. Measured experiments over vague ambition.

## Safe uses of email RAG

If the operator has authority over an inbox, retrieval and analysis may support:

- support and operations summaries,
- invoice and renewal tracking,
- inbound lead triage,
- recurring pain-point discovery,
- FAQ and knowledge-base extraction,
- partner or customer follow-up queues.

Email RAG may not be used to create saleable contact databases.

## Phase 1 revenue lanes

### Lane A: service and fulfillment automation

- package recurring services,
- automate proposal generation and follow-up,
- track delivery, invoicing, and renewals.

### Lane B: marketplace commerce

- list owned or authorized inventory,
- improve merchandising copy and assets,
- monitor pricing, fulfillment, and margin.

### Lane C: digital products and content

- produce templates, guides, datasets derived from original or licensed work,
- run affiliate content with proper disclosures,
- sell research summaries or operating playbooks.

### Lane D: compliant demand capture

- create opt-in lead magnets,
- run waitlists and newsletters,
- qualify inbound interest from owned channels.

## Immediate anti-patterns to reject

- "Extract email addresses from Gmail and files, then sell the list."
- "Harvest inbox contacts and rent them to online services."
- "Use private messages as a source of leads for unsolicited bulk outreach."

## Initial scorecard

Track:

- recurring revenue,
- gross margin,
- cash conversion speed,
- automation coverage,
- compliance incidents,
- platform-risk incidents,
- percent of revenue sourced from first-party or clearly authorized channels.
