# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Purpose

Build a low-touch, self-improving revenue system on top of Agent Zero that can discover, validate, and operate profitable online ventures while remaining legal, ethical, and privacy-preserving.

---

## Mission Statement

Create durable revenue engines that:
- use automation to reduce manual work,
- rely on first-party or lawfully sourced data,
- can be audited and improved over time,
- prioritize repeatable services, software, and research products over one-off arbitrage.

---

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

This program must follow `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`.

In particular, it will **not**:
- harvest personal emails,
- broker or sell contact lists,
- use inbox data for third-party resale,
- run deceptive or unlawful outreach programs.

---

## Initial Revenue Lanes

### Lane A: Inbox-to-CRM Assistant
- Input: owner-authorized email inboxes
- Output: structured leads, customer records, next-step tasks, summaries
- Monetization: service fee, subscription, or managed automation retainer
- Why first: high utility, directly aligned with Agent Zero's memory/RAG/tooling model

### Lane B: Autonomous Listing Service
- Input: seller-owned product photos, metadata, and marketplace accounts
- Output: enhanced listings, syndication, reply assistance, analytics
- Monetization: listing fee, subscription, or transaction-based revenue share

### Lane C: Research Product Studio
- Input: lawful public market data, operator notes, customer interviews, internal learnings
- Output: reports, curated databases, benchmark packs, operating playbooks
- Monetization: one-off report sales, subscriptions, consulting upsells

---

## Lane Selection Criteria

New lanes should score well on:
- legality and consent,
- access to owned or licensed data,
- time to first revenue,
- repeatability,
- margin potential,
- autonomy fit,
- defensibility.

The scoring workflow is documented in `instruments/strategy/score.md`.

---

## Success Conditions

The system is succeeding when it:
- converts intents into scored opportunities,
- rejects unsafe monetization paths early,
- ships at least one narrow paid workflow with measurable customer value,
- maintains a reusable diary of decisions, metrics, and improvements,
- compounds knowledge rather than restarting from scratch each cycle.

---

## Phase Order

### Phase 0: Guardrails and Intake
- Define compliance pack
- Capture inbound ideas
- Score candidate lanes

### Phase 1: First Revenue Workflow
- Choose one compliant lane
- Build the narrowest sellable version
- Track basic unit economics and operator effort

### Phase 2: Productization
- Standardize delivery
- Add telemetry, dashboards, and QA
- Convert bespoke work into templates or software

### Phase 3: Portfolio Expansion
- Add a second lane only after the first has stable demand and clear operating procedures

---

## Immediate Priority

Prioritize the **Inbox-to-CRM Assistant** as the first lane because it converts authorized first-party data into direct workflow value without relying on privacy-invasive resale models.
