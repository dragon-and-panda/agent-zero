# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining portfolio of online ventures using Agent Zero while staying legal, privacy-preserving, and operationally durable.

## Operating objective

The system should prioritize recurring revenue, low marginal cost, and workflows that can improve through memory, instruments, and repeated evaluation.

## Approved revenue lanes

1. Productized automation services
2. Opt-in newsletters and audience businesses
3. Affiliate or referral content built from published sources
4. Small software products, internal tools, and workflow packs
5. Marketplace and brokerage models where all participants knowingly opt in

## Restricted or rejected lanes

- Selling compiled email lists
- Cold outreach at scale from scraped contacts
- Accessing third-party Gmail or mailbox data without explicit owner authorization
- Monetization strategies that depend on deception, impersonation, or platform abuse

## Data policy

- First-party data and owner-uploaded files are preferred.
- Public data must still respect site terms and collection limits.
- Personal data requires authorization, narrow use, and clear retention boundaries.

## Initial system components

- Strategy intake queue in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`
- Compliance rules in `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`
- Opportunity scoring instrument in `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
- Planning tool in `python/tools/revenue_planning.py`

## Phase 1 focus

1. Use owner-authorized knowledge ingestion for internal RAG and business intelligence.
2. Build one opt-in acquisition loop.
3. Launch one monetizable offer that does not rely on paid ads or privacy abuse.
4. Score every new idea before execution.
