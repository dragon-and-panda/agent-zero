# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission
Build a self-sustaining financial system through lawful, ethical, low-touch online ventures that rely on consent, clear value creation, and auditable operating rules.

## Explicit non-goals
- No scraping or resale of personal email addresses or contact lists.
- No spam, credential abuse, inbox hijacking, or non-consensual access to Gmail or other private systems.
- No deceptive arbitrage, platform evasion, or business models that depend on terms-of-service violations.

## Phase 1 portfolio lanes
1. **Autonomous listing service**
   - Turn seller-owned product data into polished listings and managed marketplace operations.
   - Revenue model: listing fees, managed service retainers, success fees where permitted.
2. **Inbox-to-CRM assistant for first-party customers**
   - Help a business organize its own support or sales inbox with explicit customer authorization.
   - Revenue model: setup fee, subscription, per-seat support.
3. **Research and workflow products**
   - Produce lead-scoring templates, market maps, pricing research, or SOP packs using lawful public/company-owned data.
   - Revenue model: digital products, subscriptions, consulting retainers.

## Operating rules
- Score every new lane with `instruments/strategy/score.sh` or the `revenue_planning` tool before activation.
- Require documented legality, consent path, data provenance, and platform-rule compatibility before execution.
- Favor first-party and opt-in data sources over any third-party personal data.
- Keep an experiment diary and improvement backlog for each active lane.

## Success metrics
- Positive cash contribution by lane.
- Repeatable delivery with limited human intervention.
- No privacy, safety, legal, or platform enforcement incidents.
- Clear audit trail: why the lane was allowed, what data it used, and what constraints govern it.
