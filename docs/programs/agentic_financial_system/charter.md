# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system through ethical, legal, and automatable online ventures that can be operated by Agent Zero with minimal supervision.

## What this program is optimizing for

- recurring or repeatable revenue
- high automation fit
- durable customer value
- low compliance and platform risk
- transparent auditability

## Explicitly rejected approaches

The program will not use:

- harvested or brokered personal email lists
- non-consensual inbox mining
- personal-data resale
- spam-driven acquisition
- deceptive automation or terms-of-service evasion

## Initial venture lanes

### Lane 1: Inbox-to-CRM assistant
- Input: user-owned or client-authorized mailbox data
- Output: tagged conversations, extracted invoices, CRM updates, follow-up drafts for existing relationships or opted-in leads
- Monetization: internal efficiency gains or service fees for implementation and operation

### Lane 2: Autonomous listing concierge
- Input: seller-authorized product photos, notes, and marketplace targets
- Output: higher-quality listings, better pricing support, and compliant marketplace operations
- Monetization: subscription, per-listing fee, or success-based service fee
- Reference: `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md`

### Lane 3: Public-data research products
- Input: public or licensed market data
- Output: benchmark reports, monitoring dashboards, and niche research subscriptions
- Monetization: subscriptions, sponsorships, and premium reports

## Activation criteria

A lane can move from idea to pilot only if:

1. hard gates pass on legality, consent, provenance, and platform risk
2. there is a clear customer and delivery path
3. at least three execution factors are strong: time to cash, margin, repeatability, automation fit, defensibility

## KPI set

- number of compliant lanes under active evaluation
- number of pilots launched
- time from idea to first dollar
- gross margin by lane
- percentage of steps automated
- number of compliance holds or rejects
