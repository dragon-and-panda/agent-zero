# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, ethical revenue system on top of Agent Zero that can
discover, score, prototype, and operate lawful online ventures with minimal
human supervision.

## Explicit non-goals

- No scraping or resale of personal email lists or other personal contact data.
- No non-consensual inbox mining, account takeover, or private-message
  extraction.
- No spam operations, lead laundering, or platform-rule evasion.
- No ventures that depend on unclear ownership, deceptive claims, or unlawful
  data acquisition.

## Revenue lanes

### Lane 1: Client-owned inbox to CRM operations
- User-owned or client-owned mailbox workflows only.
- Use retrieval and automation to summarize inbound demand, label messages,
  extract business entities, and route contacts into consent-aware CRM systems.
- Revenue model: service retainers, setup fees, or internal productivity gains.

### Lane 2: Autonomous listing and marketplace operations
- Reuse the marketplace blueprint in `/workspace/docs/autonomous_listing_service.md`.
- Focus on lawful listing creation, media enhancement, pricing support, and
  response triage for assets the operator is authorized to manage.
- Revenue model: managed service fees and software subscriptions.

### Lane 3: Research and decision products
- Produce market maps, pricing intelligence, sourcing briefs, or workflow
  playbooks from public or licensed data.
- Revenue model: subscriptions, one-off reports, and advisory packages.

### Lane 4: Compliance-first agent infrastructure
- Package the scoring, policy, and oversight systems as reusable internal tools
  or sellable consulting assets.
- Revenue model: templates, integrations, and implementation services.

## Operating rules

1. Every proposed lane must be screened against the compliance pack before work
   starts.
2. Every lane must have clear data provenance, a consent basis, and an allowed
   use under platform terms.
3. Any lane involving regulated financial activity must begin in simulation and
   use reserve, drawdown, and exposure caps before any live deployment.
4. High-risk proposals default to HOLD or REJECT until the uncertainty is
   resolved with evidence.

## Success criteria

- The system can rank opportunities by legality, consent, margin,
  repeatability, defensibility, and automation fit.
- At least one lane is operable end-to-end without relying on prohibited data
  sources.
- Mission diaries and scoring logs make every important decision auditable.
