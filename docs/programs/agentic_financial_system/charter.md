# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining, compliance-first financial system using Agent Zero to create, operate, and improve online ventures that produce recurring revenue with minimal human intervention.

The program must optimize for:

- lawful revenue
- durable margins
- repeatable workflows
- strong data provenance
- low operational overhead
- honest customer relationships

It must not rely on personal-data resale, spam, or hidden acquisition tactics.

---

## Program Thesis

Agent Zero is strongest when it acts as an operator for workflow-heavy businesses that benefit from:

- continuous research
- structured decision-making
- SOP generation
- multi-agent delegation
- tool and instrument creation
- persistent memory and knowledge reuse

That makes the best early monetization paths service-backed automation and productized intelligence, not gray-market data brokerage.

---

## Approved Revenue Lanes

### Lane 1: Inbox-to-CRM Operations for Consenting Owners

Use RAG over an authorized mailbox to:

- classify inbound messages
- extract account, order, and support context
- draft replies
- update a CRM or task queue
- surface recurring pain points and upsell opportunities

Customer profile:

- solo operators
- agencies
- local businesses
- service businesses with overloaded inboxes

Revenue model:

- setup fee
- monthly retainer
- usage-based premium automation tier

### Lane 2: Autonomous Listing Service

Use the adjacent blueprint in `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md` to build an AI-assisted listing operation that improves product presentation, syndication, and buyer response handling.

Revenue model:

- per-listing fee
- subscription for active sellers
- rev-share or premium service tier where appropriate

### Lane 3: Productized Research and Monitoring

Create subscription-grade outputs such as:

- competitor monitoring
- market maps
- pricing intelligence
- policy and compliance digests
- niche watchlists

Revenue model:

- recurring subscriptions
- custom reports
- enterprise monitoring retainers

### Lane 4: Agent Implementation Services

Package Agent Zero into repeatable client automation offerings:

- research copilots
- internal SOP copilots
- inbox and support copilots
- listing or marketplace copilots

Revenue model:

- implementation fee
- monthly maintenance
- premium support

---

## Lane Selection Criteria

A lane should be preferred when it has:

- clean legality and consent posture
- near-term path to paying customers
- high repeatability
- good automation leverage
- defendable know-how or proprietary workflow design
- measurable value to the buyer

High revenue potential alone is insufficient if the lane has weak consent, weak provenance, or fragile platform dependence.

## Operating Model

### Phase A: Intake

Add candidate opportunities to `docs/strategy/incoming.md`.

### Phase B: Gate

Run the opportunity through `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.

### Phase C: Design

If the result is `PASS` or `HOLD`, define:

- target customer
- pain point
- offer structure
- workflow map
- telemetry
- kill criteria

### Phase D: Launch

Start with service-backed automation before trying to fully productize.

### Phase E: Improve

Log every experiment, win, and failure in `journal.md` and keep cross-cutting work in `improvements.md`.

## Initial Prioritization

1. **Inbox-to-CRM Operations**
   - Best balance of legality, speed, and immediate business value.
2. **Autonomous Listing Service**
   - Strong operational leverage and adjacent blueprint already exists.
3. **Productized Research**
   - Good recurring potential once one vertical has traction.
4. **Broader Agent Implementations**
   - Scale after a narrow offer is proven.

---

## Success Metrics

Track at minimum:

- monthly recurring revenue
- setup revenue
- gross margin
- automation coverage
- hours saved per client
- lead-to-close rate
- churn
- incident count

---

## Hard Rejections

The following are explicitly rejected:

- selling compiled email lists
- scraping personal contacts from inboxes for resale
- non-consensual cold outreach systems
- workflows that require TOS evasion or account abuse

---

## Standing Instruction for Agents

If an opportunity depends on unclear consent, unclear provenance, or likely privacy or anti-spam violations, do not optimize it. Reject it, document why, and redirect effort into a compliant lane that uses opt-in, first-party, or client-authorized data instead.
