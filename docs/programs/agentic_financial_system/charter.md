# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining revenue engine on top of Agent Zero using legal, ethical, low-touch online ventures that can be operated or expanded by autonomous agents with limited human supervision.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

- no personal-data brokerage
- no inbox scraping for resale
- no unsolicited spam systems
- no hidden platform abuse or credential abuse
- no monetization model that depends on unclear consent or questionable data provenance

## Phase 1 Revenue Lanes

1. Productized automation services
   - workflow audits
   - lead-routing or support automation for client-owned systems
   - recurring optimization retainers
2. Agent-native digital products
   - prompt packs
   - industry playbooks
   - automation templates
   - micro-tools and internal dashboards
3. Research and intelligence subscriptions
   - market maps
   - competitor monitoring
   - public-data synthesis for niche operators
4. Owned audience channels
   - opt-in newsletter
   - original content
   - compliant conversion funnels

## Operating Loop

1. Intake new opportunities into `docs/strategy/incoming.md`
2. Score them for legality, consent, provenance, and platform risk
3. Reject unsafe concepts early
4. Turn safe concepts into experiments with clear success metrics
5. Capture what works in reusable prompts, tools, instruments, and knowledge artifacts

## Success Metrics

- recurring revenue by lane
- gross margin by lane
- customer acquisition cost for owned channels
- experiment throughput
- percentage of opportunities rejected or redesigned for compliance reasons

## Data Policy

Only first-party, owner-authorized, and appropriately consented data may be used for contact-centric workflows. Private inboxes may support internal organization or service delivery, not list extraction or resale.
