# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining portfolio of lawful, ethical, and low-touch online revenue programs that compound over time through software, automation, reusable knowledge, and customer value.

## Explicit exclusions

The program does not pursue personal email list brokerage, non-consensual inbox mining, spam operations, credential abuse, or platform-evasion tactics. Any request in those categories is redirected into a compliant alternative.

## Operating principles

1. Prefer first-party, opt-in, or licensed data.
2. Favor recurring or repeatable offers over one-off manual hustle.
3. Ship narrow revenue loops first, then layer automation.
4. Require clear legality, consent, provenance, and platform compatibility before execution.
5. Keep a written trail of decisions, metrics, and next bets.

## Current portfolio thesis

### Lane A: First-party inbox-to-CRM operations assistant
- Customer: owner-operated businesses and solo operators
- Offer: summarize, classify, and route authorized email into CRM, follow-up queues, and revenue reports
- Data policy: only customer-authorized inboxes and files, no resale
- Why it fits: clear value, defensible automation, and a recurring operations wedge

### Lane B: Autonomous listing concierge
- Customer: resellers, local service operators, and small merchants
- Offer: turn raw photos and notes into better listings, pricing guidance, and response workflows
- Data policy: first-party seller content and public marketplace policies only
- Why it fits: concrete ROI and direct overlap with existing repo blueprints

### Lane C: Research and intelligence products
- Customer: founders, operators, and niche professionals
- Offer: curated reports, opportunity maps, templates, or briefings based on public or licensed data
- Data policy: public, licensed, synthetic, or customer-provided material only
- Why it fits: reusable assets, low fulfillment cost, and easy packaging

### Lane D: Owned-audience monetization
- Customer: opted-in subscribers and followers
- Offer: newsletter, sponsorship, affiliate, template packs, or playbooks
- Data policy: opt-in audience only
- Why it fits: compounds distribution instead of renting attention forever

## Opportunity gate

Every proposed lane or experiment must be screened through:

- `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`
- `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
- `docs/strategy/incoming.md`

## Execution loop

1. Capture idea in the intake queue.
2. Score legality, consent, provenance, platform risk, time, margin, repeatability, automation, and defensibility.
3. Reject, hold, or activate the idea.
4. Run a narrow pilot with measurable output.
5. Record outcomes in the journal.
6. Promote successful tactics into reusable tools, prompts, or knowledge artifacts.

## Baseline success criteria

- At least one active revenue lane with a compliant offer definition
- A documented intake and scoring workflow
- Program journal entries for decisions and learnings
- Clear separation between approved first-party data use and prohibited data resale
