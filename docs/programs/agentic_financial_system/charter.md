# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining financial system through ethical, legal, and automatable online ventures that can operate with minimal supervision while preserving privacy, consent, and platform compliance.

## What this program will not do

- harvest private inbox data for resale
- compile or sell personal email lists
- run spam, scraping, or deceptive outreach campaigns
- depend on unclear data ownership or terms-of-service violations

## Core design principles

1. asset-first monetization
   - Build reusable assets such as software, workflows, listings, research, or operating playbooks.

2. consent-based data handling
   - Work only with user-owned, client-owned, or explicitly opt-in data.

3. automation after validation
   - Validate a lane manually or in simulation before scaling it with autonomous agents.

4. evidence over intuition
   - Score each lane for legality, consent, provenance, platform fit, time to cash, margin, repeatability, automation leverage, and defensibility.

5. reversible execution
   - Prefer workflows that can be paused quickly if compliance, quality, or economics degrade.

## Phase 1 revenue lanes

### Lane A: client-owned inbox to CRM operations
- summarize and classify inbound messages from an authorized inbox
- convert opted-in contacts into a client-owned CRM
- draft follow-ups, proposals, and reminders for approval
- monetize as an operations service, not as data resale

### Lane B: autonomous listing and marketplace operations
- improve seller photos, descriptions, pricing, and cross-posting for owner inventory
- manage inquiries and negotiation within pre-set guardrails
- monetize through service fees, subscriptions, or revenue share

### Lane C: research and intelligence products
- package market research, benchmarking, competitive scans, and niche reports
- monetize through subscriptions, custom reports, or consulting retainers

### Lane D: first-party audience growth
- create lead magnets, newsletters, calculators, templates, or educational products
- collect explicit opt-ins and nurture them through compliant channels

## Operating cadence

1. intake ideas in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`
2. score them with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
3. reject any lane that fails legality, consent, provenance, or platform-fit gates
4. activate the highest-scoring compliant lane
5. log outcomes in `journal.md`
6. feed improvements into `improvements.md`

## Success criteria

- revenue depends on created value, not private-data extraction
- each active lane has a documented owner, KPI set, and pause condition
- at least one lane can run autonomously on a repeatable schedule with low operational risk
