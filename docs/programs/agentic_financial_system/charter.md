# Agentic Financial System Charter

## Mission

Build a self-sustaining revenue system that compounds through legitimate software, services, digital products, and consent-based distribution with minimal human supervision.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- Stay inside legal and ethical acquisition paths.
- Do not monetize harvested personal data.
- Do not sell email lists or inbox-derived contact inventories.
- Favor first-party consent, clear provenance, and durable platform relationships.
- Prefer revenue models that improve customer outcomes rather than exploit attention or privacy.

## Initial venture lanes

1. Agent-assisted listing optimization services
2. Automation audits and implementation for small businesses
3. Digital products built from owned expertise and reusable prompts
4. Opt-in newsletters, audience products, and partner offers
5. Internal CRM and inbox organization for the account owner

## Opportunity filter

Every idea must be reviewed against:

- `docs/policies/compliance_pack.md`
- `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
- `docs/strategy/incoming.md`

## Target characteristics

- Recurring or repeatable revenue
- Low marginal delivery cost
- Strong data provenance
- Clear customer value
- Limited legal and platform risk

## Rejected lanes

- Email list brokerage
- Inbox scraping for resale
- Spam-driven lead generation
- Credential sharing, bypass tooling, or anti-platform tactics
- Sensitive-data arbitrage

## Execution posture

Start with narrow, testable offers that can be delivered with the current repo:

- planning and scoring tools
- documentation-backed playbooks
- prompt and workflow improvements
- marketplace and operations assistance

Expand only after a lane proves compliant, useful, and repeatable.
