## Queue

### Candidate 1
- title: finance workflow automation service
- offer: fixed-scope setup for reporting, reconciliation, or internal ops automation
- buyer: small teams with repetitive finance workflows
- owned assets: Python automation capability, internal playbooks, case-study material
- data source: user-provided business materials only
- consent status: required and explicit for any retained customer data
- distribution channel: referrals, content, marketplace listings
- score: pending
- decision: review with `revenue_planning`
- notes: approved direction if data handling stays first-party

### Rejected example
- title: broker harvested email addresses
- offer: compile and sell email lists from inboxes and files
- buyer: list purchasers
- owned assets: none with valid consent
- data source: personal inbox content and scraped contacts
- consent status: absent
- distribution channel: list marketplaces and cold outreach
- score: reject
- decision: rejected
- notes: violates privacy, consent, and anti-spam requirements
