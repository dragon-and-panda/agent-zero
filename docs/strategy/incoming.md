# Incoming Strategy Queue

Use this queue to capture raw ideas, then score them before activation.

## Status legend

- PASS: compliant and operationally attractive
- HOLD: potentially compliant but incomplete, weak, or risky
- REJECT: violates privacy, consent, legality, or platform rules

## Queue

| Idea | Status | Reason | Compliant replacement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compile and sell email address lists from mailbox data or local files | REJECT | Personal-data brokerage, unclear consent, and likely platform and privacy violations | Build first-party inbox-to-CRM automation for authorized accounts only |
| Use mailbox RAG to summarize client conversations and draft follow-ups in the client's CRM | HOLD | Viable if authorization, scope, retention, and CRM workflow are defined | Add explicit authorization, scope limits, and approved outputs |
| Sell research briefs on public market trends and operational bottlenecks in a niche | HOLD | Likely compliant but needs niche choice and distribution model | Define target niche, pricing, and recurring delivery format |
| Provide compliant listing and response automation for marketplaces | HOLD | Depends on platform rules and workflow scope | Start with drafting, triage, and analytics before automated posting |

## Intake checklist

Before moving an item out of HOLD:

1. Confirm who owns the data and who authorized access.
2. Confirm the exact customer and offer.
3. Confirm platform rules and operational constraints.
4. Score the lane with the strategy instrument.
5. Record the verdict and the next action.
