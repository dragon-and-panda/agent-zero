# Strategy Intake

Use this page to classify new monetization ideas before execution.

## Status Meanings

- GO: lawful, consent-based, and worth prototyping now.
- HOLD: potentially useful, but missing evidence, access, or operating detail.
- REJECT: unlawful, privacy-invasive, deceptive, or structurally incompatible with the compliance pack.

## Examples

### GO

- Client-authorized inbox-to-CRM hygiene service.
- Marketplace listing optimization for a customer that owns the inventory.
- Subscription market brief based on public or licensed data.

### HOLD

- New SaaS idea with unclear buyer demand but good compliance posture.
- Affiliate content lane without a distribution plan.
- Any workflow that requires a contract, OAuth scopes, or retention policy that has not yet been defined.

### REJECT

- Personal email list brokerage.
- Scraping or compiling Gmail contacts for sale.
- Bulk outreach based on harvested or purchased personal emails.
- Any lane that depends on bypassing platform terms, access controls, or anti-spam rules.

## Intake Questions

1. What is the customer problem?
2. What data source is required, and who owns it?
3. Is there explicit consent or a lawful contractual basis?
4. What is the smallest compliant version that can earn revenue?
5. Can the lane become repeatable or subscription-like?
6. Which existing repo component or doc does it build on?
