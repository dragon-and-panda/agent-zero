## Active Intake: Agentic Financial System

- **Idea:** Build an autonomous financial system that launches online revenue
  ventures with minimal human supervision.
- **Customer:** Initially the operator, then paying clients or buyers for
  compliant services/products.
- **Problem solved:** Turn scattered automation capability into repeatable,
  governed revenue operations.
- **Data source:** First-party operational data, explicitly opted-in leads,
  public business information, and licensed/compliant datasets only.
- **Why now:** The repository already contains strong multi-agent, RAG, memory,
  and service scaffolding that can be redirected into lawful monetization.
- **Monetization model:** Service retainers, SaaS subscriptions, marketplace
  fees, research subscriptions, digital products.
- **Compliance notes:** Reject any lane based on harvesting or reselling private
  contact data. Email/RAG use is restricted to owner-authorized workflows.
- **Next step:** Score candidate lanes and activate the best compliant pilot.

## Rejected Intake: Contact List Brokerage

- **Status:** Rejected
- **Reason:** Fails the compliance pack on consent, privacy, lawful basis, and

- **Replacement:** Inbox-to-CRM operations assistant for owner-authorized
