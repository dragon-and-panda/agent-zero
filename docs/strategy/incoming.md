## Queue

### Rejected example: inbox-derived email list resale

- idea: compile email addresses from mail data and sell the resulting lists
- required data: inbox contents and personal contact details
- consent/provenance notes: weak and misaligned with resale use
- monetization model: list brokerage
- decision: reject
- reason: fails legality, consent, provenance, and platform-compliance standards

### Candidate lane: authorized inbox-to-CRM extraction

- idea: use RAG on a user-authorized business inbox to extract inbound leads, deal stages, and follow-up actions into a private CRM
- target customer: the mailbox owner or their business
- required data: user-authorized mailbox content
- consent/provenance notes: acceptable when used only for first-party operations
- monetization model: internal revenue ops automation or paid service for consenting clients
- decision: hold pending scoring and implementation details

### Candidate lane: autonomous listing service

- idea: convert seller inventory into polished listings, distribute them across approved marketplaces, and help manage responses
- target customer: individuals or small businesses with inventory to sell
- assets already owned: existing listing-service blueprint in repo docs
- required data: client-provided item data and marketplace credentials
- decision: hold pending prioritization versus inbox-to-CRM

### Candidate lane: productized research subscription

- idea: sell recurring market maps, price intelligence, sourcing leads, or competitor summaries
- target customer: operators who value synthesized insight over raw data dumps
- required data: public, licensed, or client-provided data sources
- decision: hold pending market focus selection
