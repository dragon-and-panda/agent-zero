# Incoming Opportunity Triage

Use this page to classify new money-making ideas before spending implementation time.

## GO
These are good fits for autonomous execution with normal review.

- **Consent-based Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene Service**
  - Client owns the inbox.
  - Data is used to organize the client's own pipeline.
  - Output is automation and operational clarity, not a contact list for resale.

- **Autonomous Listing Service**
  - Seller provides the product data and approves publishing.
  - Revenue comes from helping the seller transact faster.

- **Research brief subscriptions**
  - Built from public, licensed, or customer-supplied sources.
  - Revenue comes from insight delivery, not personal-data brokerage.

## HOLD
These may be viable later but need tighter scope, legal review, or infrastructure first.

- **Orange-based segmentation on customer exports**
  - Hold until data provenance, consent fields, and deletion rules are documented.

- **Automated outreach at scale**
  - Hold unless the list is explicitly opt-in and the legal basis, suppression flow, and unsubscribe handling are all defined.

- **Trading or capital-allocation agents**
  - Hold until paper trading, risk limits, capital segregation, and monitoring exist.

## REJECT
These should not move forward.

- **Selling compiled email lists**
  - Reject because it depends on personal-data resale and likely spam.

- **RAG over someone else's Gmail without explicit authorization**
  - Reject because the access and use are non-consensual.

- **Scraping private groups or gated communities for leads**
  - Reject because it relies on access abuse and poor data provenance.
