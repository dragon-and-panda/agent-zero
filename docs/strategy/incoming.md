# Strategy Intake Queue

Every proposed monetization lane should enter this queue before implementation.

## Current opportunities

| Lane | Status | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harvest Gmail and file-based email addresses for sale | REJECT | Fails consent, provenance, privacy, and platform-risk gates. |
| Authorized inbox-to-CRM assistant for the owner or a client | PASS | Useful, automatable, and lawful when access is explicitly authorized and output stays internal. |
| Orange-assisted segmentation on opted-in contacts | HOLD | Allowed only on permissioned datasets with documented provenance and retention rules. |
| Autonomous listing service | PASS | Strong fit for agentic automation and clear service monetization. |
| Research reports and niche intelligence products | PASS | Original work product with low privacy risk and high defensibility potential. |
| Purchased or scraped cold-email outreach lists | REJECT | High legal and platform risk; replace with first-party opt-in funnel. |

## Intake checklist

Before a lane moves from idea to execution, answer:

1. What is the customer pain and who pays?
2. What data is used and who authorized it?
3. Does the lane depend on personal-data resale, spam, or policy evasion?
4. Can the work be productized or repeated?
5. What is the first narrow deliverable?
