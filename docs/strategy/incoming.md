# Strategy Intake Queue

This file is the intake surface for new missions submitted to the Apex Orchestrator.

## Current Mission

### Mission: Build a self-sustaining, ethical financial system
- Objective: Create an agentic operating model that can discover, validate, and run online revenue workflows with minimal human supervision.
- Constraint: Revenue generation must remain legal, privacy-safe, consent-based, and operationally auditable.
- Explicitly rejected path: harvesting, compiling, or selling personal email lists from Gmail, files, or other data sources.

## Approved Direction

Focus the system on:

1. first-party data intelligence over the operator's own inboxes and business records
2. opt-in lead capture and CRM operations
3. service and product monetization instead of personal-data resale
4. repeatable experiments with measurable acquisition, conversion, retention, and margin metrics

## First Execution Priorities

1. Stand up a compliance-first revenue roadmap.
2. Build RAG over owned email and document data for internal triage and opportunity detection.
3. Launch one consent-based monetization motion:
   - niche service agency
   - digital product
   - subscription workflow tool
   - affiliate/content funnel
4. Instrument the system with KPI tracking and decision logs.
