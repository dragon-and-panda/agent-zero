# Strategy Intake Triage

Use this file to classify new monetization ideas before execution.

## GO Examples

- client-authorized inbox summarization that updates the client's own CRM
- marketplace listing optimization for real inventory
- paid competitive-intelligence brief built from lawful market research
- opt-in newsletter plus digital product funnel

## HOLD Examples

- affiliate content plan with weak differentiation
- research product with unclear buyer persona
- automation-heavy service whose margins are not yet validated

## REJECT Examples

- scrape inboxes or files to compile personal email lists for sale
- buy, rent, or broker contact databases of unclear origin
- mass outreach fueled by harvested contacts
- any scheme that depends on violating platform rules or privacy law

## Review Questions

1. Is the value proposition real without monetizing personal data?
2. Is there explicit consent or clear contract scope for any personal-data use?
3. Can the offer be sold with an honest landing page and an opt-out path?
4. Does the lane survive if list resale and spam are removed?
