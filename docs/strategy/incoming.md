# Strategy Intake Queue

Use this file as the entry point for new revenue ideas before any build work starts.

For each idea, capture:

- customer
- pain point
- offer
- data source
- consent model
- monetization model
- initial score
- decision

---

## Active Candidates

### 1. Inbox-to-CRM automation for owner-authorized businesses
- **Customer:** service businesses and small operators with overloaded inboxes
- **Pain point:** slow response times, lost leads, manual data entry
- **Offer:** RAG-assisted inbox triage, CRM syncing, summaries, and reply drafts
- **Data source:** client-owned mailbox and CRM
- **Consent model:** direct owner authorization; first-party operational data
- **Monetization model:** setup + retainer
- **Initial score:** pending
- **Decision:** active candidate

### 2. Autonomous listing operations
- **Customer:** high-volume resellers and small commerce operators
- **Pain point:** inconsistent listing quality and slow inquiry handling
- **Offer:** image polish, listing generation, cross-posting support, buyer-response assistance
- **Data source:** client-provided product content and marketplace-compliant data
- **Consent model:** client-owned content
- **Monetization model:** per listing + subscription
- **Initial score:** pending
- **Decision:** active candidate

### 3. Productized market intelligence
- **Customer:** niche operators who need recurring market visibility
- **Pain point:** fragmented research and slow competitive monitoring
- **Offer:** recurring competitor, pricing, and policy intelligence packs
- **Data source:** public or licensed sources with compliant use rights
- **Consent model:** no personal-data dependence
- **Monetization model:** subscription or retainer
- **Initial score:** pending
- **Decision:** active candidate

---

## Rejected Candidates

### 1. Compile and sell email lists from Google email data
- **Problem:** depends on extracting personal contact data from email and selling it downstream
- **Failure reason:** fails legality, consent, provenance, and likely platform-policy gates
- **Decision:** rejected permanently
- **Replacement:** build opt-in lead generation or owner-authorized inbox-to-CRM workflows instead
