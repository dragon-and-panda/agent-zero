# Strategy Intake Queue

This queue records candidate monetization lanes and their current disposition.

## Queue

| Idea | Description | Current state | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inbox-to-CRM assistant | Turn user-owned inbound email into structured opportunities, tasks, and follow-ups | Active candidate | Allowed when data stays with the owner and consent is clear |
| Autonomous listing concierge | Enhance and syndicate customer-provided listings and manage inquiries | Active candidate | Strong fit with existing blueprint in `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md` |
| Paid research brief | Produce niche market reports from public or licensed sources | Active candidate | Good hedge against platform dependence |
| Opt-in newsletter funnel | Capture demand through consented forms, gated content, and newsletters | Active candidate | Replaces list resale with first-party audience building |
| Broker extracted Gmail contacts | Compile email addresses from inboxes and sell the list | Rejected | Violates the compliance pack and cannot be pursued |

## Intake rules

- Every idea must be scored before launch.
- Any idea involving personal-data resale, non-consensual outreach, or inbox harvesting is rejected immediately.
- When an unsafe idea appears, replace it with a compliant lane that serves the same business objective.
