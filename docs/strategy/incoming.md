# Strategy Intake Queue

Use this file as the landing zone for new autonomous missions before they are activated.

## Review protocol
- Score every proposed venture with `instruments/strategy/score.sh`.
- Reject lanes that fail legality, consent, data provenance, or platform-rule checks.
- Hold lanes that are compliant but weak on margin, repeatability, or automation potential.
- Promote only lanes that pass the hard gates and demonstrate a plausible path to ethical revenue.

## Current queue

### Approved candidate: inbox-to-CRM assistant
- Summary: help a business owner triage their own inbox, classify incoming leads, and sync opted-in prospects into a CRM.
- Revenue path: service retainer, implementation fee, or software subscription.
- Data policy: only first-party data controlled by the customer, with clear notices and revocation paths.
- Status: prioritize first.

### Approved candidate: autonomous listing concierge
- Summary: transform seller-owned photos and notes into polished marketplace listings and manage buyer replies within guardrails.
- Revenue path: per-listing fee, monthly subscription, or revenue share.
- Data policy: operate on customer-owned listings and marketplace-compliant messaging only.
- Status: active hedge.

### Approved candidate: research-backed niche intelligence brief
- Summary: assemble lawful public-source research into niche market reports or watchlists for paying subscribers.
- Revenue path: subscription, consulting, or sponsored research.
- Data policy: no resale of personal contact data; only publish aggregate or public-domain insights.
- Status: active hedge.

### Rejected example: harvested email-list brokerage
- Summary: compile email addresses from inboxes, files, or scraped sources and sell them.
- Rejection reason: violates privacy expectations, likely breaches platform terms, and creates spam and data-brokerage risk.
