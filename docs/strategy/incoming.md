# Strategy Intake Queue

Use this page to record candidate revenue lanes before execution. Every entry should be screened for legality, consent, provenance, platform compliance, and business quality.

## Queue

### REJECTED: Contact-list brokerage from Gmail or local files

- Idea: compile email addresses from Gmail and other files, then sell the lists to online services.
- Decision: REJECT
- Why:
  - non-consensual personal-data extraction
  - privacy and legal exposure
  - platform-rule conflict
  - weak long-term business quality
- Safe replacement:
  - owner-authorized inbox intelligence for internal CRM use
  - opt-in lead generation
  - research products or seller-authorized listing services

- Decision: HOLD until implemented with explicit auth, provenance, and export rules.
- Notes:
  - outputs stay internal unless owner explicitly approves a transfer
  - Orange Data Mining may be used for internal clustering and analysis of the authorized dataset
