## Current Queue

### 1. Productized automation audit for small online businesses

- target customer: SMB operators using manual back-office workflows
- offer: fixed-scope automation audit plus implementation upsell
- acquisition channel: content, referrals, direct partnerships
- data sources: public website info, customer-provided process documents
- consent basis: customer contract and direct submission
- platform dependencies: website, email, scheduling stack
- expected revenue model: audit fee plus implementation retainer
- status: hold
- notes: needs tighter ICP and pricing

### 2. Inbox intelligence for owned or authorized mailboxes

- target customer: operator team or authorized client inbox owner
- offer: summarize demand, identify recurring requests, extract service opportunities
- acquisition channel: internal ops workflow
- data sources: authorized email inboxes only
- consent basis: account owner authorization
- platform dependencies: email provider API terms
- expected revenue model: internal discovery support, later productization
- status: pass
- notes: output must remain internal and cannot be repackaged as a saleable contact list

### 3. Broker and sell compiled email lists

- target customer: third-party marketers or outbound services
- offer: personal contact data compiled from inboxes and files
- acquisition channel: list marketplaces or direct sale
- data sources: Gmail and other files
- consent basis: none established
- platform dependencies: high abuse risk
- expected revenue model: one-time list sales
- status: reject
- notes: rejected under the compliance pack because it depends on non-consensual personal-data resale
