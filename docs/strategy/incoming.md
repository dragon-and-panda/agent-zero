# Strategy Intake Queue

Use this queue to turn broad ambitions into concrete, scoreable revenue lanes.

## Intake Template

For each candidate lane, capture:

- lane: short name
- customer: who pays
- problem: what painful job gets solved
- offer: product or service being delivered
- data_source: where data comes from
- consent_model: why use of any personal data is permitted
- acquisition_path: how customers are reached
- delivery_path: how value is delivered
- constraints: legal, operational, or platform limits
- status: draft, scored, active, hold, or rejected

---

## Active Candidates

### 1. Inbox to CRM Copilot
- lane: inbox-to-crm-copilot
- customer: small businesses with inbound lead volume in Gmail or Google Workspace
- problem: leads and requests sit in shared inboxes without follow-up discipline
- offer: extract opted-in business inquiries, classify them, and sync them into a CRM with summaries and next actions
- data_source: owner-authorized shared inboxes plus CRM records
- consent_model: first-party business inbox processing for a defined customer-support or sales purpose
- acquisition_path: direct outreach to businesses, partner referrals, or inbound content
- delivery_path: managed service or software subscription
- constraints: no resale of contacts, strict mailbox authorization, audit logs required
- status: active

### 2. Autonomous Listing Service
- lane: autonomous-listing-service
- customer: resellers, consignment operators, and side-hustle sellers
- problem: creating high-quality multi-channel listings is slow and inconsistent
- offer: listing enhancement, price support, marketplace syndication, and response assistance
- data_source: seller-provided photos, notes, and marketplace policy documents
- consent_model: first-party seller content
- acquisition_path: creator content, marketplace communities, direct outreach, or agencies
- delivery_path: per-listing fee or monthly managed service
- constraints: per-platform posting rules, anti-bot risk, manual fallback for blocked flows
- status: active

### 3. Vertical Benchmark Research Packs
- lane: vertical-benchmark-research-packs
- customer: agencies, founders, and operators
- problem: teams lack current, structured intelligence on competitor positioning and offers
- offer: benchmark reports, offer maps, and market-monitoring subscriptions
- data_source: public websites, first-party notes, licensed sources, and internal analysis
- consent_model: public and licensed data only
- acquisition_path: inbound content, outbound to businesses, warm referrals
- delivery_path: one-off report or recurring subscription
- constraints: cite sources, avoid violating site terms, no deceptive scraping
- status: active

### 4. Contact List Brokerage
- lane: contact-list-brokerage
- customer: unknown
- problem: none beyond data resale
- offer: sell compiled email lists
- data_source: inboxes and arbitrary files
- consent_model: absent
- acquisition_path: gray-market data sales
- delivery_path: list export
- constraints: unlawful or non-compliant in many contexts
- status: rejected

Reason: rejected by compliance pack due to lack of consent, weak provenance, and pure personal-data arbitrage.
