# Strategy Intake Queue

Use this file as the landing zone for new venture ideas before they are activated.

Each opportunity should be scored for:
- legality,
- consent,
- data rights,
- autonomy fit,
- time to revenue,
- margin,
- defensibility,
- execution complexity.

---

## Current queue

### Opportunity 1: Inbox-to-CRM Assistant
- Summary: ingest owner-authorized inbound emails, classify them, extract lead/customer fields, and prepare compliant next actions.
- Data basis: first-party, owner-authorized mailbox data
- Monetization: monthly subscription or managed-service retainer
- Notes: strong fit for Agent Zero RAG, memory, and workflow automation

### Opportunity 2: Autonomous Listing Service
- Summary: transform seller-owned product inputs into improved listings, multi-channel publishing, and response support.
- Data basis: seller-owned assets and marketplace accounts
- Monetization: listing fee, subscription, or revenue share
- Notes: existing blueprint already lives in `docs/autonomous_listing_service.md`

### Opportunity 3: Research Product Studio
- Summary: convert lawful public research and internal operating knowledge into benchmark packs, niche market maps, or playbooks.
- Data basis: public business data, internal notes, customer-approved materials
- Monetization: report sales, membership, consulting upsell
- Notes: lower privacy risk than contact-data brokerage

## Rejected concepts

### Rejected: selling compiled personal email lists
- Status: rejected
- Reason: conflicts with the compliance pack and creates unnecessary privacy and legal exposure
- Safer replacement: sell workflow value, research value, or automation value instead of personal data
