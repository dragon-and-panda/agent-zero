## Intake checklist

Before a new idea moves forward, capture:

- customer and pain point
- what data is used and who owns it
- whether consent is required and how it is documented
- platform accounts or integrations required
- how the offer makes money
- how the lane will be scored for time-to-cash, margin, repeatability, automation fit, and defensibility
