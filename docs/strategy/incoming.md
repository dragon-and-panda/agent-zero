### REJECTED EXAMPLE - Contact-list brokerage from Gmail data

- title: sell compiled email lists from Google email data
- lane: out-of-bounds
- reason:

  - mailbox-derived contact harvesting
  - privacy and platform-risk conflict
- compliant pivots:
  - inbox-to-CRM assistant for the mailbox owner
  - opt-in lead capture and CRM hygiene service
  - public-data or client-owned research product

### CANDIDATE - Inbox-to-CRM assistant

- title: convert a consenting mailbox into a searchable CRM workflow
- lane: inbox-to-CRM operations assistant
- customer: founders, solo operators, small sales teams
- offer: triage inbox, summarize threads, draft replies, sync CRM
- monetization model: subscription plus premium setup
- data sources: user-authorized mailbox plus first-party CRM exports
- consent basis: mailbox owner authorization and first-party customer records
- platform or legal constraints: Google API terms, privacy law, channel rules
- success metric: hours saved per week and response-time improvement
- initial notes: no resale of mailbox contents or contacts
