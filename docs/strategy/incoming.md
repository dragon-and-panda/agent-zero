# Strategy Intake Queue

Use this file to drop new revenue or automation ideas before the orchestrator scores them.

Each entry should include:

- mission name
- customer or operator
- desired revenue model
- data sources
- data ownership and consent basis
- platform or jurisdiction constraints
- expected delivery workflow
- go / hold / reject recommendation

## Queue

### GO: Inbox-to-CRM Hygiene Service
- Revenue model: setup fee plus monthly retainer
- Data sources: client-owned inboxes, contact forms, CRM records
- Consent basis: client authorization over its own business systems
- Why it passes: strong first-party data posture and clear operational value

### GO: Autonomous Listing Concierge
- Revenue model: per-listing fee or monthly plan
- Data sources: seller-provided photos, notes, marketplace policies
- Consent basis: seller provides the data for listing creation and management
- Why it passes: service value is clear and personal-data resale is not required

### HOLD: Research Brief Subscription
- Revenue model: subscription
- Data sources: public research plus client-authorized internal materials
- Consent basis: public sources or explicit client authorization
- Why it is on hold: valuable but needs tighter packaging and positioning

### REJECT: Personal Email List Brokerage
- Revenue model: sell compiled email addresses
- Data sources: inboxes, files, scraped sources
- Consent basis: absent or unclear
- Why it fails: privacy-invasive, likely unlawful, and incompatible with the compliance pack
