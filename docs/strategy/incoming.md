## Seed opportunities

### 1. REJECT example: contact-list brokerage

- name: Export Gmail and file-based email addresses, organize them in Orange, sell the list
- owner: agentic_financial_system
- target customer: list buyers
- value created: none beyond access to personal contact data
- data sources: Gmail, uploaded files, contact exports
- monetization path: sell email lists
- compliance notes: violates privacy, consent, and data-provenance requirements
- score: reject
- decision: REJECT
- next step: replace with a first-party, opt-in demand generation workflow

### 2. HOLD example: first-party inbox opportunity mining

- name: Use owned inbox data to identify warm inbound leads and recurring pain points
- owner: agentic_financial_system
- target customer: inbound prospects or existing customers
- value created: faster response and better product prioritization
- data sources: authorized business inbox
- monetization path: convert inbound demand into services or products
- compliance notes: requires authority, data minimization, and retention rules
- score: hold pending workflow design
- decision: HOLD
- next step: define allowed fields, retention window, and CRM handoff
