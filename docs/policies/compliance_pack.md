# Compliance Pack

This pack defines the minimum compliance posture for autonomous revenue workflows built in this repository.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. use only lawful, documented, and consent-compatible data sources
2. do not harvest inboxes, contacts, or personal data without explicit authorization
3. do not compile, broker, rent, or sell personal email address lists
4. do not send spam, evade platform rules, or disguise outreach origin
5. do not use scraped, purchased, leaked, or uncertain-provenance contact datasets
6. collect only the minimum data needed for the approved workflow
7. keep deletion, unsubscribe, and audit requirements operational

## Prohibited workflows

- exporting Gmail or other inbox data to build resale lists
- combining files to create third-party contact dossiers
- scraping public sites for personal email addresses to resell or cold-blast
- disguising cold outreach as transactional messaging
- bypassing platform, anti-spam, or privacy rules with rotating accounts or obfuscated domains

## Allowed workflow patterns

- first-party CRM cleanup for a business that owns the contacts and has a lawful basis
- opt-in newsletter and lead magnet funnels
- inbox productivity for the mailbox owner, such as tagging, summarization, routing, and invoice extraction
- research, product, or content workflows using non-personal data or properly licensed datasets
- marketplace and listing automation from seller-provided assets

## Required controls for contact data

- document source and ownership of every contact dataset
- document lawful basis such as consent, contract, or legitimate customer relationship where applicable
- store proof of consent or provenance alongside the dataset
- separate internal operational use from marketing use
- respect geographic, platform, and sector-specific restrictions

## Safe monetization lanes

1. productized services based on first-party customer assets
2. digital products such as templates, reports, datasets without personal data, or courses
3. marketplace operations and listing optimization
4. opt-in audience building and premium research subscriptions
5. business process automation for clients who control their own data

## Escalation policy

Reject the task and redirect to a compliant alternative when a request involves:

- personal data resale
- unauthorized inbox access
- unclear ownership or consent
- spam or policy evasion
- regulated data without the required controls
