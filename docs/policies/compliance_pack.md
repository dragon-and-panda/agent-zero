# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Programs

This pack defines the minimum legal, privacy, and platform-compliance rules for any mission that aims to generate revenue with Agent Zero.

## Core rule
Revenue must come from lawful products or services, not from exploiting personal data, consent gaps, platform abuse, or deception.

## Allowed lanes
- First-party workflow automation for the data owner or an explicitly authorized client.
- Opt-in lead management, CRM hygiene, and consent-aware segmentation.
- Research products built from public, licensed, or client-supplied data.
- Listing, content, and operations services that improve a customer's existing business process.
- Internal analytics on lawfully obtained datasets.

## Explicitly prohibited
- Extracting, compiling, brokering, renting, or selling personal email lists.
- Accessing Gmail or other inboxes without explicit authorization from the account owner.
- Reusing inbox data for unrelated resale, spam, or third-party enrichment.
- Circumventing terms of service, anti-bot controls, or access restrictions.
- Impersonation, deceptive outreach, fake identities, or undisclosed automation where disclosure is required.
- Buying or selling regulated data categories without a verified lawful basis.

## Inbox and email data rules
If a workflow touches email or inbox data, all of the following must be true:
1. The account owner or client has explicitly authorized access.
2. Access is scoped to the minimum necessary permissions.
3. The data is used only for the owner's or client's direct workflow.
4. Retention, export, and deletion rules are documented.
5. The workflow does not create or sell third-party prospect lists.

## Outreach rules
- Prefer inbound demand, opt-in newsletters, referrals, and customer-success workflows.
- Only send outreach where there is a documented lawful basis and a practical unsubscribe path.
- Honor platform rate limits, anti-spam policies, and local law.
- Keep claims truthful, specific, and auditable.

## Monetization rule of thumb
Sell outcomes, software, analysis, or automation services. Do not sell personal contact data.

## Decision framework
Reject any opportunity that depends on at least one of the following:
- non-consensual personal data collection,
- resale of contact information,
- opaque data provenance,
- spam as a growth engine,
- platform evasion,
- legal ambiguity that cannot be resolved quickly with documentation.

When an opportunity is rejected, convert it into a compliant alternative. Examples:
- Reject: selling harvested inbox email lists.
- Convert to: consent-based inbox-to-CRM cleanup for a client that owns the inbox.
- Reject: scraping leads from private communities.
- Convert to: building a public-content research brief or opt-in waitlist funnel.

## Trading and financial-risk note
Do not deploy autonomous capital into speculative strategies as a first revenue lane. Any future trading-related work must stay in research or paper-trading mode until explicit risk limits, reserves, telemetry, and human review gates exist.
