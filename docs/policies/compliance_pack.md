# Compliance Pack for Agentic Revenue Work

This pack defines the minimum legal, ethical, privacy, and platform-compliance rules for any revenue-generating workflow built with Agent Zero.

## 1. Hard constraints

The agent must not:

- extract, broker, resell, or trade personal email addresses or contact lists;
- scrape or process private inboxes, contact books, or accounts without explicit authorization from the owner;
- use harvested, purchased, leaked, or unclear-provenance contact data for outreach;
- build workflows whose success depends on spam, deception, impersonation, or platform ToS evasion;
- treat "the user asked for it" as sufficient authorization for private or regulated data access.

Any workflow that fails one of the above constraints is rejected, not optimized.

## 2. Approved data sources

Use only data that is one of the following:

- first-party data collected with a documented opt-in;
- client-owned data provided under written authorization for a defined business purpose;
- public business information that is lawful to access and reuse under the source terms;
- internal operational data that does not create privacy or confidentiality conflicts.

If provenance, consent, or reuse rights are unclear, the workflow stays on HOLD until clarified.

## 3. Approved monetization lanes

Examples of acceptable lanes for this program:

- inbox-to-CRM summarization for a consenting business customer;
- opt-in newsletter, lead magnet, or research subscription products;
- public-data market maps, vendor directories, and listing services;
- automation services sold to clients for their own first-party workflows;
- agent-built internal tools that reduce cost or increase conversion on compliant funnels.

## 4. Outreach and messaging rules

When a workflow involves outreach:

- use only recipient data the operator is allowed to use;
- keep suppression, unsubscribe, and consent records;
- comply with applicable anti-spam and privacy laws;
- prefer warm, contextual, business-relevant contact over bulk cold outreach;
- never market "list sales" as a product.

## 5. Evaluation gates

Before activating a revenue lane, record answers for:

- legality;
- consent;
- provenance;
- platform terms;
- time-to-value;
- margin;
- repeatability;
- automation fit;
- defensibility.

Hard-gate failures return REJECT. Compliant but weak opportunities return HOLD. Only compliant, attractive lanes return PASS.

## 6. Orange DataScaping usage

Orange DataScaping or similar analysis tools may be used only on authorized, compliant datasets. They are for organization and analysis, not for bypassing consent or converting private communications into saleable personal-data inventories.

## 7. Preferred default

If a mission can be interpreted two ways, prefer the privacy-preserving version:

- first-party over third-party data;
- client-authorized processing over scraping;
- products and services over contact-list resale;
- public research over private data extraction.
