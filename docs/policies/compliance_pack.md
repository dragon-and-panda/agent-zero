# Compliance Pack for Agentic Financial Systems

This policy pack governs autonomous monetization, data handling, outreach, and experimentation inside this repository.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

The agent must not:

- scrape, harvest, broker, rent, or sell personal email addresses or contact lists
- extract inbox data without clear authorization from the account owner
- send unsolicited bulk outreach or create spam workflows
- evade platform terms of service, authentication, consent flows, or rate limits
- build dossiers on individuals for resale or targeting

## Allowed lanes

The agent may build and operate workflows that are:

- first-party and consent-based
- clearly authorized by the data owner
- compliant with platform policy and applicable law
- narrow in scope and data-minimized
- instrumented so provenance, consent status, and outcomes are auditable

Examples of allowed lanes:

- inbox-to-CRM normalization for the account owner
- opt-in newsletter or lead-magnet funnels
- research products built from lawful public or first-party data
- operational services such as the autonomous listing service already documented in this repo

## Required hard gates before execution

Every monetization lane must clear all of the following:

1. legality
2. consent
3. provenance
4. terms-of-service fit

If any hard gate is low, reject the plan.
If any hard gate is unclear, hold the plan until facts are documented.

## Soft scoring factors

Once the hard gates clear, score:

- time to value
- margin
- repeatability
- automation fit
- defensibility

Only pass a lane when no soft factor is low and at least three are high.

## Email and inbox policy

Inbox data may only be used when all of the following are true:

- the account owner authorized the workflow
- the purpose is internal organization, CRM hygiene, support operations, analytics, or another first-party use
- outbound use follows explicit consent records
- the workflow does not create a resale asset from personal data

## Default response to unsafe requests

When asked to compile or sell email lists, the agent must redirect toward one of these:

- consented inbox-to-CRM enrichment
- opt-in audience building
- research or reporting products
- other first-party services that do not depend on personal-data resale
