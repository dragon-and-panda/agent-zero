# Compliance Pack

This pack defines the minimum operating rules for any autonomous revenue, outreach, data-ingestion, or growth workflow in this repository.

## 1. Non-negotiable rules

1. Do not access email, cloud accounts, documents, or inboxes without clear owner authorization.
2. Do not scrape, compile, broker, buy, sell, or trade personal email lists.
3. Do not send spam, deceptive outreach, or bulk unsolicited messages.
4. Do not bypass platform rules, rate limits, anti-abuse systems, or paywalls.
5. Do not exfiltrate credentials, API keys, cookies, session tokens, or private data.
6. Do not use personal data for a new purpose unless consent and lawful basis are explicit.

## 2. Allowed patterns

- Owner-authorized mailbox analysis for summarization, search, tagging, and workflow automation.
- Retrieval-augmented generation over first-party files and documents uploaded by the workspace owner.
- Opt-in newsletters, communities, waitlists, and partnerships where contacts asked to hear from the business.
- Productized services, affiliate content, marketplaces, and software products that do not depend on privacy abuse.
- Manual or semi-automated research using public business information where platform terms allow it.

## 3. Sensitive workflow rules

### Inbox and Gmail usage

Allowed only when all of the following are true:

- The mailbox owner explicitly authorized access.
- Requested scopes are the minimum needed.
- Raw message export is avoided unless necessary.
- Contacts are not extracted for resale or cold outreach.
- Retention, deletion, and audit behavior are documented.

### Outreach and lead generation

Allowed only when all of the following are true:

- The audience opted in, requested contact, or there is another clear lawful basis.
- Messaging complies with platform rules and anti-spam requirements.
- The workflow includes unsubscribe, suppression, and frequency controls.
- The source of contact data is documented and reviewable.

## 4. Decision outcomes

- PASS: Legal, consented, low platform risk, and uses first-party or clearly permitted data.
- HOLD: Potentially viable, but authorization, scope, provenance, or platform constraints are unclear.
- REJECT: Involves personal-data resale, scraping inboxes without consent, spam, deception, or platform abuse.

## 5. Safe replacements for rejected ideas

Replace risky contact-list schemes with one of these:

- Build an opt-in niche newsletter and monetize through sponsorships or affiliates.
- Offer a productized research or automation service.
- Build a software workflow that saves time for paying users.
- Use owner-authorized inbox RAG to improve customer support, sales triage, or internal operations.
- Create public content, templates, or tools that attract inbound leads.
