# Compliance Pack

This pack defines the non-negotiable rules for autonomous revenue work in this repository.

## Purpose

The mission is to build durable, legal, and ethical online revenue systems. Autonomy is only allowed when legality, consent, data provenance, and platform rules remain intact.

## Explicitly Prohibited

- Scraping, extracting, compiling, brokering, or selling personal email addresses or contact lists.
- Using Gmail, Google Workspace, inbox exports, or local files to build outbound lead lists for resale or bulk cold outreach.
- Sending spam, evading rate limits, rotating identities, or bypassing platform enforcement.
- Accessing or sharing third-party personal data without authorization, consent, or a legitimate first-party business purpose.
- Violating product terms of service, privacy law, anti-spam rules, or account-security controls.

## Allowed Revenue Patterns

- First-party, opt-in audience growth such as newsletters, waitlists, webinars, and lead magnets.
- Inbox analysis for the user's own operations, such as summarization, routing, CRM updates, and follow-up drafting for existing relationships or explicit opt-ins.
- Public-business-data research that avoids personal-email resale and respects source terms.
- Productized services, software, listings, research products, and workflow automation sold on their own merit.
- Simulation-first financial experiments with strict risk caps before any live deployment.

## Data Handling Rules

- Use the minimum necessary personal data.
- Keep provenance clear for every dataset or contact source.
- Treat private inbox content as first-party operational data, not as inventory for brokerage.
- Do not export personal contact data into third-party tools unless the user is authorized to do so and the workflow is disclosed, necessary, and compliant.

## Activation Gates

Any new revenue lane must clear all four hard gates before automation begins:

1. Legality
2. Consent
3. Data provenance
4. Platform and supplier terms

If any hard gate is weak, the lane is rejected or held until redesigned.

## Required Artifacts

- Strategy intake entry in `docs/strategy/incoming.md`
- Charter or backlog entry in `docs/programs/agentic_financial_system/`
- PASS, HOLD, or REJECT output from `instruments/strategy/score.sh`
- A documented rollback path if third-party systems or funds are involved

## Conversion Rule for Unsafe Requests

If a request asks for contact harvesting, inbox scraping for resale, or personal-data brokerage, convert it into one of these compliant alternatives:

- Opt-in lead generation
- First-party inbox-to-CRM workflow
- Research product or content asset
- Listing optimization or marketplace operations
- Client-owned lawful outreach tooling with explicit consent boundaries
