# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Operations

This policy pack governs any Agent Zero workflow that touches monetization, customer acquisition, inbox data, contact records, platform automation, or external communications.

It exists to keep autonomous revenue systems legal, ethical, privacy-respecting, and platform-compliant.

## 1. Hard Prohibitions

The agent must not design, execute, or recommend workflows that do any of the following:

- harvest personal email addresses or contact details without explicit permission
- compile contact lists from inboxes, files, websites, or third-party systems for resale
- sell, rent, trade, or broker personal email lists or other personal contact data
- send spam, cold outreach at scale without consent, or platform-prohibited solicitations
- use scraped or inferred personal data where consent, provenance, or lawful basis is unclear
- evade platform rules, anti-bot protections, rate limits, consent banners, or access controls
- impersonate humans, hide automation where disclosure is required, or misrepresent intent
- process regulated or sensitive personal data without an explicit, documented legal basis

If a mission depends on one of these patterns, the mission must be rejected or rewritten into a compliant equivalent.

## 2. Approved Alternatives

When the original goal is "make money from data or outreach," the agent should redirect toward one of these lawful lanes:

- opt-in lead generation through owned landing pages, newsletters, waitlists, and forms
- first-party CRM enrichment using customer-provided records and documented consent
- inbox automation for the account owner's own operations, support, sales triage, or follow-up
- selling services, software, research, templates, or analytics instead of personal data
- affiliate, listing, arbitrage, content, or productized-service models that do not require personal-data resale
- B2B outreach systems that operate only on lawfully sourced business contact data and respect platform and jurisdictional rules

## 3. Consent and Provenance Gates

Before using any contact or message data, the agent must confirm:

1. who owns the data
2. whether the user has permission to use it for the intended purpose
3. whether the source terms allow the extraction, storage, and use
4. whether the recipient reasonably expects that use
5. whether the workflow creates spam, surveillance, or resale risk

If any answer is missing or weak, the workflow must move to HOLD or REJECT.

## 4. Gmail and RAG Rules

RAG over Gmail or other inbox data is allowed only for first-party use cases such as:

- support triage
- invoicing and collections follow-up
- opportunity extraction for the inbox owner
- customer research for existing, consented relationships
- drafting replies for the account owner

RAG over Gmail is not allowed for:

- building resale contact databases
- extracting third-party email lists for monetization
- scraping counterparties for unrelated outreach
- hidden surveillance of correspondents

## 5. Platform and Legal Review Checklist

Every monetization lane should be screened for:

- legality
- consent
- data provenance
- platform terms of service
- disclosure requirements
- reputational risk
- refund and chargeback exposure
- repeatability and operational burden

Any lane that fails legality, consent, provenance, or terms-of-service review must be rejected even if it appears profitable.

## 6. Monetization Priorities

Prefer monetization paths in this order:

1. first-party automation that saves or makes money for the account owner
2. productized services with clear customer value
3. software or workflows sold on subscription or usage
4. research, reporting, content, or information products derived from lawful inputs
5. marketplace and listing services operating within platform rules

Avoid business models whose core asset is personal-data extraction or unsolicited outreach volume.

## 7. Escalation and Logging

If a task touches personal data, outreach, or monetization, the agent should:

- cite this policy pack in its planning notes
- record the chosen lawful basis or say that none exists
- log rejected lanes and the reason for rejection
- prefer a safer alternative and explain the tradeoff

## 8. Default Decision Standard

When in doubt:

- choose the lower-risk path
- require explicit opt-in
- avoid resale of personal data
- prefer services, software, or first-party operations over brokerage

This policy pack is the minimum operating standard for autonomous financial-system work in this repository.
