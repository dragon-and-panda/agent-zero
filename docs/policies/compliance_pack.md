# Compliance Pack

This project may be used for monetization, automation, research, and data-processing tasks, but it must stay inside legal, ethical, and platform-policy boundaries.

## Hard prohibitions

Do not build, automate, or monetize workflows that involve:

- scraping or brokering personal email lists
- selling, renting, or trading contact data gathered from inboxes or files
- non-consensual inbox access, credential capture, or privacy-invasive collection
- spam, bulk cold outreach without consent, or platform-evasion tactics
- deceptive identity, fake testimonials, fabricated traffic, or false lead provenance

## Required gates before action

Every revenue lane must clear these gates before execution:

1. legality: the activity is lawful in the target jurisdiction
2. consent: the data owner and contact subjects have granted appropriate permission
3. provenance: the source of records is first-party or client-authorized and auditable
4. platform rules: the workflow complies with API terms, marketplace rules, and anti-spam policies

If any gate is weak or unknown, pause the lane and redesign it.

## Approved alternatives

Use these patterns instead of contact-data resale:

- client-authorized inbox-to-CRM hygiene
- opt-in newsletter growth and lead magnets
- productized research briefs and data products built from lawful public or licensed data
- autonomous listing and marketplace optimization services
- affiliate or referral funnels with honest disclosures

## Gmail and other email data

Email content may only be processed when all of the following are true:

- the mailbox owner explicitly authorized the workflow
- the purpose is operational help for that owner or client
- extracted contacts stay within the owner or client's first-party systems
- the workflow preserves unsubscribe, suppression, and consent metadata

Email-derived contacts must not be exported for resale.

## Risk posture for autonomous monetization

- start with simulation, scoring, and small pilots
- log data provenance and consent assumptions
- keep humans responsible for account creation, tax, legal, and compliance decisions
- prefer recurring value creation over one-time arbitrage
