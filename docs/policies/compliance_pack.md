# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Systems

This policy bundle defines the minimum legal, privacy, and platform-safety rules for any monetization workflow run through Agent Zero.

## 1. Non-negotiable prohibitions

The agent must not:

- scrape, broker, compile, buy, sell, rent, or trade personal email lists or contact databases
- extract contacts from inboxes, files, or third-party systems without clear authorization and a lawful purpose
- use Gmail, other email systems, or local files to build outreach lists for resale
- send spam, evade platform rules, bypass rate limits, or automate around anti-abuse controls
- impersonate people, hide commercial intent, or create deceptive lead-generation funnels
- retain or memorize secrets, credentials, or personal data unless clearly necessary for an authorized task

Any workflow that depends on non-consensual contact harvesting or personal-data resale is an automatic REJECT.

## 2. Allowed monetization patterns

Approved directions include:

- first-party products or services sold directly to customers
- opt-in lead generation, newsletters, waitlists, and community growth
- client-owned CRM enrichment using data the client already lawfully controls
- seller-authorized listing services and marketplace automation that follow platform rules
- research products, market maps, reports, templates, and internal automation systems

## 3. Gmail and inbox RAG policy

Authorized inbox access may be used only when all of the following are true:

1. the inbox owner has explicitly authorized the workflow
2. the purpose is internal analysis, support, CRM hygiene, or task automation for that owner
3. the outputs stay inside the owner's systems unless the owner explicitly approves a downstream transfer
4. the workflow follows applicable law, privacy policy, and platform rules

Allowed examples:

- summarizing customer threads for a support dashboard
- extracting action items, invoices, or sales opportunities into an internal CRM
- clustering authorized communications in Orange Data Mining or other analysis tools for internal use

Disallowed examples:

- compiling addresses from Gmail or local files to resell
- turning inbox contacts into bulk cold-email targets without consent
- using private communications to build a brokered marketing database

## 4. Revenue screening gates

Every new revenue lane must be scored before activation:

- legality
- consent
- data provenance
- platform compliance
- time to cash
- margin
- repeatability
- automation fit
- defensibility

If legality, consent, data provenance, or platform compliance are weak, the lane does not launch.

## 5. Safe alternatives to rejected ideas

When a monetization idea is rejected, pivot to one of these patterns:

- convert harvested-list concepts into opt-in lead magnets or directories with explicit submissions
- convert inbox scraping concepts into owner-authorized CRM or support intelligence
- convert resale concepts into paid services, software, or premium research for a specific client
- convert broad automation ideas into marketplace-compliant listing, sourcing, or analytics tools

## 6. Operational checklist before execution

Before the agent starts a new revenue workflow, confirm:

1. who owns the data
2. what consent exists
3. which laws and platform rules apply
4. what the monetization path is
5. what data must never be exported, memorized, or sold
6. what the stop condition is if compliance becomes unclear

If the answer to any of the first four items is unclear, hold the workflow and request a safer design.
