# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Systems

This policy pack defines the minimum rules for any monetization workflow built on Agent Zero.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Legality first**
   - Do not run workflows that are likely unlawful in the relevant jurisdiction.
   - Respect privacy law, consumer protection law, advertising rules, and platform terms.

2. **Consent and data rights**
   - Use personal data only when it is first-party, permissioned, or otherwise clearly authorized.
   - Do not harvest private inbox data, scrape personal contact details, or repurpose data beyond the scope the user agreed to.

3. **No personal-data brokerage**
   - Do not compile, buy, sell, rent, trade, or "monetize" personal email lists, phone lists, or private contact databases.
   - Do not build systems whose value proposition is bulk outreach to non-consenting people.

4. **No spam or deceptive growth**
   - No mass unsolicited messaging.
   - No fake reviews, fake identities, misleading claims, impersonation, or hidden affiliate relationships.

5. **Human approval for irreversible actions**
   - Require explicit approval before spending money, publishing to third-party platforms, contacting customers, or changing live systems.

## Approved revenue lanes

- Digital products sold to willing buyers
- Productized services with transparent scope and pricing
- Marketplace listings for lawful goods or services the operator has rights to sell
- Affiliate or partnership content with accurate disclosures
- Opt-in newsletters, waitlists, or CRM workflows where contacts knowingly subscribed
- Analytics and RAG on first-party business data with a clear business purpose

## Disallowed examples

- "Compile Gmail addresses from messages and sell the list"
- "Scrape contact info from inboxes or websites for cold outreach at scale"
- "Use private email data to build a lead database without consent"
- "Sell customer exports to third parties"

## Required launch checklist

- Legal basis is clear
- Data provenance is documented
- Consent state is documented
- Platform rules reviewed
- Human approval gate defined
- Rollback plan defined

If any of the above is missing, the workflow should default to HOLD or REJECT rather than execution.
