# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Programs

This pack defines the minimum legal, privacy, and platform-safety constraints for any autonomous monetization workflow built inside Agent Zero.

## Core rule

The system may pursue revenue only through lawful, ethical, consent-based activity. If a plan depends on privacy abuse, spam, deceptive claims, non-consensual data use, or breaking a platform's terms, the plan must be rejected or redesigned.

## Explicitly prohibited workflows

- Scraping or exporting inbox data without a clear user-owned, permissioned purpose.
- Harvesting, brokering, renting, or selling personal email lists or contact databases.
- Using Gmail or any mailbox as a source for bulk lead extraction or resale.
- Non-consensual outreach, spam campaigns, or attempts to evade sender-policy controls.
- Bypassing platform rate limits, anti-bot systems, identity checks, or marketplace rules.
- Reselling data whose provenance, license, or consent status is unclear.
- Performing regulated financial, legal, medical, or compliance work without the required approvals and controls.

## Allowed data sources

Only use data when at least one of these is true:

1. It is first-party data collected with clear consent for the intended use.
2. It is client-owned data, and the client has authorized the workflow.
3. It is public or licensed data whose terms allow the intended use.
4. It is internal operational data generated by the venture itself.

## Special handling for email and RAG

Email data may be used only for user-benefiting workflows such as:

- inbox search and summarization,
- support triage,
- CRM enrichment for the mailbox owner,
- drafting replies for the user or client,
- extracting tasks, invoices, or opportunities for the account owner.

Email data may not be used to build third-party mailing lists, broker contacts, or populate resale datasets.

## Approved Phase 1 monetization lanes

- Client-owned workflow automation where the customer pays for the automation service.
- Opt-in lead generation systems such as forms, lead magnets, waitlists, and newsletters.
- Research products, market maps, and data reports built from lawful sources.
- Listing, concierge, and marketplace automation that operates on customer-provided inventory and content.
- Internal tools that reduce operating cost or increase conversion on compliant channels.

## Required decision gates

Before activating a lane, score it for:

- legality,
- consent,
- data provenance,
- platform risk,
- time to cash,
- margin,
- repeatability,
- automation leverage,
- defensibility.

Any lane with weak legality, weak consent, weak provenance, or high platform risk must not launch.

## Operating checklist

Before execution, confirm all of the following:

- Who pays and why.
- What value is delivered.
- What data is used and where it came from.
- Whether the data subject or customer consented.
- Which platform rules apply.
- What happens if the workflow makes a mistake.

## Stop conditions

Stop and escalate or redesign the plan when:

- the data source is ambiguous,
- the workflow requires mass unsolicited contact,
- the plan depends on selling personal data,
- the workflow could lock, suspend, or harm user accounts,
- the offer cannot be explained honestly to a customer or regulator.
