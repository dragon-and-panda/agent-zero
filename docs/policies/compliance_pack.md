# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Programs

This policy pack defines the non-negotiable rules for any revenue-seeking workflow operated inside Agent Zero.

## 1. Hard prohibitions

Do not design, execute, or optimize workflows that:

- harvest personal email addresses or contact details without informed consent
- scrape inboxes, CRMs, address books, or platform messages without clear authorization
- compile, broker, rent, trade, or sell email lists or other personal contact datasets
- send or enable spam, bulk unsolicited outreach, or evasion of platform anti-abuse controls
- bypass terms of service, access controls, paywalls, captchas, or rate limits
- use regulated financial flows in a way that would require licensing, registration, or legal review that has not been completed
- hide provenance of data, misrepresent consent, or disguise automated behavior when disclosure is required

Any proposal that violates one of the rules above is rejected even if it appears profitable.

## 2. Allowed data principles

Autonomous workflows may use data only when all of the following are true:

1. The operator has the right to access and use the data.
2. The intended use matches the scope of user consent.
3. The provenance of the data can be explained and documented.
4. The planned workflow complies with platform rules, privacy law, and contract terms.
5. The minimum necessary data is used for the task.

## 3. Approved replacements for risky requests

Replace prohibited contact-list or inbox-brokerage ideas with one of these lawful patterns:

- first-party inbox to CRM enrichment for the operator's own business accounts
- opt-in newsletter, waitlist, or lead magnet programs
- productized research reports, market maps, and competitive intelligence
- listing optimization, syndication, and negotiation services for client-owned inventory
- client-authorized outreach systems that operate only on customer-provided and consented contacts
- internal workflow automation for finance, bookkeeping, vendor sourcing, or revenue operations

## 4. Gmail and email handling

RAG over email is allowed only for user-authorized productivity or business operations such as:

- summarizing the user's own inbox
- triaging support mail
- extracting structured records into a user-owned CRM or knowledge base
- identifying follow-ups on messages the user is already permitted to act on

RAG over email is not allowed for:

- harvesting third-party contacts for resale
- building cold outreach databases from inbox contents
- exposing personal correspondence beyond the minimum required use
- repurposing messages for unrelated monetization without consent

## 5. Financial-system mission gates

Before activating a monetization lane, score it on:

- legality
- consent
- data provenance
- platform-rule alignment
- time to cash
- margin
- repeatability
- automation leverage
- defensibility

Only activate lanes that clear the hard gates and remain attractive after the soft-factor review.

## 6. Operating rule for agents

When a request includes privacy abuse, unauthorized data use, or contact-data resale:

1. reject the unsafe portion
2. explain the blocking constraint briefly
3. redirect to a compliant alternative
4. log the safer lane in strategy docs or memory for future runs
