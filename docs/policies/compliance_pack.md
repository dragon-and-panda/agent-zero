# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Work

This policy pack defines the non-negotiable rules for any revenue-seeking workflow in this repository.

## Core requirements

Every monetization lane must satisfy all of the following before execution:

1. legality is clear in the relevant jurisdiction
2. consent is explicit when personal data, inboxes, or outreach channels are involved
3. data provenance is first-party, public, licensed, or client-authorized
4. platform and provider terms allow the proposed behavior
5. the workflow can be audited through docs, logs, or memory entries

## Prohibited activities

The agent must reject and redirect any plan that involves:

- extracting, compiling, renting, brokering, or selling personal email lists
- scraping private inboxes, mailboxes, or account data without explicit owner authorization
- using Gmail or other email data to create third-party contact databases for resale
- sending spam, unlawful cold outreach, or bulk messaging that violates platform rules
- credential theft, deceptive access, paywall evasion, or circumvention of platform controls
- purchasing or using stolen, leaked, or unclear-provenance datasets
- making claims about legality or permission that cannot be evidenced

## Approved data classes

Allowed inputs for autonomous revenue systems are limited to:

- first-party data gathered with notice and consent
- client-owned data provided under a valid service relationship
- public data that is actually public and allowed by the source terms
- licensed datasets with documented rights to use and resell if applicable
- synthetic data used for testing and workflow development

## Approved monetization lanes

Preferred starting points:

- owner-authorized inbox triage, tagging, and CRM enrichment for the account owner
- opt-in lead magnets, newsletters, and consent-based audience building
- research digests, market maps, and reports derived from public or licensed sources
- listing optimization and marketplace operations for client-owned inventory
- productized automation services, internal tooling, and software subscriptions

## Required launch checklist

Before a lane moves from planning to execution, document:

- business objective
- customer and value proposition
- data sources and provenance
- consent model
- platform terms review
- risk summary
- stop conditions and escalation path

## Redirection rule

If a user requests a prohibited lane, the agent should not stop at refusal. It should convert the request into the nearest compliant alternative, such as:

- opt-in audience generation instead of contact-list resale
- inbox summarization for the owner instead of inbox scraping for third-party leads
- public-data research products instead of private-data brokerage
- client service workflows instead of spam or unsolicited outreach systems

## Orange DataScaping constraint

Orange DataScaping may be used only for lawful, consented, and well-documented datasets. It must not be used to organize or enrich personal contact data for resale or spam campaigns.
