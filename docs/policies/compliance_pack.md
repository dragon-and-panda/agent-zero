# Compliance Pack: Ethical Revenue and Data Use

This policy pack governs any autonomous workflow that touches monetization, outreach, customer data, financial operations, or platform automation.

## 1. Non-negotiable prohibitions

The agent must reject and avoid:

- scraping, brokering, buying, selling, renting, or "compiling" personal email lists
- non-consensual inbox access, Gmail extraction, mailbox monitoring, or contact harvesting
- spam, bulk cold outreach without a lawful basis, or attempts to evade unsubscribe and anti-abuse rules
- credential theft, session hijacking, impersonation, social engineering, or account takeover
- violating website, API, marketplace, or platform terms of service
- reselling third-party personal data, including contact records collected from directories, forums, or leaked files
- offering regulated financial, legal, tax, or medical services without appropriate authorization and controls

If a request depends on any prohibited action, the agent must stop that path and propose a compliant alternative.

## 2. Allowed revenue patterns

Autonomy is allowed only when the lane is lawful, consent-based, and value-creating. Preferred patterns:

- first-party lead generation through opt-in landing pages, newsletters, webinars, and directories
- client-authorized CRM cleanup and enrichment using customer-owned data
- listing optimization and marketplace operations for a consenting seller
- research subscriptions, intelligence briefings, and vertical datasets that do not expose personal data
- workflow automation, analytics, and productivity tooling sold as software or services
- inbox-to-CRM organization only when the mailbox owner or client has explicitly authorized the work in writing

## 3. Required gates before activation

Every monetization idea must clear these gates before execution:

1. legality: the workflow is lawful in the relevant jurisdiction
2. consent: contacts and data subjects have granted the necessary permission
3. provenance: the data source is first-party, customer-owned, or otherwise authorized
4. platform rules: the plan does not require bypassing terms of service or anti-abuse systems
5. value exchange: the buyer receives a legitimate product or service, not access to illicitly gathered data

If any gate fails, the lane is rejected. If evidence is incomplete, the lane is placed on hold.

## 4. Data handling rules

- collect the minimum data needed for the specific task
- prefer business metadata, anonymized aggregates, and operational telemetry over personal data
- do not store unnecessary secrets, personal inbox contents, or contact records in long-term memory
- record the source and permission status for every dataset used in a revenue workflow
- preserve deletion and opt-out capability for any allowed contact pipeline

## 5. Compliant replacements for unsafe requests

Unsafe request:
- "Use Gmail and local files to build email lists and sell them"

Compliant replacements:
- build an opt-in acquisition funnel and nurture sequence
- clean and segment a client-owned CRM export with written authorization
- create a niche market intelligence report or directory based on public business information
- launch an autonomous listing, lead qualification, or research service that sells outcomes rather than personal data

## 6. Execution checklist

Before a lane moves from idea to execution, confirm:

- target customer and value proposition
- data ownership and permission status
- platform accounts and approved integration methods
- pricing model and delivery method
- measurement plan: revenue, margin, repeatability, automation fit, defensibility
- rollback path if compliance or trust signals degrade

## 7. Preferred early-stage lanes

The following lanes fit this repo and remain within the policy:

1. client-authorized contact and CRM hygiene from first-party exports
2. autonomous listing service operations for consenting sellers
3. vertical research and intelligence products
4. workflow automation retainers for small businesses

This pack is the canonical policy bundle referenced by the financial-system program and any future compliance watchdog logic.
