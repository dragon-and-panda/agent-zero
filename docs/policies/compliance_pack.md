# Compliance Pack for the Agentic Financial System

This pack defines the non-negotiable guardrails for any autonomous revenue program operated from this repository. It is written to keep the system profitable, lawful, privacy-preserving, and defensible over time.

---

## 1. Mission Constraint

The mission is to build self-sustaining online revenue systems using legal, ethical, and operationally durable methods.

Profit is a goal, but it does **not** override:
- privacy rights,
- platform terms,
- anti-spam and consumer-protection laws,
- intellectual-property rights,
- truthful marketing requirements,
- financial-risk controls.

---

## 2. Explicitly Prohibited Activities

The system must not be used to:
- scrape, compile, broker, or sell personal email addresses or contact lists,
- extract Gmail or other mailbox data without the account owner's informed authorization,
- repurpose inbox data for third-party resale,
- send cold outreach that bypasses consent, unsubscribe, or identity requirements,
- impersonate individuals, falsify sender identity, or obscure commercial intent,
- buy or sell datasets of personal information gathered without a clear lawful basis,
- evade marketplace, payment, or email-service rules,
- automate behavior that is deceptive, fraudulent, or manipulative.

Any request that centers on harvesting or reselling personal data must be re-scoped into a compliant alternative before work continues.

---

## 3. Allowed Data Uses

Approved data handling patterns include:
- first-party processing of data owned by the operator or client,
- inbox triage and CRM extraction for the owner's own business operations,
- enrichment of records using lawful, documented business sources,
- opt-in lead capture with clear notice and consent,
- analytics on anonymized or aggregated data,
- use of public business information where the source terms allow it.

### Gmail / email RAG rule

If email data is used in RAG workflows, all of the following must be true:
1. The mailbox owner has authorized the workflow.
2. Retrieval is limited to a defined operational purpose such as support triage, invoicing, follow-up, or CRM hygiene.
3. Retrieved content is not exported into resale datasets.
4. Sensitive fields are minimized, redacted, or excluded where possible.
5. Retention and deletion rules are documented.

---

## 4. Approved Revenue Lanes

The default opportunity set for this mission is:

1. **Inbox-to-CRM automation**
   - Parse the operator's or client's inbound emails.
   - Convert qualified conversations into structured CRM records.
   - Draft compliant follow-up tasks for human approval or policy-bound sending.

2. **Autonomous Listing Service**
   - Improve and syndicate seller-owned listings.
   - Monetize through service fees, subscriptions, or revenue share.

3. **Research products**
   - Produce market maps, prospect intelligence, benchmark reports, or category datasets from lawful sources.
   - Sell insights, not personal contact lists.

4. **Content and educational assets**
   - Turn operating playbooks into templates, audits, micro-courses, or premium newsletters.

5. **Client automation services**
   - Build compliant automations for businesses using their own systems, audiences, and permissions.

---

## 5. Opportunity Gate

Before a new revenue lane is activated, it must pass all of the following gates:

| Gate | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Legality | No obvious conflict with privacy, anti-spam, consumer, or contract law |
| Consent | Data subjects or clients provide the permissions required for the workflow |
| Platform safety | Execution respects source and destination platform rules |
| Data rights | Inputs are owned, licensed, or lawfully accessible |
| Auditability | Actions and decisions can be reconstructed from artifacts and logs |
| Unit economics | There is a credible path to positive contribution margin |

If any gate fails, the lane is rejected or redesigned.

---

## 6. Operating Controls

- Keep a mission diary for each active revenue lane.
- Record why a lane was accepted or rejected.
- Prefer sandbox data and simulations before touching live customer workflows.
- Keep a clear separation between exploratory research, production automations, and any live financial exposure.
- Require human review for contracts, regulated claims, and account-level permission changes.

---

## 7. Default Strategic Position

When there is ambiguity, prefer:
- opt-in over scraped,
- first-party over third-party,
- service revenue over data resale,
- recurring software or workflow value over one-off list arbitrage,
- transparent automation over stealth automation.

That posture lowers legal risk and improves long-term business durability.
