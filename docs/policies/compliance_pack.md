# Compliance Pack: Ethical Revenue Automation

This pack defines the minimum rules for any autonomous workflow intended to earn revenue inside Agent Zero.

## Mission Standard

Revenue plans must be legal, consent-based, transparent, and compatible with platform terms. If a plan depends on privacy abuse, deception, spam, or personal-data resale, the plan must be rejected or converted into a compliant alternative.

## Non-Negotiable Prohibitions

- Do not scrape, broker, rent, trade, or sell personal email addresses or contact lists.
- Do not access inboxes, mailboxes, drives, or account data without clear owner authorization.
- Do not export contacts from Gmail or other systems for resale, spam, or non-consensual outreach.
- Do not automate deceptive outreach, impersonation, hidden tracking, or platform-evasion behavior.
- Do not bypass authentication, rate limits, paywalls, or terms of service.
- Do not store third-party personal data in memory or knowledge bases unless it is necessary, authorized, and tied to a legitimate user-owned workflow.

## Allowed Patterns

The framework may support the following when the operator has the right to act and the workflow is clearly disclosed:

1. First-party opt-in audience generation
   - newsletters
   - lead magnets
   - waitlists
   - webinar or community signups

2. Client-owned CRM and inbox automation
   - deduping or segmenting contacts already owned by the client
   - owner-authorized Gmail or mailbox summarization, search, triage, or routing
   - inbox-to-CRM extraction for internal operations only

3. Marketplace and listing workflows
   - listing generation
   - listing optimization
   - catalog enrichment
   - compliant publishing automation

4. Research and information products
   - market maps
   - niche directories created from public business information
   - non-personal aggregate datasets
   - paid reports or recurring research briefs

5. Commissioned agent services
   - support automation
   - prospect research using public business sources
   - document processing
   - reporting
   - workflow automation for paying clients

## Special Handling: Gmail, RAG, and Orange Data Workflows

- Retrieval over Gmail or email archives is only allowed for owner-authorized search, summarization, categorization, or internal CRM syncing.
- Extracted contacts must remain inside the owner-authorized system of record unless the contact originally opted in for the new use.
- Orange DataScaping or similar tooling may be used only on lawful, documented, user-owned or opt-in datasets. It must not be used to organize scraped or purchased personal contact lists for resale.

## Required Decision Gates

Before a revenue lane is activated, the agent must confirm:

1. legality is high
2. consent is high
3. data provenance is high
4. platform risk is low
5. the monetization model does not rely on personal-data resale

If any gate is unclear, hold the lane. If any gate fails, reject the lane.

## Safe Conversions for Rejected Requests

If a request asks for inbox scraping, contact harvesting, or email-list sales, convert it into one of these:

- build an opt-in funnel instead of harvesting contacts
- operate on a client-owned CRM instead of third-party inbox data
- sell a research product instead of personal data
- sell a workflow automation service instead of a contact list
- sell listing optimization or marketplace operations instead of spam-driven lead resale

## Operator Reminder

The fastest path to cash is not automatically the safest path to durable revenue. Default to repeatable, permissioned systems that can survive audits, platform review, and customer scrutiny.
