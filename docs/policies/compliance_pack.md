# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Programs

This pack defines the non-negotiable rules for any monetization lane run through this repository. It exists to keep the agent profitable through lawful, ethical, privacy-respecting work rather than short-term tactics that create regulatory, platform, or reputational risk.

## 1. Core policy

All revenue programs must satisfy these conditions before execution:

1. Legal in the relevant jurisdiction.
2. Based on clear consent, authorization, or an obvious legitimate business relationship.
3. Built from first-party, client-owned, or lawfully public data with documented provenance.
4. Compatible with the terms and policies of the platforms involved.
5. Capable of being explained honestly to a customer, partner, regulator, or platform reviewer.

If a lane fails any of the above, the agent must reject or redesign it before proceeding.

## 2. Explicit prohibitions

The framework must not:

- scrape, compile, broker, or sell personal email lists;
- mine Gmail or other inbox data for resale, spam, or non-consensual outreach;
- bypass consent requirements or hide the true source of contact data;
- use stolen credentials, session hijacking, or unauthorized account access;
- run spam campaigns, deceptive lead generation, or fake engagement schemes;
- create or trade datasets that contain personal data without a clear lawful basis;
- ignore platform rules because the user requested it or because the tactic looks profitable.

These restrictions apply even if the user claims the data is "available" somewhere in files, emails, exports, or browser sessions.

## 3. Allowed data handling patterns

The framework may work with data when the use is narrowly scoped, documented, and compliant. Examples:

- first-party CRM enrichment for a business that owns the customer relationship;
- inbox triage for the account owner when the task is support, operations, or follow-up rather than resale;
- public business research that avoids collecting personal contact details beyond what is necessary for legitimate B2B workflow support;
- opt-in newsletter, waitlist, or lead-magnet systems where consent is captured and stored;
- analytics and segmentation on consented or client-provided records using tools such as Orange Data Mining.

## 4. Approved monetization families

Preferred revenue lanes for this repo:

- done-for-you services with explicit customer authorization;
- software products, internal tools, or agentic workflow automation sold to consenting clients;
- research products, reports, templates, and operational playbooks;
- listing management, reputation support, and business profile maintenance for paying clients;
- consent-based outbound systems where messaging and suppression controls are documented.

## 5. Gmail and RAG boundaries

RAG over email or inbox content is allowed only when all of the following are true:

- the inbox owner or business has explicitly authorized the workflow;
- the purpose is operational support, knowledge retrieval, summarization, or client-owned CRM upkeep;
- the extracted data stays within the owner’s environment or a documented downstream system they control;
- no personal email list is sold, rented, or reused for unrelated outreach.

If the intended use is list resale, mass cold outreach, or personal-data brokerage, the lane is rejected.

## 6. Platform and outreach rules

Before using any marketplace, directory, CRM, advertising network, or outreach channel, verify:

- acceptable-use policy;
- automation policy;
- data-use and resale rules;
- spam and suppression requirements;
- geographic compliance requirements.

When uncertain, hold the lane until policy is confirmed.

## 7. Required documentation for each new lane

Every proposed revenue lane should record:

- customer and value exchange;
- data sources and provenance;
- consent basis or customer authorization model;
- platform dependencies;
- hard-gate score for legality, consent, provenance, and platform alignment;
- soft score for time to cash, margin, repeatability, automation, and defensibility;
- pilot plan and rollback criteria.

## 8. Default redirect for rejected requests

If a user asks for email-list brokerage, inbox scraping, or contact resale, redirect them toward one of these compliant alternatives:

- build an opt-in audience and newsletter;
- sell a research report instead of raw contacts;
- offer a client-authorized CRM cleanup or inbox-to-CRM service;
- create a business listing or reputation-management service;
- package automation or analytics as software or a managed service.
