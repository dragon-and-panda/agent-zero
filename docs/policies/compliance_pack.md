# Compliance Pack

This project can be used to automate research, workflows, and monetization planning. That freedom requires explicit guardrails.

## Hard prohibitions

Do not use Agent Zero to:

- scrape, harvest, or compile personal email addresses for sale or unsolicited outreach
- access Gmail, Google Workspace, or any inbox content without explicit authorization from the account owner
- sell, rent, broker, or otherwise monetize personal contact lists
- buy third-party contact data of unclear provenance
- run spam, bulk unsolicited outreach, credential abuse, fraud, or deceptive impersonation
- retain or exfiltrate secrets, inbox contents, or personal data beyond the minimum needed for an authorized workflow

## Approved data sources

Use only:

- first-party customer or subscriber data with explicit consent
- user-uploaded business materials and documents
- public company information that does not expose personal contact details
- licensed datasets with terms that explicitly allow the intended use
- aggregated or anonymized data products that cannot reasonably identify a person

## Approved revenue patterns

Prefer:

- productized services
- subscription software
- digital products and templates
- affiliate content with disclosure
- premium research built from lawful, non-personal, or anonymized sources
- opt-in newsletters, communities, and lifecycle messaging

## Required controls

Before launching a revenue workflow, document:

1. data source and provenance
2. consent status
3. platform rules and account terms
4. customer value proposition
5. fallback or review path if legality or privacy is unclear

## Example

- rejected: extract Gmail addresses from archived mail and sell the list
- approved alternative: offer a lead magnet, collect explicit newsletter consent, and monetize with sponsorships, services, or software
