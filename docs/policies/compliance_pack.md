# Revenue Compliance Pack

This policy pack defines the minimum legal, privacy, and ethical constraints for autonomous revenue programs run with Agent Zero.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Do not harvest, broker, buy, sell, or exchange personal email lists or other personal contact databases.
- Do not access, process, export, or monetize inbox contents without the informed consent of the account owner and a legitimate user-benefiting purpose.
- Do not send spam, evade platform anti-abuse controls, bypass CAPTCHA systems for prohibited use, or automate outreach that violates a provider's terms.
- Do not misrepresent identity, fabricate testimonials, impersonate a human operator, or conceal that an automated system is acting on a business's behalf when disclosure is required.
- Do not collect or process personal data beyond what is necessary for the approved service lane.
- Do not move into regulated activities such as investment advice, lending, or claims of guaranteed returns without an explicit compliance review and domain-specific controls.

## Approved monetization patterns

- Consent-based inbox-to-CRM hygiene for a user or client who authorizes mailbox processing.
- First-party lead capture systems where contacts opt in through forms, landing pages, waitlists, or explicit business relationships.
- Marketplace listing optimization, reposting, and customer-response assistance within platform rules.
- Research products, curated datasets built from public or licensed sources, and workflow automation sold as software or services.
- Agency services for copy, analytics, enrichment of client-owned lawful data, or operational support.

## Required checks before launch

1. Confirm the buyer, operator, and data owner relationships are documented.
2. Confirm the workflow has a lawful data source and a clear consent basis where personal data is involved.
3. Confirm platform terms and anti-spam rules are compatible with the automation plan.
4. Confirm there is an operator-visible audit trail: source, transforms, exports, recipients, and retention.
5. Confirm the offer creates user or client value beyond raw data extraction or resale.

## Automatic rejection examples

- "Build a scraper to collect Gmail contacts for resale."
- "Sell compiled email lists to marketing services."
- "Use RAG over inboxes to extract every address and package them for buyers."
- "Automate outreach from harvested contacts without opt-in."

## Redirection examples

- Replace email-list resale with a consent-based inbox cleanup and CRM synchronization service.
- Replace harvested outreach with first-party lead magnets, newsletters, and opt-in prospect funnels.
- Replace raw data brokering with a research product, benchmark report, or marketplace operations service.

## Delivery expectations

- Prefer products or services with clear customer benefit, repeatable operations, and low regulatory overhead.
- Keep humans available for exception handling, consent verification, and account-level approval when needed.
- Maintain documentation under `docs/programs/` and score each lane before activation.
