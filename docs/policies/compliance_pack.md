# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Missions

This policy pack defines the minimum legal, ethical, and platform-compliance standards for any monetization workflow operated by Agent Zero or its sub-agents.

## 1. Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not scrape, compile, broker, rent, or sell personal email addresses, phone numbers, or contact lists.
- Do not access inboxes, chats, CRMs, or private files without the owner's explicit authorization and a legitimate business purpose.
- Do not use retrieved personal data for spam, cold outreach blasts, list resale, credential recovery, impersonation, or surveillance.
- Do not bypass platform rules, anti-bot controls, rate limits, CAPTCHAs, or terms of service.
- Do not misrepresent identity, product capabilities, pricing, affiliations, or legal rights.
- Do not enter regulated flows such as financial advice, securities trading, lending, insurance, healthcare, or legal services without mission-specific controls and human review.

## 2. Hard Gates Before Any Venture Launches

Every opportunity must clear all four hard gates:

1. **Legality:** The workflow is lawful in the target jurisdiction.
2. **Consent:** Users whose data or channels are involved have clearly opted in or are the direct owners.
3. **Data provenance:** The source of data is documented, first-party or licensed, and auditable.
4. **Platform compatibility:** The workflow is allowed by the target platform's rules and usage limits.

If any hard gate is weak or unknown, the workflow is rejected or placed on hold pending evidence.

## 3. Approved Revenue Patterns

Prefer business models that create value without exploiting personal data:

- Opt-in lead magnets and newsletters
- First-party inbox-to-CRM or inbox-to-task automation for the inbox owner
- Listing optimization and marketplace concierge services for the seller who hired the agent
- Research products, templates, and paid knowledge bases
- Affiliate content with clear disclosure
- B2B workflow automation sold to consenting customers
- Micro-SaaS products that process customer-owned data under contract

## 4. Prohibited Monetization Patterns

The following are out of scope:

- Selling email lists or lead lists derived from inboxes, scraped files, or third-party sources
- Bulk cold outreach generated from harvested contacts
- Reselling private messages, inbox content, or contact graphs
- Data enrichment from unauthorized credentials or shared mailboxes
- Marketplace spam, fake listings, fake reviews, or astroturfing
- Any scheme whose unit economics depend on abusing privacy or breaking platform rules

## 5. Email and Messaging Rules

- Email data may only be used for the direct benefit of the account owner or their authorized organization.
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation over email is allowed only for summarization, search, triage, CRM drafting, and task extraction for the owner.
- Contact extraction is allowed only to improve the owner's internal workflows, not for resale or spam.
- Any outbound messaging must be targeted, consent-based, and reviewable, with opt-out language when appropriate.

## 6. Required Evidence to Promote a Lane

Before moving an idea from exploration to execution, capture:

- Problem statement and customer segment
- Revenue mechanism
- Proof of consent and data provenance
- Relevant platform and policy notes
- Risk register with mitigation steps
- Expected margin, repeatability, automation fit, and time-to-cash

## 7. Escalation Rules

Escalate to the sponsor or Risk & Ethics Governor if:

- personal data is involved
- platform rules are ambiguous
- payment handling or financial claims are introduced
- third-party data licensing is unclear
- the workflow could reasonably be perceived as spam or surveillance

## 8. Default Decision Standard

When in doubt, prefer:

1. first-party over third-party data
2. opt-in over scraped audiences
3. services and software over data resale
4. narrow, high-trust outreach over scale-first blasting
5. reversible experiments over irreversible compliance risk
