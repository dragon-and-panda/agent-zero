# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Programs

## Purpose

This pack defines the non-negotiable rules for any autonomous revenue mission run inside Agent Zero. Its job is to keep growth efforts lawful, privacy-preserving, platform-compliant, and ethically defensible.

## Non-negotiable prohibitions

- Do not access, scrape, export, sell, rent, or broker personal email addresses, inbox contents, contact graphs, or other personal data without explicit, revocable authorization from the data subject or lawful data controller.
- Do not build or operate businesses around personal email list brokerage, unsolicited bulk outreach, spam, account takeover, credential sharing, or session theft.
- Do not use Gmail, Google Workspace, or any inbox RAG workflow to harvest contacts for resale or cold outreach.
- Do not bypass CAPTCHA, anti-bot protections, API restrictions, rate limits, or platform terms of service to obtain leads, listings, or customer data.
- Do not run regulated activities such as investment advice, securities trading for others, lending, legal services, tax advice, medical advice, or employment screening without the required licenses, disclosures, controls, and approvals.
- Do not store more personal data than is necessary for the user-approved workflow, and do not retain personal data once the approved purpose has ended.

## Approved operating patterns

- First-party inbox-to-CRM automation for the account owner or lawful business operator.
- Opt-in lead generation through forms, lead magnets, newsletters, webinars, or customer-requested demos.
- Productized services such as listing optimization, research briefs, content packages, workflow automation, or internal operations tooling.
- Marketplace, media, affiliate, template, data-cleaning, and analytics businesses built on public, licensed, synthetic, or customer-authorized data.
- RAG over authorized files or inboxes when the purpose is operational assistance for the data owner, not resale or unsolicited outreach.

## Safe Gmail and file-extraction pattern

If a workflow uses inbox or file data, all of the following must be true:

1. The account owner or lawful controller gave explicit permission for the exact use case.
2. The workflow uses the minimum data scope needed to deliver the service.
3. Extracted data stays inside the owner-approved system of record, such as a CRM, help desk, invoicing system, or research workspace.
4. The workflow does not resell, publish, or syndicate personal contact data.
5. The workflow keeps an auditable description of consent, purpose, and retention.
6. Access can be revoked and data can be deleted on request.

## Orange DataScaping / analysis rules

- Only analyze first-party, public, licensed, synthetic, or properly anonymized data.
- Do not use Orange or similar tooling to organize or score personal email lists for resale.
- Do not attempt to deanonymize data sets or join them with harvested contact information.
- Prefer analyses that improve a customer-owned workflow, such as segmentation of opted-in leads, ticket clustering, inbox categorization, or listing performance analysis.

## Decision gates

- Reject any idea that depends on personal-data resale, non-consensual inbox access, or unsolicited mass outreach.
- Hold any idea with unclear consent, unclear data provenance, unclear platform legality, or regulated-domain exposure.
- Proceed only when legality, consent, provenance, and platform compatibility are all clearly supported.

## Preferred substitutes for unsafe ideas

When a request proposes contact-list resale or inbox harvesting, convert it into one of these lawful alternatives instead:

- Customer-authorized inbox-to-CRM assistant
- Opt-in newsletter and sponsorship business
- Research brief or intelligence subscription
- Listing optimization and marketplace concierge service
- Workflow automation templates or managed services

## Operating note

This pack is a product and workflow policy, not legal advice. When a task touches regulated domains or jurisdiction-specific rules, escalate for specialist review before live deployment.
