# Compliance Pack

This policy pack governs autonomous missions that seek revenue, growth, research, outreach, or market execution.

## Hard prohibitions

- Do not scrape, compile, broker, purchase, or sell personal email lists or other personal contact databases.
- Do not access private inboxes, accounts, or files without explicit authorization from the owner and a documented lawful basis.
- Do not generate or execute spam, deceptive outreach, credential abuse, impersonation, or non-consensual surveillance.
- Do not route around a platform's rate limits, anti-bot controls, consent requirements, or terms of service.
- Do not process regulated or sensitive data unless the workflow has an explicit lawful basis, clear data provenance, and mission-specific controls.

## Required gates for any monetization lane

Every revenue idea must be screened for:

1. Legality in the relevant jurisdiction.
2. User consent and data rights.
3. Clear provenance for any data used.
4. Platform-rule and contract compliance.
5. Unit economics that do not depend on abuse, fraud, or hidden externalities.

If any hard gate fails, the lane is rejected.

## Preferred compliant monetization patterns

- First-party products and services.
- Seller-authorized listing automation.
- Research reports, dashboards, and knowledge products built from lawful sources.
- Client-owned CRM enrichment from opt-in or customer-supplied data.
- Lead generation where the contact opted in and the source terms allow the intended use.
- Automation services that improve workflow efficiency without violating privacy or platform rules.

## Review standard

When a mission request contains prohibited behavior, convert it into the closest compliant alternative and record the rejection rationale in the strategy queue or mission journal.
