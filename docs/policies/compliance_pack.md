# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Programs

This policy pack defines the non-negotiable rules for any autonomous revenue lane run from this repository.

## 1. Hard prohibitions

The system must not:

- collect, broker, rent, resell, or trade personal email addresses or contact lists
- scrape inboxes, Google email data, or private files for resale or unsolicited outreach
- send spam, mass cold outreach without consent, or deceptive lead-generation campaigns
- impersonate people, bypass access controls, or automate account abuse
- use data in ways that violate privacy law, contracts, or platform terms of service
- make financial, legal, tax, or investment claims without explicit human review

Any plan that depends on personal-data resale or non-consensual inbox access is an automatic reject.

## 2. Allowed patterns

The system may pursue revenue only through lawful, consent-based, and auditable lanes such as:

- first-party inbox assistance for the owner of the mailbox
- opt-in newsletter growth and customer acquisition
- AI-assisted listing, catalog, or merchandising services for consenting clients
- research products built from public, licensed, or aggregated data
- workflow automation, analytics, or content operations sold as a service
- customer-owned CRM enrichment where data provenance and permissions are documented

## 3. Data governance rules

Before using any data source, verify and record:

1. who owns the data
2. whether the owner granted permission
3. where the data came from
4. whether the intended use matches the original consent and terms

If any of those are unclear, the lane stays on hold or is rejected.

## 4. Required review questions

Every monetization idea must answer:

- legality: Is the workflow lawful in the operating jurisdiction?
- consent: Did the data subject or customer explicitly permit this use?
- provenance: Is the data source documented and trustworthy?
- terms: Does the workflow comply with provider and platform rules?
- repeatability: Can the lane produce revenue without resorting to abusive tactics?

## 5. Phase gates

- Phase 0: policy screen and scoring
- Phase 1: dry run with synthetic or first-party consented data
- Phase 2: small live pilot with logging and clear opt-out paths
- Phase 3: scale only after quality, compliance, and unit economics are proven

## 6. Default safe pivots

If a proposal fails because it relies on private contact data, pivot to one of these:

- build a consent-based lead magnet and capture opt-ins directly
- summarize a founder's own inbox and convert inbound demand into CRM records
- sell a service that improves listings, landing pages, or support operations
- package public-market research into a report, dashboard, or subscription
