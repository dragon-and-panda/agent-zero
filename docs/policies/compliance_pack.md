# Compliance Pack

This pack governs autonomous revenue, growth, data, and outreach workflows in Agent Zero.

## 1. Non-negotiable rules

- Do not scrape private inboxes, mailboxes, or closed files without explicit authorization from the owner.
- Do not compile, broker, buy, sell, rent, or trade personal email lists or contact lists.
- Do not build spam, bulk unsolicited outreach, credential abuse, or platform-evasion workflows.
- Do not repurpose personal data beyond the purpose the subject or account owner authorized.
- Do not ignore platform rules, API terms, privacy law, or channel-specific consent requirements.

## 2. Allowed data handling patterns

- Owner-authorized mailbox automation:
  - search
  - summarization
  - task extraction
  - CRM sync
  - follow-up drafting
- First-party lead generation with clear opt-in capture and unsubscribe handling.
- Client-owned or licensed datasets used for the client who controls that data.
- Public, licensed, or synthetic datasets used to build research products or automation tools.

## 3. Monetization lanes that are in-bounds

- Inbox-to-CRM automation for a consenting mailbox owner or client.
- Listing optimization and marketplace operations using seller-provided assets.
- Agency services that improve conversion, operations, or analytics for consenting customers.
- Research, benchmarking, and workflow products built from public, licensed, or client-owned data.

## 4. Monetization lanes that are out-of-bounds

- Reselling extracted email addresses from Gmail, Google Workspace, CSVs, or any other source.
- Harvesting contact lists from a mailbox and selling them to third parties.
- Unsolicited bulk outreach based on scraped or purchased contact data.
- Using Orange or any other analysis tool to organize personal contacts for resale or spam.

## 5. Required screening before execution

Every revenue idea must document:

1. customer
2. offer
3. monetization model
4. data provenance
5. consent basis
6. platform and legal constraints
7. measurable success metric

If any of the first six items are unclear, the idea stays on HOLD.
If privacy abuse, personal-data resale, non-consensual harvesting, or spam appears, the idea is REJECTED.

## 6. Guidance for mailbox and RAG workflows

- Gmail or Google email data may only be processed for the mailbox owner or an explicitly consenting client.
- Use minimum necessary scope, retention, and export.
- Mailbox RAG is for retrieval, support, CRM assistance, and workflow automation for the authorized account.
- Mailbox RAG is never a justification to create or sell contact inventories.

## 7. Finance-adjacent caution

If a future lane touches trading, securities, or regulated finance:

- start in simulation
- define reserve limits and halt conditions
- log every decision path
- require objective risk thresholds before any live exposure

## 8. Default pivot when a plan is rejected

When a plan is rejected for privacy or consent reasons, convert it into one of these:

- an inbox operations product for the owner
- an opt-in lead capture and CRM hygiene product
- a client-owned workflow automation service
- a public-data research product
