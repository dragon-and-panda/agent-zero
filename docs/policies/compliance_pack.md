# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Programs

This policy pack governs any monetization or growth workflow built in Agent Zero.

## Core rules

1. Operate lawfully, ethically, and within platform terms of service.
2. Use only first-party, opted-in, public, or properly licensed data.
3. Treat private inboxes, contact lists, and files as restricted data even when technically accessible.
4. Do not help extract, broker, sell, rent, or trade personal email addresses or other personal data.
5. Do not automate spam, deceptive outreach, credential abuse, scraping behind authentication, or privacy-invasive enrichment.
6. Prefer transparent automation that produces value for the data owner, customer, or paying client.

## Explicitly prohibited

- Harvesting email addresses from Gmail, Google Workspace, or other inboxes for resale.
- Building or selling unsolicited contact lists.
- Scraping private or semi-private platforms for personal data without clear authorization.
- Using RAG over mailbox contents to identify targets for cold outreach unless those contacts already opted in for that exact use.
- Circumventing rate limits, anti-bot systems, access controls, or consent requirements.

## Allowed with care

- RAG over the account owner's own email for summarization, invoicing, support triage, or task extraction.
- Owner-authorized inbox-to-CRM sync, contact dedupe, or follow-up drafting for existing opted-in relationships.
- Segmentation of first-party opted-in leads, customers, or subscribers.
- Analysis of public or licensed market data to build products, research, or pricing intelligence.
- Outreach drafting for prospects sourced from public business information when the workflow remains compliant with local law and platform policy.

## Preferred monetization lanes

1. Marketplace listing automation for owned inventory.
2. Paid research or reporting using public and licensed sources.
3. Consent-based newsletters, communities, and educational products.
4. B2B automation services that improve operations for a paying client.
5. Affiliate or partnership workflows that rely on content and opted-in traffic instead of personal-data resale.
6. Internal tools, templates, and micro-SaaS products with clear user value.

## Data handling checklist

Before a workflow touches data, answer all of the following:

- Do we know who owns the data?
- Do we have the right to use it for this purpose?
- Is there explicit consent or a clear contractual basis?
- Would the workflow still be acceptable if reviewed by the platform, a regulator, or the end user?
- Can the same goal be achieved with first-party, public, or licensed data instead?

If any answer is "no" or "unclear," the workflow must pause and move to a safer alternative.

## Orange Data Mining / data analysis guidance

Orange or similar tooling may be used only on approved datasets such as:

- opted-in subscriber records,
- customer support conversations,
- owner-authorized CRM or mailbox exports where consent/provenance fields are preserved,
- inbound marketplace inquiries,
- public business datasets, or
- licensed vendor exports.

Do not use Orange to organize or resell harvested personal inbox data.
