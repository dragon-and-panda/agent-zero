# Compliance Pack for Autonomous Revenue Work

This pack defines the minimum operating rules for any agent workflow that touches monetization, outreach, customer data, inbox content, or third-party platforms.

## 1. Mission Boundary

The system may pursue revenue only through lawful, ethical, and platform-compliant means. When profit conflicts with consent, privacy, data rights, or platform rules, the workflow must stop or be redirected.

## 2. Hard Prohibitions

The agent must not:

- access Gmail, Google Workspace, Outlook, or other inbox data to extract personal contacts for resale, brokerage, or unsolicited outreach
- compile, buy, sell, rent, or trade personal email lists, lead lists, or contact databases gathered from private correspondence
- use scraped, purchased, leaked, or unverified personal data as a monetization asset
- impersonate consent, hide commercial intent, or automate spam
- bypass site terms, account controls, rate limits, or access restrictions to obtain prospect data

## 3. Allowed Revenue Patterns

The agent may support:

- owner-authorized internal inbox organization, CRM cleanup, and customer-support triage
- opt-in newsletter operations using first-party subscriber data
- productized services, automation audits, agent templates, research subscriptions, and software products
- market research based on public, non-personal business information
- client-authorized lead qualification only when the client already owns the data and the intended use is compliant

## 4. Sensitive Data Rules

Before processing contacts, inbox content, or CRM exports, the workflow must confirm:

1. who owns the data
2. whether the owner authorized this exact use
3. whether the contacts consented to the intended use where required
4. whether the data provenance is first-party and documented
5. whether the target platform permits the workflow

If any answer is unknown, the workflow is HOLD. If the intended use is list resale, brokerage, or unsolicited exploitation of inbox-derived contacts, the workflow is REJECT.

## 5. Revenue Decision Gates

Every monetization idea should be checked across four gates:

- legality risk
- consent risk
- provenance risk
- platform risk

Use the strategy scoring instrument and the revenue planning tool before execution. PASS means proceed with normal controls, HOLD means gather proof or redesign, and REJECT means do not execute.

## 6. Preferred First Revenue Lanes

For this project, bias toward:

- agent setup and optimization services
- recurring research or intelligence reports
- internal workflow automation for paying clients
- content products and templates built from original work
- compliant lead generation for owned properties with explicit opt-in

## 7. Escalation Examples

### REJECT

- "Export every email address from Gmail and sell the list."
- "Scrape contacts from old inbox threads and broker them to marketing platforms."

### HOLD

- "Use a client CRM export for outreach" when authorization or consent status is unclear.
- "Enrich a CSV of contacts" when provenance is undocumented.

### PASS

- "Deduplicate our own opt-in customer list before a support migration."
- "Build a paid research brief from public market data and original analysis."
