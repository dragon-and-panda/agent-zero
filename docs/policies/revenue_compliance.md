# Revenue Compliance Policy Pack

This policy pack governs any Agent Zero workflow related to revenue,
marketing, customer acquisition, market research, or data handling.

It is a practical operating policy, not legal advice.

---

## 1. Default Principle

The system may pursue revenue only when the underlying workflow:

- creates clear customer value,
- uses data lawfully and transparently,
- respects platform terms,
- preserves a working unsubscribe, opt-out, or stop mechanism where relevant,
- avoids deceptive or privacy-invasive tactics.

When uncertain, prefer the lower-risk alternative.

---

## 2. Prohibited Actions

The system must not:

- extract, compile, buy, or sell personal email lists,
- scrape personal contact details for resale or unsolicited bulk outreach,
- access accounts, inboxes, or files without clear authorization,
- evade platform controls, rate limits, or access restrictions,
- impersonate people or businesses,
- send misleading outreach, fake urgency, or false claims,
- hide that content is automated when disclosure is required,
- store more personal data than needed for the task.

Any plan centered on personal-data resale is automatically rejected.

---

## 3. Allowed Data Handling

Allowed inputs include:

- first-party customer data collected with consent,
- organization-owned operational data,
- customer-uploaded files,
- public non-personal information,
- internal support, product, and analytics records,
- mailbox data from a user-controlled account with explicit authorization.

Allowed uses include:

- summarization,
- intent detection,
- segmentation for internal operations,
- FAQ generation,
- customer support improvement,
- sales process analysis,
- product feedback synthesis.

---

## 4. Restricted Data Handling

Personal contact data may be processed only when all of the following are true:

1. the data subject or account owner authorized the use,
2. the use is tied to a legitimate internal business purpose,
3. the data is not resold, repackaged, or shared beyond that purpose,
4. retention is limited,
5. deletion is possible on request when applicable.

If any condition fails, the workflow must stop.

---

## 5. Outreach Rules

Any outbound messaging workflow must satisfy these constraints:

- use consent-based or otherwise lawfully sourced recipients,
- provide an unsubscribe or stop path where relevant,
- throttle sends to avoid spam patterns,
- preserve an audit trail of source, consent basis, and content template,
- avoid deceptive subject lines or claims,
- respect regional requirements such as CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CCPA, and platform rules.

Cold outreach is higher risk and must be reviewed before activation.

---

## 6. Gmail and Inbox Access Rules

Inbox access is allowed only for:

- the user's own mailbox,
- an organization mailbox the user is authorized to connect,
- internal support, sales, or operations analysis.

Inbox access is not allowed for:

- harvesting contacts for sale,
- building third-party lead lists,
- exporting private address books for unrelated use,
- reading another person's mail without explicit authority.

Use the minimum required scopes and store only the derived data needed for the
approved workflow.

---

## 7. Decision Gates

Before launching a new revenue workflow, verify:

### Gate A: Value

- What customer problem is being solved?
- Why would a customer pay for this outcome?

### Gate B: Data rights

- Where did the data come from?
- Is there explicit authority or consent?
- Is any personal data involved?

### Gate C: Platform and legal fit

- Does the workflow comply with platform terms?
- Does it require disclosure, opt-out, or retention controls?

### Gate D: Reputational risk

- Would a reasonable customer view the workflow as helpful and fair?
- Would we be comfortable documenting the tactic publicly?

If any gate fails, redesign or reject the idea.

---

## 8. Logging and Retention

Each revenue workflow should log:

- objective,
- data sources,
- consent or authorization basis,
- messages or offers sent,
- result metrics,
- deletion or retention policy.

Personal data should be minimized, encrypted where appropriate, and retained
only as long as necessary.

---

## 9. Escalation Triggers

Escalate for review if a workflow involves:

- personal data from third parties,
- regulated industries,
- scraping behind logins,
- automated outreach at scale,
- ambiguous ownership of inboxes or files,
- platform anti-bot countermeasures,
- claims about compliance that have not been verified.

If the risk remains unclear after review, do not launch.

---

## 10. Preferred Alternatives to Risky Monetization

Prefer:

- opt-in audience building,
- affiliate or sponsorship revenue,
- service delivery,
- workflow software,
- analytics products,
- research subscriptions,
- marketplace services,
- compliance-aware automation.

Avoid:

- data brokerage,
- spam-heavy lead generation,
- unauthorized scraping,
- monetization based mainly on private records.
