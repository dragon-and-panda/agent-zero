# Commercial Safety and Data-Use Policy

This policy defines the minimum rules for any revenue-oriented workflow built on Agent Zero. It is designed to keep autonomous execution lawful, ethical, and operationally durable.

## 1. Non-negotiable prohibitions

The system must not:

- harvest email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal contact data from Gmail, inbox exports, documents, or scraped sources for resale or cold-list building;
- compile, enrich, broker, trade, rent, or sell contact lists derived from private communications;
- generate or automate spam, unsolicited bulk outreach, or evasive growth tactics;
- bypass rate limits, CAPTCHAs, account restrictions, or marketplace anti-abuse controls;
- misrepresent identity, impersonate a human, or hide commercial intent;
- retain personal data longer than required for the user-approved business purpose.

## 2. Allowed uses of mailbox and message data

Mailbox-connected RAG and message analysis are allowed only for first-party operations such as:

- support inbox triage;
- lead-response drafting for contacts who explicitly reached out;
- invoice, receipt, and order extraction;
- CRM deduplication and consent-status tracking;
- internal knowledge extraction from the operator's own communications.

These workflows must not convert private conversations into marketable lead datasets.

## 3. Contact-source policy

Allowed contact sources:

- website forms with explicit consent;
- newsletter signups and lead magnets with clear disclosure;
- current customers, trial users, and inbound partnership requests;
- public, role-based business addresses when the use is relevant and compliant;
- operator-supplied CRM exports that already include permission to contact.

Disallowed contact sources:

- scraped personal email addresses;
- inbox-mined addresses gathered without consent for marketing use;
- third-party lead dumps of unknown provenance;
- purchased lists lacking verifiable consent and suppression history.

## 4. Required controls for autonomous revenue workflows

Every workflow that touches contacts should maintain:

- source attribution for each record;
- consent or lawful-basis notes;
- unsubscribe and suppression handling;
- rate limits and sending caps;
- audit logs for outreach and follow-up actions;
- a deletion path for revoked consent or stale records.

## 5. Approved monetization lanes

High-fit, low-risk monetization patterns for this repo include:

1. **Marketplace listing automation** for legitimate sellers.
2. **Productized services** such as inbox triage, listing optimization, or data cleanup.
3. **Digital products** including templates, reports, playbooks, and niche knowledge packs.
4. **Affiliate or referral content** with transparent disclosures.
5. **Inbound demand generation** using opt-in forms, newsletters, and lead magnets.
6. **Public-company research services** based on non-personal business intelligence.

## 6. Escalation defaults

Escalate or halt automation when:

- the workflow depends on personal-data resale;
- the source of contact data cannot be verified;
- the channel's terms or applicable law are unclear;
- a task would create deceptive or non-consensual outreach.

When in doubt, prefer opt-in acquisition, first-party workflows, or non-contact-based monetization.
